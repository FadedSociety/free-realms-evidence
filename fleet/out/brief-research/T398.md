**Where 46 and 141 come from (verified in code, not from a hidden constant):**
- The 930060 base damage is 15. At load, `field.rs:224` multiplies it by `1.75^(weapon_tier(level)-1)`.
- `weapon_tier` (`encounter.rs:52-59`): levels 1-3 -> 1, 4 -> 2, 5-7 -> 3, 8-11 -> 4, 12+ -> 5.
- MID-H1 (level 7, tier 3): 15 x 3.0625 = 45.9 -> 46. ELITE-H2 (level 20, tier 5): 15 x 9.379 = 140.7 -> 141.
- The 8 is the W102-H1 Hooligan at level 3 (tier 1), so no multiplier. The hit is then multiplied by `player.mitigation` (`field.rs:882`, `1.` if no player). The native run's 46/141 imply mitigation 1.0, which is unverified.
- Health is also scaled at load: `max_health x level_health(level)/1000` (`field.rs:222-223`). The W102-H1 Hooligan is 220 x 1.323 = ~291 HP at level 3.
- The damage path is fully data-driven except the tier table. A changed `damage` row changes the hit linearly. The brief's step 2 is already met; what is missing is a sanity bound and the curve change.
