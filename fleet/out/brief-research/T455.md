**Risks and corrections:**
1. The brief's "moving flag from the owner's last 125 state byte" is unsupported. The owner's movement arrives as a validated `MovementState` (gateway.rs:1576-1580), and its state bits are unnamed. Derive moving from observed displacement.
2. Enemies send state 1, which takes the zero-speed branch. Native notes flag this as a suspect for the stationary-model result (note :32, :40). Choose the pet state byte deliberately and check it in IDA or in a visible test.
3. The brief omits 35/8 animation. Enemies send animation 3 while moving and 1 when stopped. Decide whether pets do the same.
4. Late-viewer position depends on `staged_world.pets` (osfr-gateway.rs:1886-1888). Dropping the override at :784 makes it stale unless the pose is refreshed on every pet tick.
5. Hub headroom is 10 lines in gateway.rs and 40 in gameplay.rs. The moves must be real, not a rewrite.
6. Lead-only: roster save (`save_with_pet_roster`, gateway.rs:1342) and the ride relay. The brief keeps both.
7. Brief tweaks: `terrain` field is :173; `on_location` is :1806; `tick_at` is :1821.
