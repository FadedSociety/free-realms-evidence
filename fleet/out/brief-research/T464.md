**Risks and corrections:**

2. **False claim: "Sanctuary leaves the winner lines out."** Sanctuary does use per-side winner lines (:168-171, :180-182, picked at :495). It does not use 432407 or 432432, which are ours alone.
3. **False claim: "everything leaves with a poof."** Sanctuary removes with effect 0 (no poof). The brief names no poof id. Bruce's brief uses poof 21, but that is a different event.
