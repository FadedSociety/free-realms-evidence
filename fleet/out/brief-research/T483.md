**Risks and corrections:**
1. The brief's "Verify first" fails: `ActiveRide` lacks the owned instance and `Inventory::look` is absent. Either merge T482 first or have the builder carry the instance in `ActiveRide` (or `RideView`), and fetch the item by its id from `ctx.inventory` at spawn.
2. The brief says Sanctuary sends 46 on the 168/4. PC2009 sends 0 (see above). Only the 168/2 is confirmed to carry 46. Recommend 46 on 168/2 only, and 0 on 168/4 unless a capture shows otherwise.
3. `MountsDocument` and `MountDefinition` use `deny_unknown_fields` (mounts.rs:48, :69-70). Adding `mount_effect_id` to mounts.json requires a field on `MountsDocument` (schema_version 1 check at :108), or the catalogue fails to load.
4. `restoration/piece_092.rs:571-602` already has `encode_mount_response(…, composite_effect_id, …)` and `encode_dismount_response(rider, composite_effect_id)`, with tests at `tests/piece_092.rs:367,374` using 46. The brief does not mention these. Reuse them rather than duplicate.
5. Field position: the notes (Mounts.md:140-141, :210) say the 168/2 fifth field is stored on the rider. The brief and encoder put the effect in the fourth i32 (piece_092 numbering: seat, queue, unknown, effect, float). Unresolved (R); the builder must check which index the client uses for the effect and for +0x17bc.
