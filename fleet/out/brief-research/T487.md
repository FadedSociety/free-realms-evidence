**Risks and corrections:**
1. **Prerequisites missing.** T455 and T424 are queued, not merged. Do not build until both land, or the builder must write AUTOPILOT: BLOCKED.
2. **Pets gate list is incomplete.** `occupation.rs:66-67` and `:160-162` also close pets for Chess and Fishing. Housing keeps pets open (`:74`, pin test excludes Housing).
3. **No pet tick.** `tick_world` (gameplay.rs:1885) never touches pets. The brief's "first location update or pet tick" has only `on_location` (gameplay.rs:1806) to use. `on_location` returns early when `blocks(location)` (:1807-1809), and Derby, Soccer, Kart and Housing close that gate. 4. **Summon wire.** The summon path emits removal (if any), AddNpc (`pets.rs:927-938`), then 53/9 (`pets.rs:939-944`). It does not emit 53/5. 53/5 is pushed only at world entry (gameplay.rs:1711, :1800). The brief's "53/9, 53/5" is therefore wrong for the normal path, unless a list refresh is intended (R).

7. **Session reset.** `pets.reset_session()` (pets.rs:755-758) clears only `active` and `ui_mode`. Put `remembered` there. The disconnect path is `gateway.rs:1382` -> `gameplay.rs:1507/1516`; whether disconnect calls it is unverified.
