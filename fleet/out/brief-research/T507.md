**Client evidence:**
- The 35/8 decoder is not `0x00908a50`. It is case 8 of the opcode-35 dispatcher `0092f460` (`BaseClient::DispatchBasePlayerUpdate`, BaseClient vtable[69]). In order: `008b3ee0()` constructs `PlayerUpdatePacketSetAnimation`; `00908a50(...)` is a range/validity gate (returns 1 only when a length check passes); `00984960()` resolves the actor. If the actor exists, `flags & 1` is set: write `actor+0x51c = animation id` and destroy (`008a07c0`), then return. Otherwise call `0096c780` (PlayOrQueueActorAnimation) with flag bit 1 as a separate boolean. Source: `client_sql` fn 0092f460 pseudocode (case 8), fn 008b3ee0 label, fn 008a07c0 label. So bit 0 = base animation, and clear = play now. That matches `notes/Research/Native NPC Motion and Animation.md:46`.
- The 21-byte field order `u16 35, u16 8, u64 guid, i32 anim, i32 0, u8 flags` is taken from the research note and the three Rust copies. I did not see the field reads in the pseudocode I read (they sit in `00984960` or the ctor path); mark the byte layout as `(unverified at byte level)` until the builder reads `00984960`.
