**Client evidence:**
- 00b60220 (`Pet.EquipItem` sender) builds the payload: byte flag = 0; first u32 = `param_3`; second u32 = `*param_2` (the first dword of the record). Calls wrapper 00b5c7e0 when the pet object's `+0x280` is set.
- 00b602c0 (clear sender): byte flag = 1; first u32 = `param_2`; the second u32 is never assigned in the decompile. Possibly uninitialised (unverified).
- Serializer 00b5a790 writes, in order: byte at payload +0xc, dword at +0x14, dword at +0x10. So wire body = flag, u32 `param_3`/`param_2`, u32 `*param_2`/unset.
- Reader 00b55c30 reads byte → +0xc, dword → +0x14, dword → +0x10. Matches the wire order.
- Meaning of the two u32 (slot vs instance) is not settled from the client. Sanctuary's layout (below) suggests slot then item instance (R). Confirm with the caller of 00b60220 before coding.
