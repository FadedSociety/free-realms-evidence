**Risks and corrections:**
1. **Stale premise.** The server does not drive pet movement. The client sends the pet's position and permission (`piece_091.rs:567-575`), and the server only records a target. There is nothing to "warp" or "follow" server-side. The builder would have to design an outbound pet move, and the wire for that is unrecovered (unverified: I did not search the client for a pet move packet).
2. **Dependencies unmet.** T490 and T534 are not merged. T535 needs T534's `segment_blocked` and `surface_below`.
3. **Size.** The 2 m height grid for FabledRealms is 2561×3073 nodes (`notes/Design/Studio Toolkit.md:55`). A 0.5 m grid over the same extent is about 126 M cells, which is past the 64 M cap at `terrain.rs:45`. It needs sparse or chunked storage, or a coarser cell. The extent is inferred, not checked.
4. **Hubs.** `gameplay.rs` and `encounter.rs` are capped, and neither should grow for this. Put the work in `navigation.rs`, and make the `pets.rs` change minimal.
5. **Lead-only.** Cargo changes, and the bin wiring if a new `--walk` flag is added to the gateway, are lead-only.
6. Keep `pathing.rs` unchanged, as the brief says.
