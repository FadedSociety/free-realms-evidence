**Risks and corrections:**
1. **Zone gating missing from the brief.** The `.map` is FabledRealms only (zone 0). Without a zone check, FabledRealms nodes would be applied to other zones (`pathing.rs:158` takes any zone with terrain).
2. **Gate 46 conflicts with the reply layout.** The reply starts at the raw player position and ends at the raw `to`. Each end can be up to `snap_radius_m` (40 m) from its node. So the "every sample within 5 m of an edge" gate fails on those two legs unless the builder excludes them or walks them differently. Sanctuary checks this first leg; we cannot, since there is no navmesh.
3. **Road edges can be blocked.** The Farnum note says MAP adjacency is "a road reference, not a proved player navigation mesh", and one road segment crosses a fence (`Farnum note:9,66`). So the road graph does not fully stop routes running through obstacles. Note this in the notes.
4. **Edge symmetry is unverified.** "4,302 edges" may count directed adjacency entries. If so, the "refuse asymmetric edges" rule will refuse the real graph on load. Check this before writing the loader.
5. **Source path.** The brief's `Assets_002/...FabledRealms.map` path cannot be checked from my roots. The Farnum note gives the raw file as `lab/extracted/client-assets/pack_14/006082.map` with the same sha256 (`Farnum note:72`). The manifest line `refs:osfr-asset-manifest/Assets_manifest.txt:146194` reads `FabledRealms.map,845126296,46084`; it carries no sha256 (verified).
6. **Hub growth.** Ceilings in `tests/spine_rules.ceilings:7` are `gameplay.rs` 5515 and `gateway.rs` 2242. The ceilings only go down, so any new line in `gameplay.rs` (the `answer` call site, or a graph argument) breaks spine_rules. Keep the change in `pathing.rs` and `road_graph.rs`, and let the caller pass the graph the way `terrain` is passed.
7. **Pets navmesh.** I found no navmesh in `src`. I did not find a pets navmesh brief in the `briefs` listing (unverified across the whole list).
8. **Cost (R).** A* over 2,019 nodes and about 4,302 edges is roughly 5×10⁴ heap operations per request, well under the 4/s budget. The graph JSON should fit under 1 MiB (R).
9. **Canary.** Loading an empty graph must make the road test fail. Note that the `walk()`-based tests in `tests/pathing.rs` pin straight lines, so a graph that loads on the test zone would change them.

**Suggested tests:**
1. Road test: zone 0, nodes ~200 m apart. Every interior sample lies within 5 m of an edge, and the sample count is at most `MAX_POINTS`.
