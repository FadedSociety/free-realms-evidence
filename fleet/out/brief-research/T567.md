**Param1 offset (correction).** Param1 is the `Unknown36` wire field (`scripts/prepare-opening-base.py:73` maps `Unknown36` from `Param1`). Its offset is **not fixed**. It is 127 + len(ModelName) + len(TextureAlias) + len(TintAlias), derived from the field order above. Check: 127 + 12 + 4 + 4 = 147 matches the base size in `Item Catalogue.md:37`. The brief's "every other byte equals the base row" must therefore allow shifts when the model, texture or tint strings change length. A fixed-offset patch would corrupt the row.
