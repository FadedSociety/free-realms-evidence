- **[low] kart_weapons.rs:1093-1097 - a missile's terminal leg is integrated from `next_update_ms - MISSILE_UPDATE_INTERVAL_MS`, which is not the last integrated time once `next_update_ms` has been clamped to `expires_ms`, so the missile overshoots its expiry point by up to about 3.5 m.** The doc comment at 1091-1092 says the terminal point is "where the missile actually died".

  - **Failure scenario:** spawn at s=0 with expiry e=4000. Ticks at 3850 (next_update becomes 3950), then at 3960. The 3960 tick integrates 3850→3960 correctly and sets `next_update_ms = min(4060, 4000) = 4000` at line 1181-1183. A tick at 4000 then computes `last_integrated = max(4000-100, 0) = 3900` and `remaining = 100`. The true remaining from 3960 is 40 ms, so the missile advances 3.5 m instead of 1.4 m. Worst case: the last tick lands at e-1, and the leg is computed as 100 ms instead of 1 ms (about 3.5 m over).
  - **Reach:** `WeaponState::tick` (1078) is called from `kart_service.rs:1754` whenever `content.weapon_effects` is on. The overshoot needs a missile in `self.weapons`. Production spawning goes through `use_item`/`use_item_with_matrix`, which no production caller reaches at present (the only hits are the gateway's unrelated item-use packet and tests). So this is dormant until spawn is wired, and it only moves the visual actor.
  - **Fix:** record the last integrated timestamp explicitly instead of inferring it from `next_update_ms`. Add a serde-defaulted `last_integrated_ms` field (set to `spawned_ms` at spawn, updated wherever `position` is integrated at 1099-1103 and 1125-1129), and use it at 1093-1096. Removing the `.min(expires_ms)` at 1181-1183 is not a fix, because `validate_for` at 1288 rejects `next_update_ms > expires_ms`.
