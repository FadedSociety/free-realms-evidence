# SOE Fan Faire 2007

**Result: not an in-game Free Realms event.** "SOE Fan Faire" was a real-world Sony Online Entertainment convention in Las Vegas. Nothing in the pack ties it to a scheduled in-game event, quest, NPC or zone. The pack also merges several years, so each year gets its own record below. Use `live: "no"` for the convention as a whole.

## Per-year records

**2007 (SOE Fan Faire 2007)**
- Occurrence: Aug 2-5, 2007, per the pack's fact statement only. Unconfirmed: the article summaries give no dates. Source: http://www.tentonhammer.com/node/10347 ("3:45 Free Realms", 2007-08-20). Only a podcast segment title mentions Free Realms.
- Live: no. Pre-launch podcast mention only.

**2008 (SOE Fan Faire 2008)**
- Occurrence: Aug 14-17, 2008, Las Vegas Hilton. Source: http://tentonhammer.com:80/node/30684 (2008-04-18, pre-show press release). The show itself took place per the critique's cited poster Ff2008.jpg ("LAS VEGAS 2008 AUGUST 14 - 17") and the day-one recaps http://www.tentonhammer.com/node/41251 and http://www.tentonhammer.com:80/node/41260. I confirmed those recap summaries exist, but did not confirm the poster myself.
- Free Realms: demos were planned (node/30684). The day-one recap has pre-launch remarks, including a CEO quote on Free Realms (node/41260). Neither is a live in-game event.
- Attendee beta: attendees were flagged for Free Realms beta access starting April 16, 2009. Source: http://www.tentonhammer.com:80/node/66874 (summary confirms it). Headline only: http://www.tentonhammer.com:80/node/66944.

**2009 (Fan Faire 2009, later "SOE Live")**
- Occurrence: June 25-28, Las Vegas. Source: wiki image Ff2009.jpg only. No article body confirms it.
- Item: Fan Faire '09 T-Shirt (locale:392941). Its description is locale:151319 ("exclusive shirt given to atendees of Fan Faire 2009"). The earlier "Fan Faire 2009 T-Shirt" name is not the client name. Source: wiki page Fan Faire T-Shirt.

**2010 (SOE Live 2010)**
- Occurrence: Aug 5-8, 2010, Bally's, Las Vegas. Source: wiki page SOE Live; wiki image Ff2010.jpg.
- Items: Fan Faire 2010 Gold and Silver Reward Pack (locale:156924, 156925); Platinum Reward Pack (156926, 156927); VIP Reward Pack (156928, 156929). Attendee rewards, no prices.

**2011 (SOE Live / SOE Fan Faire 2011)**
- Occurrence: July 7-9, 2011, Las Vegas. Start date from the SOE Live wiki body. End date 9 from promo art Ff2011.png only; the body excerpt cuts off.
- Items:
  - Fan Faire 2011 T-Shirt (locale:435895, 435896). Chest slot, acquirable only through trade per the wiki page. Some non-attendees got it through the month's Membership package.
  - Fan Faire Pack (locale:435898, 435899). Granted to all active Members in late July 2011 by a processing error. Not an event schedule.
  - Fan Faire 2011 Bicycle (locale:436136, 436137, 5102901). Acquisition path unknown. Source: wiki File:Fan Faire 2011 Bicycle.jpg.
