# Spinnerette (Briarwood)

**NPC:** 9500000218, Spinnerette, Briarwood (southern Bristlewood, near the Thistlerow entrance).

**Greeting:** `locale 71541`: "Please. Defeat my sister Arachnia in her lair. She has become corrupted and I cannot bear to see her this way."

Note on the id: the guide cites this line as client key `2784241076`. The locale tool indexes by a different number, and the client dat maps key 2784241076 to this exact text (`refs:opening-native-lobby-20261009T163603Z/locale/en_us_data.dat:73371`). Key 2784241076 does not resolve through `locale`, so the tool id is 71541. The text match is the same as the cross-check I used for Gorg's line (key 715016349 = locale 16121).

**Proof:** Rule 1 (guide attribution).
- `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Arachnia's Lair.md:19` (Story hook row): "Her sister Spinnerette says: "Please. Defeat my sister Arachnia in her lair. She has become corrupted and I cannot bear to see her this way." (`loc 2784241076`)". The guide attributes the quoted first-person line to Spinnerette, and the line is in her voice (she speaks of her sister).
- The line is not a greeting already claimed by another NPC. A search of `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/*.json` for 71541 and 2784241076 returned no hits.
