# Alyssa Eggelyn (Sanctuary)

**NPC:** 9500000436, Alyssa Eggelyn, Sanctuary (name string 672706230; Pixiewood Hills).

**Greeting:** `locale 5100007`: "A mean robgoblin took one of my eggs and hid them somewhere. Spring Eggstravaganza is around the corner and I need your help finding it."

**Proof:** Rule 1, plus rule 3 in part.
- `freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 322 (wiki page "Quest:The Spring Eggstravaganza", the quest that starts this chain) has a "To Start" step, "Speak with Alyssa Eggelyn located in Pixiewood Hills in Sanctuary.", and under Steps quotes: "A mean robgoblin took one of my eggs and hid them somewhere. Spring Eggstravaganza is around the corner and I need your help finding it." -Alyssa Eggelyn. That is the exact text of locale 5100007, attributed to her by name, and it is the opening line of her quest.
- The quest's giver is Alyssa (same page, To Start), and `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sanctuary.md:133` lists her as the giver of The Spring Eggstravaganza.

**Caveat:** This line is quest-start dialogue, not a generic idle greeting. The wiki proves she speaks it, but it is not proof that it is her ambient greeting. Nothing I checked shows a separate greeting field for her.

**Checked and rejected:**
- `locale 5100000` and `5100009` ("Greetings [*player*], Spring Eggstravaganza time and is here..."): a greeting-style opener, but no repo, ref, or wiki source attributes it to Alyssa. Searches for these ids in the content data found nothing.
- `locale 5100089`, `5100091`, `5100093`, `5100094` (reward and follow-up lines): no source binds them to her as a greeting. The wiki quotes the second-step line "Wow I thought you would never find it..." with her name, but that is a mid-quest line, not a greeting.
- `locale 5100006`, `5100086`–`5100088` (quest summaries) and `5100142`–`5100157` (journal objectives): these are descriptions, not spoken lines.
- `locale 9544` ("These are some nice eggs..."): no attribution to Alyssa found.
- Blackspore Swamp notes (`NPCs - Blackspore Swamp.md:234`) name Darci as the Eggcellent Egg Hunt giver, which conflicts with the Sanctuary record. That is not used.
