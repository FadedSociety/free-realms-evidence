# Florina (Sanctuary)

**NPC:** 9500000482, Florina, Sanctuary (Pet Adoption Center, southern Sanctuary).

**Greeting:** `locale 46909`: "Welcome to the Sanctuary Pet Adoption Center. Pick out your pet, then take it for a walk or buy it!"

**Proof:** Rule 1. The community wiki page for Florina quotes this exact line and attributes it to her through the speaker argument of the quote template: `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/11816-45843-a22ba4df330a.wiki:15`, `{{Quote|Welcome to the Sanctuary Pet Adoption Center. Pick out your [[Pets|pet]], then take it for a walk or buy it!|Florina}}`. The infobox on the same page gives her as Occupation: Merchant, Location: Sanctuary, which matches the NPC record.

**Checked and rejected:**
- The same wording appears in `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Highroad Junction.md:20`, but there it is the Highroad Junction Pet Adoption Center line, id 2235627259. It is a different string and a different character, so it does not bind to Florina.
- A search of `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/` for id 46909 finds no NPC greeting bound to it. The only hit is a numeric coordinate in wugachug.json, which is unrelated.
- The quest text "Pick up a gift from Florina in Sanctuary" (3523981689) is a pick-up objective with an unknown title. It names Florina but is not an intro line, so it is not used as her greeting.
- Locale strings about pets that are not sourced to Florina (e.g. Serena's pet-learning lines, Prissi, Alena Goldenheart's Highroad lines) belong to other characters.
