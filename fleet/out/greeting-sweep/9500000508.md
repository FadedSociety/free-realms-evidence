# Necronomicus (Sanctuary)

**NPC:** 9500000508, Necronomicus, Sanctuary zone record (Gloam Lord of Necromancy; boss). His overworld "Mysterious Stranger" form is the same character per the wiki.

**Greeting:** `locale 5101114`: "Do not meddle in my affairs, &quot;hero.&quot; I have planned far too long and far too well for you to interfere now.<BR><BR>Consider this your final warning!"

**Proof:** Rule 1 (wiki page quotes the line and attributes it to this character).
- `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/16877-50130-b385ebd250b0.wiki:16`: `{{Quote|Do not meddle in my affairs, hero. I have planned far too long and far too well for you to interfere now.<br>Consider this your final warning!|Mysterious Stranger AKA Necronomicus}}`. The same page (line 14) says the Mysterious Stranger met in Bixie-B-Gone "is later found out [to have been] Necronomicus", and its infobox caption (line 4) is "Mysterious Stranger".
- The wording matches the client text exactly, so the id is the one used in the client table.
- No NPC file uses 5101114 as a greeting. `grep` over `repo` finds it only in the Necronomicus dungeon notes, not in any `npcs/*.json` greeting.

Caveat: this is a warning from the temporary field form, not a plain greeting. It is the strongest proven line I found for this NPC.
