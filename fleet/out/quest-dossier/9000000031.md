## Notes on the built set (not new quests)

- **Postman: Summit in Shrouded Glade** (row 24, title ucdt 223924213 / ugdt 1696141100) is listed in this NPC record as a Brin quest, but it is built as record 3130027 (`postman.json:1348`) with `giver_npc_id` 9000000132. That id is Loryn (`npcs/snowhill.json:22284`), and the Postman guide also says Loryn gives it (`Postman.md:118`). The NPC record's attribution to Brin is probably wrong. The text "Head to Shrouded Glade and speak to Brin Galeglow" (3000430270) is the hand-off, not the offer. Flag for the builder, no new record needed.
- The Achieve Level 20 gate (3130031) has no reward or objective data beyond the title (see Gaps).
