**Intro (offer), giver Brody Sparfist.**
- 3260595528 (locale index 22771): "There have been some complaints in Snowhill regarding bats flying out of the mines. Brody Sparfist would like for you to clear out some of these bats from the Bat Caves."
- Evidence: the dat line 86145, and the Bat Cave note row at `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/The Bat Cave.md:85`, which lists 3260595528 as "(Brody Sparfist)".
- The same quest has a second intro without Brody's name: 530958019 / 1655845185 (locale index 22777 / 24984), "There have been some complaints about bats flying out of the mines at night ... Do you think you could go into the Bat Cave and clear out some of the bats?" This may be another NPC's version. Treat the Brody wording as the offer.

Location text: 2852057197 ("Brody can be found near the entrance to the Singing Crystal Mines in Snowhill."); 2021240221 ("Visit Brody Sparfist near the mines in Snowhill and talk to him."). NPC id 9000000033 (`npcs/snowhill.json`).
2. Objective: "Defeat 15 bats within the Bat Cave!" (2373852221; locale index 24486; `opening-anvil-placement-v1/locale/en_us_data.dat:62447`). Target: bats in the Bat Cave, the dungeon behind the barrier in the Singing Crystal Mines (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/The Bat Cave.md`, entrance row 18). Count 15 is evidenced. The enemy name "Bat" is evidenced only by the dungeon note (Bats; the wiki lists Bats and Elder Deep Bats). Which enemy counts is unknown.
3. Return objective: "Return to Brody Sparfist." (3105175498; locale index 37689; dat line 81989).
4. Completion, with reward: "Great job clearing out those bats. Hopefully they won't bother the citizens of Snowhill any longer. Take this. I think you've earned it." (2805814185; locale index 37691; dat line 73951). The line says "Take this" but names no item.

(2664987915; locale index 24377; dat line 70170). It fits this quest's theme, but nothing ties it to Brody. Do not use it without confirmation.
