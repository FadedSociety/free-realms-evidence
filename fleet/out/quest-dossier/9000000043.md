### 1. Brawler: Prized Brawlers (Brawler Club initiation; the 3-boss errand)

- **Title:** locale 72992 "Brawler: Prized Brawlers" (inferred by position, see above).
- **Giver:** Chaz Strongarm, Lakeshore (npc 9000000043).
- **Intro/offer (72991):** "Chaz here has the final say on who gets to join Brawler Club. You must be able to pack the same punch he can. <BR><BR>Defeat the scum on Chaz's list if you want a chance to call yourself a Brawler Club member."
- **Description (72993):** "Chaz wants you to take care of some trouble makers around Sacred Grove. Defeat Mugwort in Mugwort's Hollow, Necrolaceous in Dark Spore Depths, and Snognosh in the Howling Hills."
- **Completion text (72994):** "Congratulations! Welcome to Brawler Club! It's an honor to fight beside you. Remember the rules and always remain vigilant. <BR><BR>Check in with Chaz and he should have some work for you."
- **Turn-in line (notes only, hash 3264721046):** "Return to me once you have taken care of Mugwort, Necrolaceous, and Snognosh." Cited from `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Howling Hills.md:93`. I could not read it from the locale tool.

**Steps, in order**
1. Talk to Chaz in Lakeshore and accept (offer 72991). Prerequisite: the Rourke Ironfist chain (see below).
2. Defeat Elder Mugwort in Mugwort's Hollow. Locale names: "Elder Mugwort" 16811; "Defeat the druid, Mugwort!" 16174. Dungeon: `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Mugwort's Hollow.md:79`.
3. Defeat Necrolaceous in Dark Spore Depths. Locale names: "Necrolaceous" 4580 (and 10292); "Defeat the knights to bring out Necrolaceous!" 4656; "Defeat Necrolaceous's fungal guardians to anger him to face you in combat." 20237.
4. Defeat Snognosh in the Howling Hills. Locale: "Snognosh" 5044; "Find and defeat Snognosh!" 5055. Dungeon: `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Howling Hills.md:93`.
5. Return to Chaz (turn-in line above). Completion text 72994.

**Prerequisites**
- Brawler Club gate. Rourke's M24 text (72971) and its follow-up "Sweet weapon you have there! ... there's still one more thing you have to do..." (72974) come before the Chaz intro. Level 20 is the wiki's gate for the Rourke chain (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Brawler.md:161`), so the level-20 gate is inferred.
- No earlier Chaz quest. This is the first of his Brawler Club work.

**Rewards:** unknown. None is in the text at 72991–72994. (R) Coins, XP and items: not evidenced.

**Evidence:** locale 72991, 72992, 72993, 72994, 72971, 72974 (client); `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Howling Hills.md:93`; `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Mugwort's Hollow.md:79`; `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Lakeshore.md:146`; `notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Brawler.md:165`.
