1. **Giver ids do not name Eddie.** No built record uses `9000000053`. The records carry other ids. Known cases:
   - 3140005 (Meeting The Team) uses 9000000140. That id is in `npcs/snowhill.json:23624`, and the guide names Manager Landon as the giver of Meeting The Team.
   - 3140007 (Achieve Level 2) uses 9000000058. No zone file in `npcs/` defines this id (searched), so it is unresolved.
   - The other ids (9000000547–9000000549, 9000000003, 9000000281, 9000000058) were not checked one by one.
   - The guide says Eddie gives the "Achieve Level N" quests and most story quests after Meeting The Team. The `jobs.json` soccer_star trainer list does include 9000000053 (line 1327).
2. **Objectives and rewards are empty** in all 30 records. The records state that reward values and objective targets are "(R)". The guide holds the dialog text (locale ids) for each quest. The quest-level data is in the guide, not in the records.
3. Records have `enabled: false`.
