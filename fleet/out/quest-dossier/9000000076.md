# Quests of Drill Sergeant Dewey (Snowhill)

## Scope

Drill Sergeant Dewey (npc 9000000076, Snowhill) gives three line quests per the record and the Warrior guide. **3150001 Becoming a Warrior is already built** (`quests/warrior.json`, record 1, enabled), so it is skipped. Two unbuilt quests remain. Both exist in `warrior.json` only as disabled stubs with empty `objectives` and `rewards`, so the dossier below fills them in.

## Gaps

- **Built-state check:** 3150001 is built and skipped. 3150002 and 3150003 are not fully built: they have empty `objectives` and `rewards`, and they are disabled.
- **Giver conflict:** `warrior.json` records 3150002 and 3150003 with `giver_npc_id` **9000000302**. That id is the unresolved "Dewey" ref in `npcs_unresolved.json:2275`, not Dewey's id **9000000076** from the record. The guide says Dewey gives both quests (W3 also involves Morninglory, 9000000155). A builder should reconcile this before enabling.
- **Snowhill zone file:** `npcs/snowhill.json` lists only quest 3150001 in `quests_given` for Dewey. The Dewey record's `quests_given_text` names all three quests.
- **Snarler location conflict:** intro 77222 says "mountains to the southeast". Description 77224 says "western side of Snowhill". Objective 1962735708 and 3693529699 say south-east. The two are not reconciled in the client.
- **Counts:** no kill count for Snarlers or Yeti Bouncers is evidenced in any source read.
- **Yeti Bouncer enemy id:** no NameId, NPC template, or spawn id found in client or refs.
- **Rites completion text:** not found in the locale. No hand-in line is cited for W3.
- **Rewards:** no coin, XP, or item reward is evidenced for either quest. The Amateur Warrior Axe claim for W3 comes only from the wiki.
- **Level gates:** not stated for either quest. The guide's "level 20 Brawler" note on W1 is also unverified.
- **Laps variant of W2:** the "six target dummies" cluster is unbuilt and its placement is unsettled per the guide.
- **Locale id forms:** small ids (e.g. 77222, 70892) are Global.Text indices and hash ids (e.g. 937190059) are ucdt hashes. The locale tool looks up only the small ids numerically, so hash ids were resolved by text search.
