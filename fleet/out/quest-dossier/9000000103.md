### Quests in `quest_ids_given` that are misattributed

Fisherman quests 3070040, 3070043 and 3070045 are built in `quests/fisherman.json` with `giver_npc_id: 9000000103`:
- 3070040 "Fisherman: Plenty of Fish" (title 409040), line 1941, prereq 3070039.
- 3070043 "Fisherman: To the Rescue!" (title 412262), line 2081.
- 3070045 "Fisherman: Sunken Treasure" (title 412195), line 2175, prereq 3070044.

The Fisherman guide places these at Wintery Basin with a giver named Helena (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Fisherman.md:136, 139, 141`). The NPC guide says this is a different Helena, "treated as two NPCs; unproven" (`NPCs - Stillwater Crossing.md:104, 187`). The Chef guide also says the Wintery Basin Helena is "not a Chef quest giver" (`Chef.md:38`). The fisherman records therefore point at the Stillwater chef's id. I did not change anything. The builder should decide whether these need a separate Wintery Basin Helena id, and whether giver 9000000103 is wrong on them.
