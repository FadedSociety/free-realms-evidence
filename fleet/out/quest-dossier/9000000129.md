# Quests of Kazuo the Dragon (Lakeshore)

## Scope and summary

Kazuo the Dragon (npc 9000000129; ucdt 748673828, ugdt 1465130661) is a quest giver in name only. The record, `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/lakeshore.json:5010-5051`, says `quests_given_text` is "Not mapped to titles", and `quest_ids_given` is empty. `quests/ninja.json` holds only the Ty line (3110001 onward), with no Kazuo quest. A `refs` search found no Kazuo page in the wiki cache. Every Kazuo-story quest below is therefore **unbuilt**. The four dossiers are the most clearly evidenced of the story's fragments, and none has a confirmed giver or title. Locale ids are copied from `locale`. Titles are marked as candidates where they are not confirmed.

Story frame (evidence: `notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Ninja.md:241`, unmatched client text): Kazuo is withered by Mari Duskblade's "Withering Touch" (3736376946, 271917780). The cure is the "Unclouded Soul Technique" (990535413, 154241800), and Rokuro, Kai and Akari are involved. The Shadow Talon camp in Greenwood is linked to thugawugs, and Kazuo is held by changelings (Amoranth, then Darvon).
