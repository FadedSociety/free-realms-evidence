## 1. Medic: Operation: Revival (3090002), stub

- **Title:** "Medic: Operation: Revival", locale 10164. Guide title ids: ucdt 511442475 / ugdt 3987711664.
- **Giver:** Nurse Naia 9000000161. Wiki and guide say Naia (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Medic.md`, Band 1 row 2).
- **Prerequisite:** 3090001 (guide "After 1"). The record has `prerequisite_quest_ids: [3090001]`.
- **Level/job:** guide says level 5 band. The record has no `job` field.
- **Intro (offer):** not confirmed. The guide quotes "Here's your chance to learn about First Aid while on the job..." (4068730179), but I did not find its locale id. Locale 15584 is a candidate: "Medic! What are you doing just standing here? There are some wounded bixies in the Bixie Hive that need your assistance!" It is not confirmed as the offer.
- **Steps:**
  1. Accept from Naia (intro as above).
  2. Go into the Bixie Hive (Queensfields, per guide; dungeon locale 5702 "Bixie Hive") and **revive twelve wounded bixies**. Description, locale 15586: "You are to go into the Bixie Hive and revive twelve wounded bixies. I cannot go, I must stay at the hospital. Now hurry up and help those bixies!" The count 12 is in this text. The guide quotes the same wording as hash 3293146152.
  3. Return to Naia (turn-in is implied by the guide; not separately verified).
- **Completion text (candidate):** locale 9859, "You were able to revive all of the injured bixies? Great! You're making quite the impression here already." The guide quotes the same wording as 1897709560. The record has no `end_text_string_id`.
- **Objective kind:** the schema has no "revive N" objective. The guide's objective text is "As a medic, help the injured bixies" (3024842350), not in locale by id.
- **Rewards:** unknown. The guide says "unknown". Nothing is evidenced.
- **Evidence:** `medic.json` lines 89-131 (stub); `Medic.md` Band 1 row 2; locale 10164, 15584, 15586, 9859, 5702.
- **Warning:** locale 15336 and 15888 ("Bixies Gone Bad", "Save the bixie queen") are a different quest in the same dungeon. Do not use them.

## Gaps

- **Rewards (all three):** no evidence. The guide says "unknown" for each. `rewards` stays empty. Do not copy the W110/T194 fallback (5 or 10 coins, 15 to 20 Medic XP) from the built quests as if it were evidenced.
- **Operation: Revival:** the offer intro (4068730179) and the turn-in id are not confirmed. The count of 12 is evidenced. The "revive" objective kind has no schema support.
- **Supply Recovery:** the completion line is missing. The record's `end_text_string_id: 73363` is wrong and should be cleared or replaced. Objective 73723 is ambiguous with Medic on a Mission. Tavern Cellar is a wiki candidate only. Item ids for "Medical Supplies" are not resolved. The Terrence objective id (1057663727) is not in locale by id.
- **Tummies in Turmoil:** the named resident NPCs are not resolved to ids. The timer is not in the record. Locale 75985 is unresolved. Which of 73751 and 115721 is the offer is unknown.
- **Missing `job: 11` field:** 3090002, 3090009 and 3090011 have no `job` gate. 3090001 and 3090003 do.
- **Enabled flags:** 3090002, 3090009 and 3090011 are `enabled: false`. The builder must decide whether to enable them, and should not enable them with empty objectives.
- **Gate chain:** the guide does not give a level for Supply Recovery or Tummies in Turmoil beyond the band "5 to 10". No level field exists in the record.
- **Not checked:** the wiki cache (`refs` `freerealms-wiki`) was not searched for these three quests. The guide's wiki claims are taken as given.
