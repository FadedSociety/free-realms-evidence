## 2. Blacksmith: Breaking a Sweat (quest 3020034)

- **Title:** "Blacksmith: Breaking a Sweat", locale **439710**. Hash ids: ucdt 105327908, 931792612; ugdt 3846306900.
- **Prerequisite:** Grunt Work (3020033). Order is evidenced by the text: the Grunt Work completion (439708) says "Let's see if you're really a Blacksmith." Wiki band level 1. Using the Anvil requires the Blacksmith job ("Sorry, you need to be a Blacksmith to use the anvil!", 1535610448, per the guide).
- **Intro (439709):** "So here's the deal, [*player*]. <BR><BR>You prove to me that you've got the iron guts of a true blacksmith and I'll take you under my wing so we can figure out what's been going on out here in the Valley.<BR><BR>Smelt some <font color='#D9781A'>Copper Bars and some Metal Cactoid Illusions</font>."
- **Description (439711):** "Red wants you to prove your Blacksmithing prowess. Craft a Sunstone Consumable at the Anvil in Red's camp."
- **Steps:**
  1. Smelt Copper Bars at the Furnace. Craft a Sunstone Consumable at the Anvil in Red's camp. Objective hashes:
     - "Do you have what it takes to become a Dwarven Blacksmith?... Smelt some Copper Bars at the Furnace then craft me some Sunstone Consumables." (3075346153, dat:81245)
     - "Red wants you to prove your Blacksmithing prowess. Craft a Sunstone Consumable at the Anvil in Red's camp." (3661792598, dat:97017)
     - "Forge the Sunstone Consumable!" (792695533, dat:20861)
  2. Return to Red. The completion text (439712) is the handover to Crackin' Skullz.
  - Targets: Copper Bar (smelted from Copper Ore). Sunstone Consumable (the output item; its id is unknown). Counts: unknown.
  - **Conflict:** the locale intro (439709) names "Copper Bars and some Metal Cactoid Illusions" as the second ingredient. The objective strings (3075346153, 3661792598) name Sunstone Consumables. The guide flags this line as a possible match to row 40 (Good Ol' Sunstones). Treat the second ingredient as unresolved.
- **Completion (439712):** "Impressive, [*player*].<BR><BR>This is some quality Blacksmithing.<BR><BR>You've got me sold on your abilities, so I'll fill you in, and maybe teach you some Dwarven flair along the way. <BR><BR>I'm not sure what we're going to stumble on to, but it'll be big…<BR>"
