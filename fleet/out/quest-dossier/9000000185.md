## 1. Brawler: Fight for Your Right (quest 3030040)

- **Title:** locale **72966** "Brawler: Fight for Your Right". Wiki: "members only". Guide row M21 (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Brawler.md:160`, ucdt 584305414 / ugdt 547667738).
- **Giver:** Rourke Ironfist, NPC 9000000185, Lakeshore (`npcs/lakeshore.json:12628`; name ucdt 2420135997).
- **Intro (offer), probable:** locale **72965** "The first rule of Brawler Club is, you do not talk about Brawler Club. … If this is your first time at Brawler Club, <font>you have to brawl</font>." Not confirmed as Rourke's speech, since no speaker is recorded.
- **Objective/description:** locale **72967** "Now that you know the rules of Brawler Club, you will need to prove you can fight before Rourke will let you join. Defeat Troll Roughguards in the Forest Troll fort." Same text as wiki/client 2126173344 (Brawler.md:160). Also locale **117190** "Defeat Troll Roughguards in the Forest Troll Fort as a brawler." (wiki id 771540081).
- **Steps:**
  1. Talk to Rourke in Lakeshore (`npcs/lakeshore.json` location evidence: locale 434419887 and 3927842639).
  2. Go to the Forest Troll Fort and defeat **Troll Roughguards**, count **5**. Video tracker "Defeat Troll Roughguards - 0/5" (Brawler.md:160, video FsCYVYzjnpg/00018, /00104, /00138). Locale 95017 "Defeat Troll Roughguards" is the tracker title.
  3. Return to Rourke. The turn-in text is probably locale **72968** "You defeated the trolls? Not too shabby..." (speaker and quest not confirmed).
- **Target resolution:** Troll Roughguards is an enemy name, no id resolved. The Forest Troll Fort is a dungeon (`refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/2603-49339-e149452f20c2.wiki` lines 7–16, listing Troll Roughguards among its mobs, "Levels 13 or higher").
- **Prerequisites:** Brawler level 15–20 band. "After M20" (Brawler: Reputation Regained, locale 72868) per Brawler.md:159–160. The skeleton lists `prerequisite_quest_ids: [3030039]`. I did not verify that 3030039 is Reputation Regained.
- **Rewards:** (R) none evidenced. Brawler.md:160 says "unknown". The skeleton's `rewards` is empty.
- **Evidence:** `quests/brawler.json:2201-2248` (skeleton); `npcs/lakeshore.json:12660-12708`; `notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Brawler.md:160`; `refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/1999-48172-2810e421b744.wiki:109`; locale 72965, 72966, 72967, 72968, 117190, 95017.

## 3. Brawler: Paying your Dues (quest 3030043)

- **Title:** locale **76265** "Brawler: Paying your Dues". Wiki: "Paying Your Dues", members only. Guide row M24 (Brawler.md:163; ucdt 4000587074 / ugdt 1582345642).
- **Giver:** Rourke Ironfist, NPC 9000000185.
- **Intro / offer:** locale **72971** "You certainly have the brawn to join Brawler Club, but even skilled brawlers like yourself have to pay their dues. All members get a weapon. Of course, you'll have to make a donation to the club and find the materials yourself, but it's worth it! Interested in earning one?" Same text as wiki 1732652847 (Brawler.md:163).
- **Objective:** (R) the donation and "find the materials" from the offer. The client text does not name the items or amounts.
- **Materials:** a crafting-material description, locale **181883626** "Used in the construction of Brawler weapons. Requires quest: 'Brawler: Paying your Dues.'" The item it names is not resolved in this pass.
- **Completion (probable):** locale **100418** "[*Player*] can now proudly wield their Brawler Club weapon!" Same as wiki 1071514084 (Brawler.md:163). Locale **72974** "Sweet weapon you have there! You certainly look like a Brawler Club member, but there's still one more thing you have to do..." probably hands off to Chaz (72991 "Chaz here has the final say…"). Not confirmed as this quest's text.
- **Steps:**
  1. Talk to Rourke and accept the offer (72971).
  2. Make the donation to the club and gather the materials (exact list (R): unknown).
  3. Rourke gives the Brawler Club weapon. The wiki names it **The Rumbler**, a level 20 Brawler weapon, Power 5, Glancing Blow and Leg Sweep (Brawler.md:163; `refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/4083-26869-96e6af753d9f.wiki` line 3: "acquired by completion of the quest Brawler: Paying Your Dues").
  4. The next step is Chaz Strongarm's Brawler Club work (locale 4024416966, 72991, 72994). Not part of this quest.
- **Target resolution:** The Rumbler is not found under that name in the client locale. The `client` search for "The Rumbler" returned only the item-name strings for Rowdy Rumbler / Brawler's Forged Hammer. Item id: unknown.
- **Prerequisites:** Brawler job, level 20 (the M22 gate), and M22 Achieve Level 20 (Brawler.md:163 "After M22"). The skeleton lists `prerequisite_quest_ids: [3030041]`.
- **Rewards:** The Rumbler weapon (wiki, Brawler.md:163; wiki 4083-26869). Coin and XP rewards are (R) unknown.
- **Evidence:** `quests/brawler.json:2338-2381`; `npcs/lakeshore.json:12753-12796`; `Brawler.md:163`; `refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/4083-26869-96e6af753d9f.wiki:3`; locale 76265, 72971, 72974, 100418, 181883626.
