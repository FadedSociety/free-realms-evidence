## Samantha's Mail

**Giver:** Samantha, npc 9000000189 (`cobblestone_village.json` record; `quests_given_text`).
**Hand-in:** Samantha.
**Quest id:** none evidenced. Any id is (R) and must be assigned by the builder.

**Titles**
- Quest title: "Samantha's Mail", Global 46179, ucdt hash 321852490; ugdt hash 3476095314 (`locale`; `en_us_data.dat` line 8543 and 92074).

**Offer / intro text**
- Intro text (description slot, Global 46158): "Samantha has asked you to gather her bundle of mail she accidentally left at the warpstone next to Sanctuary. Once you find the mail, you can use the warpstone to teleport back and give the mail to Samantha." (ucdt hash 3983686098).
- Samantha's speech (Global 47895): "I just teleported back here from Sanctuary a bit ago, but I left behind a bundle of mail. I'm pretty sure I left it next to the warpstone outside of Sanctuary. It looks just like the warpstone behind you and you should be able to see it from the road that leads to Sanctuary." (ucdt hash 602356893).
- I can't verify which slot each speech fills. 47895 is probably the offer dialog; 46158 is the journal description.

**Steps**
1. **Accept from Samantha.** Speech 47895 above.
2. **Find the bundle of mail** at the warpstone outside Sanctuary, on the road to Sanctuary.
   - Objective "Find Samantha's bundle of mail." (Global 46175; ucdt hash 385628149).
   - Detail: "Samantha believes she left the mail near the Sanctuary warpstone." (Global 46176; ucdt hash 2606054477).
   - Target object: "Bundle of Mail" (Global 140301; ucdt 4200210986, ugdt 650389044). No object or item id is resolved in `client/` Resources. No spawn coordinates are evidenced.
3. **Return to Samantha** in Cobblestone Village.
   - "Return to Samantha and speak with her." (Global 46177; ucdt hash 2697193771).
   - Short label "Return to Samantha" (ucdt 334642184).
   - If the player returns early, Samantha's reminder line: "Have you found my mail yet? As I said, I believe I left it near the warpstone just outside of Sanctuary. Please find it and return it to me. You can use the warpstone to return to this area once you find the mail." (Global 46181). Speaker not stated in the text, but it sits in Samantha's block.
   - Travel hint: "Once you have the bundle of mail, you can use the Atlas to teleport back to Samantha. Click the Globe icon on the right side of your minimap to bring up the world map. Locate Cobblestone Village, click and hold, and you'll be warped back to Cobblestone Village!" (Global 46178; ucdt hash 3738478450). The notes also cite a mouse variant (1289805815) and a controller variant (3652816074). I could not resolve those two through the `locale` tool, so I haven't checked their text.

**Completion text**
- Global 46160: "Awesome, you found my mail! I'm glad it was still there. Now you should be able to warp back to Sanctuary whenever you want to!" Speaker not stated; likely Samantha.
- Global 430998: "Once you have the mail, take a look at your map by Highlighting and selecting the Atlas from your Game Dock. You should see Cobblestone Village listed. Press the X Button after moving the cursor on the Warpstone next to Cobblestone Village, and hold your click, and you should teleport to Cobblestone Village. If you find other warpstones in the world, they will work the same way.<BR><BR>Thanks for finding my mail!" Likely the completion or hand-back text, since it ends "Thanks for finding my mail!"
