# Sir Whittington (npc 9000000205, Sanctuary): unbuilt quests

Quest ids 3150016, 3150018 and 3150019 are not in `quests/warrior.json`. That file stops at 3150010 (I searched for `31500\d\d`). Sir Whittington's giver records are therefore all unbuilt. ## 1. Warrior: Legend of Fastvi Frostflutter (quest id 3150018, guide W18)

  - 71443: "Use the nearby game table and reenact the &quot;Weed Killing!&quot; battle. It is one of Fastvi's first battles against evil."
  - 100757: "Use the game table near Sir Whittington and beat the &quot;Weed Killing!&quot; tower defense minigame."

- **Steps, in order:**
  1. Talk to Sir Whittington (npc 9000000205) and accept the quest (offer 71435).
  2. Play Weed Killing! on the Sanctuary tower-defense table beside him. The guide lists Easy, Medium and Hard modes (locale 14909, 388909, 388910). The table is "Weed Killing!" (locale 15135).

The wiki says it "was previously acquired by completion of the quest ''Warrior: Legend of Fastvi Frostflutter''" (refs `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/4349-42894-e316d9a84a84.wiki:4`). The item is retired and was only a quest reward per the wiki.
  - Coins and job XP: unknown (R). The builder would have to use a fallback.
