The Fisherman job guide does not agree: row 30 names "Merri / the buyers †" as giver, and row 31 names "Kati McScuttle". The built records follow the job guide, not the NPC guide. Ernest's `quests_given` in `lakeshore.json` lists only Partner Up. I could not settle this from the sources. The two built quests may need giver attribution to be checked, or a note that Ernest is the quest-text speaker.
