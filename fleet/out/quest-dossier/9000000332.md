## Slugmud Surprise (candidate, unbuilt)

- **Title:** unknown. The record gives only the hash 1744403283, and the locale tool takes string ids, not these hashes. I did not find a title string id. Locale 24262 "Slugmud!" and 24268 "NOT LIVE - Making Slugmud Surprise" (a dev label) are the only quest-like titles nearby. Neither is confirmed as this quest's title.
- **Giver:** Glerga, from the record (`npcs/wilds.json:18359`). Her cooking table is the training spot: "Touch the barrel to my right when you are ready to begin" (24246). The speaker of 24245/24246 is not named in the text, so this is inferred.
- **Intro/offer text:**
  - locale 24243: "You must find 10 Slugmuds if you are to cook the Robgoblin delicacy, Slugmud Surprise. Search under the rocks near the Robgoblin Camp for some."
  - locale 24892 (fuller revision of 24245-24246): "You now have the recipe for Slugmud Surprise, but to make it you're going to need the secret ingredient… It's Slugmud! Ha! You would have never guessed that. Now you need to <font>find ten Slugmuds</font>…"
  - locale 24245 and 24246 are the earlier plain variant of the same text.
- **Steps, in order:**
  1. Receive the Slugmud Surprise recipe from Glerga (24245, 24892). The recipe item is locale 1518 "Slugmud Surprise Recipe", with 1545 "Requires chef level 5."
  2. Collect 10 Slugmuds from under the rocks near the Robgoblin Camp. Objectives: locale 24227 "Find 10 Slugmuds!" and 24232 "Find some Slugmud!". The Slugmud item id is not resolved here. Check `client` Resources.
  3. Cook one Slugmud Surprise at Glerga's barrel. Objective: 24270 "Glerga wants to keep a watchful eye on you while you create a Slugmud Surprise. Make one with 100% accuracy." and 24271 "Make a Slugmud Surprise with 100% accuracy." Glerga's check-in: 24274 "…I will be keeping an eye on you though!" The cooking dish label is "Cooking: Slugmud Surprise" (386043). Locale 73330 "Score 10,000 on Slugmud Surprise" is probably a minigame goal from another quest. It is not evidenced as this one.
  4. Take the dish to Lucca De'Flor in Snowhill and challenge him to eat it. Objectives: 24280 "Line cook, Lucca De'Flor… Challenge him to eat a Slugmud Surprise." and 24281 "Talk to Lucca De'Flor and challenge him to eat a Slugmud Surprise." Glerga's hand-off: 24283 "…take it to Lucca De'flor in Snowhill and challenge him to eat it." Lucca's reply: 9869 "Glerga sent you to challenge me to eat some Slugmud Surprise…".
  5. Lucca's cooking-contest counter-offer: 24317. If the player wins: 24323 (plain) or 25004 (font variant), "Ugh… I can't believe you beat me at the cooking contest… Would you like a job helping me…". Lucca's follow-on job, 24320 "Create 3 Sizzling Snowberry Sautes.", is probably a separate quest.
- **Prerequisites:** Chef level 5 is the recipe's gate (1545), not evidenced as the quest's gate. Earlier quest: unknown. Family Style (3050030) may come before this, but no source says so.
- **Rewards:** none evidenced. (R) The recipe itself is handed over by Glerga, not confirmed as a reward. Lucca's win gives a job offer (24320), not an item or coins.
- **Completion text:** 24323 or 25004 (Lucca's win text). The Glerga-side end text is unknown.
- **Other Slugmud text, not this quest:** 35908 and 35911 (a Norvin delivery where "Our cook, Glerga made it"). 9904 (a Fizzlesticks refusal, speaker not confirmed). 73098 (Glerga, "You've earned the right to purchase my Slugmud Surprise recipe" after the Fried Dumplings/Merri step). 73098 is the Family Style aftermath, which suggests the recipe may be purchased from Glerga instead of given.
