## 1. Military Intelligence (dev 2-1)

- **Title:** "Military Intelligence", locale 441690. Also ugdt 52307788 and ucdt 3013672697 per the guide (`NPCs - Greenwood Forest.md` row 167).
- **Giver:** Glugmort. The first-person offer at 47871 is attributed to Glugmort in the zone JSON (`greenwood_forest.json` ~line 11941, "speaker proof: first-person Military Intelligence offer ... NPC row 59 lists Military Intelligence under Glugmort").
- **Offer text (intro), locale 47852:** "If we are to fight off the ever constant threat of the Thugawugs, it is important we maintain a secure camp. This requires constant military intelligence. To accomplish this, I have several patrols roaming the wilds and gathering information. <BR><BR>I haven't had time to collect their information yet however. Do you think you could go find one of the patrols and get their situation reports from them? Just click on that crate next to me to begin."
  - Variant 431008 is the same text with "interact with that crate" in place of "click on that crate".
  - Variant 47871 (offer, first-person, per the JSON note; the tool output was cut off): "If we're to fight off the Thugawugs, we've got to keep this camp secure. This requires constant patrols and reports. <BR><BR>Speaking of reports... could you go find the <font color='#D9781A'>patrols</font> and get their <font color='#D9781A'>situation reports</font>? The reports look like that parc[hment]..."
- **Steps:**
  1. Click the crate next to Glugmort to start the minigame. The minigame title is "Greenwood Situation Report" (47853; objective 47877 "Complete the "Greenwood Situation Report" minigame.").
  2. Find the patrols near the Greenwood Camp and get their situation reports (386583). Interact with Archers on Patrol: "The perimeter of the Greenwood Camp is secured by Archers on Patrol. Interact with them to tell them to report to Glugmort." (430992; also 45925 "Click on them to tell them to report to Glugmort.").
  3. Return to Glugmort. Interact with even more Archers on Patrol to deliver extra reports (431083; also 78741 "Click on even more Archers on Patrol to deliver extra reports to Glugmort.").
- **Reminder (not in the offer):** "Did you collect the situation reports from my patrol yet? Just click on that crate next to me to begin." (47881).
- **Completion text:** unknown. Not found.
- **Targets:** Archers on Patrol (the ambient NPC name, ucdt 33341219 per the guide). The count is not evidenced. Minigame "Greenwood Situation Report".
- **Prerequisites:** none evidenced. Dev order implies it is first in the chain (R).
- **Rewards:** none evidenced (R).
- **Evidence:** locale 441690, 47852, 431008, 47871, 47853, 47877, 47881, 386583, 430992, 45925, 431083, 78741, 441799; `NPCs - Greenwood Forest.md` row 167; `greenwood_forest.json` ~11418 (page_lists entry, Glugmort) and ~11941.
- **Note:** a separate quest "A Situation Report" (locale 45893, objective 45924 "Have the Archers on Patrol report in", 45932 "Help Glugmort collect the latest Situation Reports from his patrols.") looks like the same patrol loop. It is not confirmed as separate from Military Intelligence.
