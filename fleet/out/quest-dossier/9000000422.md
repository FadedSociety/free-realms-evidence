**Title.** "Snowberry Harvest", ucdt `3810427204` (data file line 100798; tool 434406). Matching group title "Farming: Snowberry Harvest", ugdt `2210515643` (line 58211; tool 434269), and ucdt `4144118315` (line 109542).

**Objective lines.**
- "Gather some Snowberry", ucdt `800737854` (line 21087; tool 139232).
- "Harvest Snowberry", ucdt `4265235179` (line 112590; tool 90192). Possibly a second objective or the harvest-tile name; the order is not evidenced.

**Completion text.** Not found. "Let Perry know you gathered all the ingredients!" (tool 384731) is unplaced; it may belong to the Chef/Starnut chain and is not attributed here.

**Steps (as evidenced).**
1. Start at Perry, who stands at Perry's Pastures. Interact with the harvesting tools behind her. Text: "Interact with any of the stacks of Harvesting Tools behind Perry to start harvesting." (ucdt `490868236`, line 13049; tool 490868236 is the id the record cites). Whether this is the quest's first step is (R).
2. Harvest Snowberry from the harvesting minigame / harvesting nodes. Objective text: "Gather some Snowberry" (`800737854`) and "Harvest Snowberry" (`4265235179`). The count is unknown. The harvest product is the item "Snowberry" (tool 69328; description "A Snowberry that appears to have been grown on a farm.", tool 69337, ucdt `5028468`, line 133). The inventory item is probably "Bundle of Snowberry" (item definition 2876, per `recipes.json:740` evidence), but linking it to this quest is (R).
3. Return to Perry. The turn-in text is not found; (R).

**Target ids.**
- NPC: Perry, ucdt `900174448` (npc_id 9000000422).
- Location: Perry's Pastures. Client sentence "Perry's Pastures is located just south of Snowhill, far to the north of here. You can harvest Snowberry and Starnut at Perry's Pastures." (tool 139840). Also "Perry's Pasture", ucdt `440810541` (line 11674).
- Item: Snowberry (see step 2).

**Prerequisites.** Level: unknown. Job: none stated. Quest: unknown. The Snowhill guide describes Perry as "Farmer; harvesting teacher." (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Snowhill.md:122`).

**Rewards.** Unknown. No reward text found. (R) nothing evidenced.

**Twin quest (unbuilt, by wording).** "Perry can't seem to harvest the Starnut she needs, help her by harvesting some!", ucdt `1041917974` (line 27600; tool 384729).
