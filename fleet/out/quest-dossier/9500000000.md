# Quests of Banshee (Blackspore Swamp)

## Verdict

No quest is evidenced as **given by the Banshee (npc 9500000000)**. The only candidate, "Defeat the cemetery banshee!", is the **main goal of the Bone Bog Cemetery dungeon (normal version)**. It is an instance objective, not an NPC offer. The `quest_giver` role and the `quests_given_text` entry in `blackspore_swamp.json` look like a misreading of the video frame TjpDl22Lwpk/00326, which shows the goal panel. Nothing should be added to `quests/*.json` for this NPC. The dossier below is kept so a builder can see the evidence and decide.
