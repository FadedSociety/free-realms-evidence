# Quests of Block Loblog (Blackspore Swamp)

## Summary

Block Loblog gives no quests. He is a dead dwarf whose log is the objective of one quest. The wiki and the guide file that quest under **Vrok/Vrock Gravelfist**, not under Loblog. The record's own `quests_given_text` ("Quest item, not a speaker") agrees. A builder should attach this quest to Vrock Gravelfist (repo `npcs/blackspore_swamp.json`, Vrock entry around line 39450), with Loblog as the place where the item is found. Only one quest is tied to Loblog, so only one dossier follows.
