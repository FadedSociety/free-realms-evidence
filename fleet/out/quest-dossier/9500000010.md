# Quests of Cakenstein (Blackspore Swamp)

## Summary

The record's quest line, "Event boss; the fireworks fight (2622338937)", is a Birthday Bash fight, not a quest that Cakenstein gives. The quest is **Downing Cakenstein**, given by **Commander Kiggle** in Sanctuary (wiki: "Downing Cakenstein from Commander Kiggle in Sanctuary - continuously repeatable"). Cakenstein is the target. The Blackspore record marks Cakenstein `quest_giver`, which looks wrong. A builder should attach this quest to Kiggle's record, not to Cakenstein.

Two IDs need care:
- **2622338937** is not a text id. `locale` returns "no such id". It appears only as a directory-index hash (`en_us_data.dir`, `2622338937 4214024 303 d`), and no matching text was found. It is not the title; the title is 2760856853 / 3696061630 (below).
- The `locale` tool uses its own numbering (e.g. "428097"). The client string ids are the `en_us_data.dat` ids. Both are given below, with the `locale` key in brackets.

**Steps (in order):**
1. Talk to Commander Kiggle in Sanctuary and accept the offer (dat 2159104087).
2. Launch fireworks at Cakenstein. Cakenstein appears during the Birthday Bash. The server line is "Cakenstein has risen in <zone>!" (e.g. Blackspore: 2930679067). While he is risen, fireworks are in crates on the paths of eight locations near him (wiki `refs:freerealms-wiki/raw/df131c7af32b0f0c9d89e1456a76bcf148b22bb03adff6465dd3881084f08146.json`, "Open World"). Per the same wiki page, he runs around eating cakes and gets stronger. His cakelings bring him cake, which makes him briefly invincible. Only fireworks hurt him, as the objective says.
3. Defeat him. He melts and drops cake piles and a treasure chest. The wiki calls this a "limited Birthday Bash reward"; it is not shown to be the quest reward.
4. Return to Kiggle. The completion line (dat 2957324687) is the turn-in text. Repeatable, per the wiki.
