# Quests of Donovan Shadeweaver (Blackspore Swamp)

# Donovan Shadeweaver (npc 9500000019, name 36332792): dossier for unbuilt quests

**Scope.** No quest in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json` names Donovan or any of his six titles, so all six are unbuilt. The record and the guide (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Blackspore Swamp.md`, Part A row 28 and series table rows 1–6, lines 192–197) list them. I built the four with the clearest Donovan-side text: Darci's Deficiencies, Favors for Favors, The Mining Operation and Combating the Cray.

**Id note.** The `locale` tool uses its own sequential string ids (for example 78765). The hash-style ids in the guide (for example 1000915545) do not resolve there. Where the raw `en_us_data.dat` grep found a hash id, I cite it as `refs:opening-mining-native-20261009T121330Z/locale/en_us_data.dat:<line>`.

## 1. Darci's Deficiencies

- **Title:** locale 78765 "Darci's Deficiencies". Dat hash 1000915545 (ugdt), refs dat line 26535.
- **Giver:** Donovan Shadeweaver (36332792). Hand-in is Donovan, with Darci Flitterstride (name 1086712442) as the field target.
- **Offer, Donovan (locale 78764):** "I hired a scout, Darci Flitterstride. Her work has been, how shall I put it... less than impressive. Want her job? Find Darci and tell her I've hired you as her assistant. I hope for your sake you prove more capable."
- **Quest description (locale 78766):** "Donovan hired Darci Flitterstride to help scout better settlement locations in Blackspore since Mire's Edge is slowly sinking into the swamp. She hasn't lived up to his expectations."
- **Steps:**
  1. Find Darci at her camp in Mire's Edge and become her assistant. Objective: locale 78779 "Assist Darci Flitterstride in Mire's Edge to make sure her job gets done." Hash 1107321833 "Have you found Darci in Blackspore Swamp?" (refs dat line 29326) may be a Darci line but is not clearly this quest's.
  2. Darci's scouting brief (locale 78776): "Donovan asked me to scout the swamp for better camp locations. The current camp seems to be sinking into the mire. I have one location left to scout, just south of Passwind Pass, but there's cray all over there! Clear out the cray and take some notes on the area."
  3. Objective locale 78782 "Investigate near Passwind Pass". Clear the cray there; the guide also gives Darci's line "full of cray" (2969729964) and the report to Donovan (4214346881), but I did not pull those texts.
  4. Return to Donovan. Completion (locale 78767): "These findings are unfortunate, but you have done well. As a token of my thanks, I'm giving you Darci's payment as well. She may be a tad upset about losing out on the coin, but that's what she gets for dragging her feet."
- **Prerequisites:** Easy Money is the guide's series order (row 2 before row 3). Level, job, and any other gate are (R)/unknown.
- **Rewards:** Darci's share of coin, per locale 78767 (amount unknown). Anything beyond coin is (R)/unknown.
- **Evidence:** locale 78764, 78765, 78766, 78767, 78776, 78779, 78782; guide Part A row 30 and row 194 ("Donovan pays Darci's share to the player (1390094198)").

## 2. Favors for Favors

- **Title:** locale 90201 "Favors for Favors". Dat hash 818014811 (ugdt), refs dat line 21580.
- **Giver:** Donovan Shadeweaver (36332792). Target: Ramirez (name 1008426580, locale 45305 "Ramirez").
- **Donovan's setup (locale 90200):** "I allow Ramirez to hunt for precursor relics at a site further into the swamp. In return he gives me a cut of his finds. I haven't heard from him in a week. Go find out where my artifacts are."
- **Donovan's second line (locale 90202):** "Ramirez is a little overdue on filling his relic quota. Donovan would like you to go collect on his debt."
- **Steps:**
  1. Find Ramirez in Blackspore. Objectives: locale 90267 "Find Ramirez in Blackspore and collect Donovan Shadeweaver's payment."; locale 90268 "Speak to Ramirez". The dig site is "Ramirez's Dig Site" (locale 384227), in the swamp.
  2. Ramirez's relic-quota condition. Dat hash 529218929 (refs dat line 14055): "Ok, listen, this has been a down week for me. I had a cold, and I haven't found enough artifacts yet. If you want to get Donovan his payment, you're going to have to help me gather them." The guide ties this line to Ramirez (guide row 73). Possibly related to locale 91776 "Hunt for precursor artifacts at Ramirez's dig site in Blackspore." and 91773 "Help Ramirez in his prospecting duties." Those are not confirmed as this quest's, and the artifact count and item id are unknown.
  3. Return to Donovan. Completion (locale 90203): "Glad to see that Ramirez still remembers who his friends are. And now as I once did him a favor, you have done ME a favor, and I am in your debt. Thank you."
- **Prerequisites:** Darci's Deficiencies is the guide's order (row 4 after row 3). Other gates are (R)/unknown.
- **Rewards:** not evidenced. Donovan's thanks in locale 90203 names no item or coin.
- **Evidence:** locale 90200–90203, 90267, 90268, 384227; refs dat lines 21580, 14055; guide Part C row 73 and series row 4.

## 4. Combating the Cray

- **Title:** locale 90586 "Combating the Cray". Dat hash 2532363899 (guide, series row 5).
- **Giver:** Donovan Shadeweaver (36332792). Monster targets: Cracked Claw (name 4053599865, boss of Cracked Claw Caverns, guide Part D row 130); zone "Cracked Claw Caverns" (locale 30402).
- **Donovan's intro (locale 90585):** "You have proven quite capable, but Blackspore is a dangerous place... The swamp cray are a constant problem, and the big one, Cracked Claw, needs to be kept in check. Handle it."
- **Quest text (locale 90587):** "The swamp cray need dealing with, and Donovan in Mire's Edge has given the task to you."
- **Steps:**
  1. Clear the swamp cray. Likely Cray Marauder (locale 76573) and Cray Marauder Captain (locale 76574). Locale 76571 "Defeat the Cray Marauders." is a plausible objective but not tied to this quest by text. The guide also says "Defeat Cray Marauders, then Cracked Claw".
  2. Defeat Cracked Claw in the Cracked Claw Caverns. Objectives: locale 90595 "Defeat Cracked Claw, in the Cracked Claw Caverns of Blackspore."; locale 90597 "Defeat Cracked Claw". Guide cites 4179127062 for "Defeat Cracked Claw".
  3. Return to Donovan. Completion (locale 90588): "Mire's Edge is a little bit safer and more profitable thanks to you. Well done, I knew you'd prove up to the challenge."
- **Prerequisites:** The guide's series order puts it fifth, after Favors for Favors (row 4). Level and job are unknown (R).
- **Rewards:** unknown. Nothing evidenced.
- **Evidence:** locale 90585, 90586, 90587, 90588, 90595, 90597, 30402, 76571, 76573, 76574; guide Part D row 130 and series row 5.
- **Caution:** locale 76413 ("Find their leader Cracked Claw and defeat him to lessen their aggressive nature!") and 76570 are unattributed. They may belong to another giver, so I did not use them.
