1. Talk to Greyli Dustywings in Sanctuary (NPC 3541). Accept "A Trip to the Swamp" (415319). Offer text 415318.
2. Go to Blackspore, near Bone Bog (location evidence "In Blackspore", near Bone Bog, 1190753171). Find **Ethan** (NPC name 2444404965; `npc_id` 9500000022 in `npcs/blackspore_swamp.json:10451-10520`, placement POI 16 "Blackspore", medium confidence).
3. Recover the **Spectral Spectacles** (item **69831**, "Spectral Spectacles", `refs:FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Resources/ClientItemDefinitions.json:1193140-1193141`) from Ethan. The objective is 415387. The step mechanic (talk, kill, or steal) is unknown.

In fact, with your help, I bet we can fix them." The text suggests this is the hand-in, but it is not labelled as a completion line in the source.
