## Quest 1: Howl of War

**Giver:** Jake Blackforest (name 1273485489; locale 432094), Blackspore Swamp.
**Series:** the Team Werewolf chain in the Super Spooktacular. The wiki lists it as the last of three: Howl of Hunger, then Howl of Warning (from Lon Blackforest), then Howl of War (`refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/3113-48700-6d070a6b2cf5.wiki:48-50`). The wiki's note at line 52 says the last quest is not offered if the other side's last quest is accepted but not finished.

**Title:** "Howl of War", locale **432294** (ucdt 234157957). Also ugdt 3424675022. Sources: `locale`; `repo: notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Blackspore Swamp.md:116`.

**Intro / offer (likely):** locale **432331**: "Ah, you're the one that Lon told me not to eat. Well, friend, there's something I'd like your help with.<BR><BR>There's about to be a dance-off between us and the vampires. I want you to sabotage their dance practice to ensure our victory!<BR>"
- Why this is the offer: Jake's voice, the same task as the objective text, and the Team Werewolf setup. It is not adjacent to the title id, so the id link is inferred from content.

**Hand-off line (likely, unassigned):** locale **432288**: "You've got a foot in the door with the werewolves, and now it's time to help them win the dance off. Give them an edge in the dance-off by sabotaging the vampires' dance practice."

## Mismatch to fix in the record

- The NPC record says Jake is "boss of Vampire Dance Practice (1919166048)". The text under ucdt 1919166048 (locale 432023) says: "The vampires have given you their powers to help them sabotage the werewolves' dance practice. Defeat the werewolf leader Jake Blackforest!" So Jake is the boss of the **Werewolf** Dance Practice, which the vampire side attacks. `repo: notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Werewolf Dance Practice.md:18, :45` agrees.
