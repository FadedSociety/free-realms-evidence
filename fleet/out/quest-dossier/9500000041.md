## 2. The Pumpkin Prince (offered by Lucinda, inside Bone Bog Cemetery)

**Title:** hashes `3506549451` (ucdt, anvil dump line 92851) and `3549407549` (ugdt, line 93998). No locale table id for the quest title was found. The locale entry `414863` "The Pumpkin Prince" is the enemy's name.

**Prerequisite (wiki order):** "Sealed with a Signet" from Lord William IV. In that quest the player places the signets on the Prince's headstone and the Prince escapes (locale `416103`: "Finally, I've been released from my tomb! I have you to thank, [*player*]... Now I am free to raise my undead army in Bone Bog Cemetery."). Evidence: `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/3113-48700-6d070a6b2cf5.wiki` lines 16–18; `repo:...Bone Bog Cemetery.md:95`. Level: the wiki says a level 13 combat job is needed to enter the battle (weak, one source: `repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Bone Bog Cemetery.md:30`).

**Intro (offer), Lucinda:**
- locale `415337`: "I came running as soon as I heard all the commotion. Are you okay?<BR><BR>I can't believe you came face to face with the Pumpkin Prince!"
- locale `415338`: "Those scheming ghosts tricked you into opening the Pumpkin Prince's tomb!<BR><BR>We're in for a world of trouble now. Sacred Grove will be swarming with ghosts and ghouls again! We can't let him get away with this.<BR><BR>Meet me in <font color='#D9781A'>Bone Bog Cemetery</font>, and I'll help you send that pumpkin-faced prince back where he belongs!"
- Speaker: the record says Lucinda meets the player inside the cemetery. The speaker tag for 415337/415338 is not checked in the dump, but the content matches.

**Objectives, in order:**
1. Meet Lucinda inside Bone Bog Cemetery: locale `415340` (hash `405585147`): "Lucinda has agreed to help you send the Pumpkin Prince back where he belongs. Meet her inside Bone Bog Cemetery to plan your attack!"
2. Defeat the Pumpkin Prince with Lucinda's help: locale `416122` (hash `3465256454`): "With Lucinda's help, defeat the Pumpkin Prince and send him back to the Underworld where he belongs!" The goal line is `416121` "Defeat the Pumpkin Prince". The chat log shows "The Pumpkin Prince Goal Complete: Defeat the Pumpkin Prince" (`repo:...Bone Bog Cemetery.md:43`, video `0LoQe5VZ0PY/00098`–`00102`).
3. After the fight: "Speak to Lucinda", locale `416007` (hash `3248953191`). Chat log: "New Goal: Speak to Lucinda" (video `0LoQe5VZ0PY/00102`).
- Entry: "Speak to Lucinda just outside of Bone Bog Cemetery." (locale `416124`, hash `3325099950`). It is the entrance hint (client; `Bone Bog Cemetery.md:20`). It is not confirmed as a quest step.

**Completion text (Lucinda):** locale `415163`: "Let's get out of here! I'll talk to you outside when you are finished here." (quoted in `Bone Bog Cemetery.md:57` with the boss-fight video). The turn-in reward text is not found.

**Boss-fight context:** the event instance is Bone Bog Cemetery. Its boss is The Pumpkin Prince, with Lucinda as the friendly NPC. The dungeon itself is `Bone Bog Cemetery.md` (level 5, difficulty 3). The open-world Pumpkin Prince (Sanctuary, Snowhill, Briarwood, Blackspore) is a separate Super Spooktacular minigame and is not this quest (`refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/3113-48700-6d070a6b2cf5.wiki` lines 65–74).

**Rewards:** the wiki says the open-world Prince's chest gives a Mystery Gift and 10 Ectoplasmic Goo (`3113-48700...wiki` lines 59, 74). It is not evidenced that the quest pays these. The chest text "Pumpkin Prince's Treasure" (`loc 2369094282`) is cited in `Bone Bog Cemetery.md:71`, which is not verified here. Quest reward: (R), unknown.
