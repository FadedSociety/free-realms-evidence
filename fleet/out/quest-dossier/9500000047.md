# Milford (npc 9500000047, Blackspore Swamp): unbuilt quest dossier

Milford is Eggert's apprentice. None of his quests are in `quests/*.json` yet. `adventurer.json` has Eggert's own "Chapter 1" quests (3000049 = title 90957, "The Alchemy of Betrayal"). Those are not Milford's and are not covered here.

Locale numbers below are the `locale` tool ids. The 10-digit keys in the record and in `opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat` are hash keys for the same strings. Quest ids are not evidenced anywhere, so none are given. Use a (R) batch key.

Four quests are written up, in this order: Idle Hands, Follow Your Nose, Pulling Weeds, Grave Blossoms. Ordering between the three ingredient quests is inferred, not evidenced.

## 1. Idle Hands (find Milford)

- **Title:** 90731 "Idle Hands" (dat key 2057409462).
- **Giver:** Eggert's intro (90730) sends the player to find Milford. The NPC record lists Idle Hands among Milford's quests (`npcs/blackspore_swamp.json:22047-22049`). Eggert's own record also lists it (`npcs/blackspore_swamp.json:9929`).
- **Intro (90730):** "My pupil dances in the swamp... <font color= '#D9781A'>Milford</font>, is his name I think. <font color= '#D9781A'>Find him</font>, and have him return to me with his wonderful gifts.<BR><BR>Mischief may lie in idle hands, but there is far, far worse that dwells in the idle mind, little wanderer."
- **Journal line (90732):** "Eggert wants you to find his assistant Milford, and see what's taking him so long."
- **Steps:**
  1. Search Blackspore Swamp for Milford. Objective 90745 "Search for Milford in Blackspore Swamp." (key 1641429655).
  2. Talk to Milford. Objective 90746 "Talk to Milford" (key 4293802580).
  3. Milford's offer (90733): "The master grows weary of waiting for his ingredients? I must work twice as fast! <BR><BR>If you are willing to help me, I'll make it worth your while. I have many things I still need to find!" This opens the ingredient series.
- **Prerequisites:** none evidenced. Level and job are unknown (R).
- **Rewards:** none evidenced.
- **Evidence:** `npcs/blackspore_swamp.json:9929, 22047-22049`; `opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:43450` (1641429655), `:113325` (4293802580); wiki `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/10472-50421-a3de604228de.wiki:12` ("Eggert offers a quest to seek out his apprentice, Milford...
