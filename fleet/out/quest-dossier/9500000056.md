## 1. Crystals are Forever

**Title:** "Crystals are Forever", locale key **2088007857** (ucdt; ugdt key 3651465134). Client `locale/en_us_data.dat:55038` (ucdt) and `:96737` (ugdt). Tool row 76053 sits between the offer and the objective text.

**Intro / offer text:** key **809438111** (tool row 76052), client `locale/en_us_data.dat:21315`:
> "Dwarves need power crystals to fuel their machines, and people say <font color='#D9781A'>Cracked Claw Caverns</font> is full of <font color='#D9781A'>crystals</font>...<BR><BR>Bring me some samples, maybe we'll get lucky and they'll be the kind we can sell for fuel."

The offer text does not name its speaker. Oswald is attributed by the record and the guide (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Blackspore Swamp.md`, row 34; `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Cracked Claw Caverns.md`, line 104), which pair key 809438111 with his crystal errand.

**Objective text (quest log):** key **1250819232** (tool row 76054), client `locale/en_us_data.dat:33105`:
> "Search the Cracked Claw Caverns for any crystal samples. Oswald in Mire's Edge can determine their value."

**Return objective:** key **1468578823**, "Return to Oswald in Mire's Edge" (tool row 76103), client `locale/en_us_data.dat:38875`.

**Completion text:** key **2542932015** (tool row 76055), client `locale/en_us_data.dat:66968`:
> "Bah! These crystals are standard fare, they wouldn't power a glow worm! <BR><BR>I suppose you're still entitled to something for your trouble. Take this and leave me be."

**Steps, in order (inferred from the text):**
1. Accept the crystal errand from the dwarf (key 809438111).
2. Go to the Cracked Claw Caverns (Blackspore) and search for crystal samples. Objective text 1250819232. A second objective, "Search for crystal samples in the Cracked Claw Caverns of Blackspore." (key 4250170219, tool row 76093), may be this step's tracker wording.
3. Return to Oswald in Mire's Edge (key 1468578823). He values the samples.
4. Receive the completion line (key 2542932015). "Take this" implies a reward handed over at this point.

**Target ids:** the sample item is not resolved. The client has "Crystal Fragments" (key 647452684, and 4279405016 ugdt) and a Cracked Claw "crystal fragments" line (3774750118). The guide's entries for this quest (`Cracked Claw Caverns.md` line 104) cite only the three text keys. Whether these fragments are the samples is unproven.

**Prerequisites:** level, job and earlier quest unknown. The zone is Blackspore (Mire's Edge giver, Cracked Claw Caverns target). Donovan's camp is in Mire's Edge, but no gating is evidenced.

**Rewards:** none evidenced. The completion line implies an item ("Take this"), so any reward is (R).
