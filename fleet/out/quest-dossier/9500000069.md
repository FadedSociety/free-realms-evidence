- **Level, job, prerequisites:** none evidenced for any of the three.
- **Rewards:** only the party favor or mystery gift is evidenced, and only from the wiki's "first reward" field. No coin amounts, XP, or extra items are evidenced, and none are marked (R).
- **Outfit and pet targets:** no item ids are resolved for the luau outfit, winter outfit, or pet. The wiki hint names "tacky sweaters" and "marketplace costumes" for Winter Party; no locale id confirms this.
- **Nudge and wrong-outfit responses:** locale 422851 and 422852 are probably response lines, but the mapping to a step is not evidenced.
- **Quest ids:** the quests have no internal ids. The wiki's revision ids (31362, 31275, 35916) are the only citation keys.
