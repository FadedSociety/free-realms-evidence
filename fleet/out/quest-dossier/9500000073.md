### Quest 1 (unbuilt): It's Alive! (the "animated essences" bride quest)

**Giver correction.** The bride quest is not given by the Tormented Spirit. The giver is **Finklestein** (NPC name ucdt 195202075; ugdt 542885802), a Halloween NPC in **Pixiewood Hills, Sanctuary**. The spirits are only the collection target. The Blackspore record and guide list the quest under the spirit because the essences come from it.
