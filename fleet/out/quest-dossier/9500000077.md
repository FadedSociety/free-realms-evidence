Checked: no `quests/*.json` file mentions it (search for "Fresh Ingredients", 1793419492 and 2067348619 found only notes and npc records), so it is unbuilt.

The frog/toad is the gathering target, not the giver. - The offer text names Sal ("Sal needs some fresh ingredients...", locale 4246361889).
- The wiki Sal Monella page lists "Quest:Fresh Ingredients" (`freerealms-wiki/wikitext/18530-50407-1ffbf330645c.wiki`, lines 11-13).
- The guide's Sal Monella row (`NPCs - Blackspore Swamp.md:31`) lists Fresh Ingredients among his quests.

Suggested fix: move the quest to Sal Monella (3178725636), with the frog as a target.
