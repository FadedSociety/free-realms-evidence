3. Find Chicory inside the cave (target: Chicory, `ucdt` 1236740789; the cave's dungeon requires a level 5 combat job, `ucdt` 2145075890, per `Mushroom Cavern.md:26`).
4. Chicory has become a shambler after eating a cave mushroom. Sperry's reaction (`ucdt` 649861896): "You found Chicory? He is a shambler??! He must have eaten one of the mushrooms in the cave, the fool. I feel bad for threatening to fire him now. We must help him." (locale row 17209 / 16909)
5. Get a cure. Sperry asks for a mushroom from the cave (`ucdt` 301159145: "Go into the Mushroom Caverns and find me a mushroom. Then (`ucdt` 216020562): "I believe I have found a cure for Chicory. Take this to him and see what it does. Perhaps he will learn not to eat unidentified mushrooms. I swear that boy must be addled in the brain." (locale row 16924). The cure's item id and the mushroom's item id are unknown.
6. Take the cure to Chicory (the line "Take this to him" implies the hand-in target is Chicory). Hand-in dialogue is not evidenced.
7. Return to Sperry. Completion text (`ucdt` 2262517085): "It's about time you returned. I am surrounded by slackers! Did you find Chicory? Good. I guess a thank you is in order." (locale row 9518)
