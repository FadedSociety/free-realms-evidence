# Quests of Chugawump (Briarwood)

# Chugawump (npc 9500000103): Test Your Mettle

Only one quest is listed for this NPC, and it is not built. `quests/*.json` has no match for "Mettle", 1858118349 or 72872, and no quest there cites Chugawump. The evidence is good for the title, the fight objective and the offer text. It is weak on the return step and the rewards.

**Id note.** The `locale` tool uses its own ids. The guides and the `ucdt` keys use another set (for example `loc 1858118349`). The two sets match by identical English text, which I checked for every string below. Tool ids are given first, then the `ucdt` key in brackets where the guide cites it.

## Quest 1: Test Your Mettle (quest id unknown, (R))

**Title.** `72872` "Test Your Mettle" [ucdt 1858118349]. The same title is also used by an unrelated copper-ore quest, `439860` "Do you have stones in your ears? I'm trying to test your mettle here. Go collect Copper Ore and Sunstones from Sunstone Copper Veins around the valley." [ucdt 948067489]. That text is not part of this quest.

**Giver.** Chugawump (npc 9500000103, Briarheart, Briarwood). The record's `quests_given_text` and the NPC row in the Briarwood guide both give him as the quest-giving NPC. The guide's quest table says "Giver not shown", and its NPC table lists Mugred as the return NPC. So the giver is supported by the record, and the return NPC is unresolved.

**Intro (offer), Chugawump's dialog:**
- `72863`: "Stand tall, soldier! This is no time to be slackin' off. I'm always on the lookout for someone to fight, to test my skills in combat and maybe teach them a thing or two. Do you want to give it a go?"
- The accept reply is `140274`: "So you have decided to accept my challenge. Good! I'm pretty sure I'll win but give it your best try anyway!"
- Evidence: locale 72863 and 140274. The wiki and video `Huk5snVvXMI/00006, 00008` are cited in `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Chugawump.md:20`. The guide's "game's own text" is the same string as 72863.

**Journal description:** `72873` "Defeat Chugawump in single combat. He is always looking to test his skills against new opponents." [ucdt 2355713854]. The guide attributes it to this quest (`Chugawump.md:75`).

**Objective lines:**
- `72922` "Defeat Chugawump" [ucdt 3748953664]. This is the only goal shown ("Goals (1)"). Evidence: `Chugawump.md:36`, video `Huk5snVvXMI/00006, 00054`.
- Return step: the guide's video reading is "Return to Mugred" (`NPCs - Briarwood.md:302`). The locale has no "Return to Mugred" string. It has `72932` "Return to Chugawump" [ucdt 1701600098] and `72933` "Speak to Chugawump when the battle is over." [ucdt 3149972022]. Which string this quest uses is unresolved. The guide places 72932 and 72933 among "related" strings but does not tie them to this quest.

**Completion text:** `72871` "Defeated Chugawump in single combat!" [ucdt 430567491]. The guide lists it as related text (`Chugawump.md:37`). It is probably the fight-won message, but its link to this quest is not proven.

**Steps (in order):**
1. Click Chugawump in Briarheart. Read `72863`, then accept (`140274`). The NPC's dialog is the offer.
2. Defeat Chugawump in single combat (`72922`). He is a boss with a shield phase (`423162` "Chugawump put up a shield and is taking less damage!", `423163` "Chugawump lowers his shield and is causing more damage!"). Enemy id: `w58_chugawump_l13_boss` (`prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/enemies.json:224`, kind "Chugawump"). The enemy level 13 is the wiki's (R) minimum, not a recovered value.
3. Return to finish. The NPC is either Mugred (video, guide) or Chugawump (`72932`/`72933`). Mugred's id is 575131702 per the guide (`NPCs - Briarwood.md:247`). Mugred is listed in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npc_spawns_extra.json:24997`. He is not placed, and his own quest is a different one (Neelix, `73001`/`73011`). Unresolved.

**Prerequisites:**
- Combat job at level 13 or higher. This is from the wiki ("Level 13, Any Combat Job", `Chugawump.md:27`) and the boss's difficulty. It is not tied to this quest by name, so treat it as (R).
- No earlier quest is evidenced. The guide says "Unlocking quest: Test Your Mettle starts the same fight" for the battle node. It does not say the quest is needed to click the boss. (R).

**Rewards:**
- Chat line on completion, video `Huk5snVvXMI/00054`: "You receive 11 stars and 13 coins" (`Chugawump.md:56`).
- Wiki: 13 Stars, a 33-coin wheel slot, and "Members Only Bonus: 68 Coins" (`Chugawump.md:53-55`). The wiki's prize wheel is per job. It is battle-level, and I have not tied it to this quest.
- Conflict: 13 stars (wiki) versus 11 stars and 13 coins (video). The guide flags this (`Chugawump.md:82`).
- Item rewards: none evidenced for this quest (R).
