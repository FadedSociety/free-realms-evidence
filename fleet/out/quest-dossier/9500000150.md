# Quests of Joey the Lost (Briarwood)

## Summary

Joey the Lost (npc 9500000150, ucdt 2300336944) is not a quest giver. His record's `quests_given_text: ["Target."]` is right: he is the target of one quest. The quest is **Lost Little Joey**, which is offered by Andrew's wife, named **Mary** in the locale. The record's `quest_giver` role should be moved to her, or Joey should be marked as target-only. Lost Little Joey is not built (no hit for "Lost Little Joey", "741444917" or "412774568" under `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/`).

Locale-tool row numbers (e.g. 17283) and client hash ids (e.g. 741444917) are different keys. Both are given below. Row numbers were read with the `locale` tool; hash ids were matched in `FreeRealmsLocaleTools/test/.../en_us_data.dat` (same text on the same line).
