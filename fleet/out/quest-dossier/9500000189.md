> "Old Corky would like you to plant trees to replace the ones that have been felled."

Evidence: refs `en_us_data.dat:63260`. The "seeds" are the "Seeds." reward/item noted at `NPCs - Briarwood.md:156`.

**Steps (inferred from the lines above):**

2. Take the seeds from Corky. Item id: unknown (the guide lists "Seeds.").

Return/deliver lines `46355128` "Return to Old Corky" (refs `en_us_data.dat:1209`) and `4146709955` "Deliver to Old Corky" (refs `en_us_data.dat:109605`) are also present, but I could not tie them to this quest or to Quest B.

**Prerequisites:** level, job and earlier quest all unknown (R).

3. Recover several axes. Count unknown ("several"). Item id unknown.
4. Bring the axes back to Old Corky. Possible lines: `46355128` "Return to Old Corky" (refs `en_us_data.dat:1209`); `3590280992` "Inform Old Corky of your success." (refs `en_us_data.dat:95196`). Attribution to this quest is not certain.

**Prerequisites:** the "one final task" wording in `3219176615` suggests this is the last of a chain. That the chain includes Quest A is (R). Level and job unknown.
**Rewards:** not evidenced (R).
