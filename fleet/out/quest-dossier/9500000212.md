## Quest B: Let's Slothi's father know he is alright. (journal: "Let Slothi's father know he is alright")

**Title:** locale 16544 "Let's Slothi's father know he is alright." (ucdt 144747789, en_us_data.dat:3802). The guide and record cite it as "Let Slothi's father know he is alright" (144747789, 825261180).

(ucdt 825261180, en_us_data.dat:21782). The Bristlewood reading is backed by 46540 "Lazi can be found at a farm in Bristlewood to the north of Slothi." (ucdt 3527898521, en_us_data.dat:93409) and by the NPC guide's Lazi row (NPCs - Briarwood.md:88: "A farm in Bristlewood to the north of Slothi").

3. Talk to Lazi. Completion text, Lazi speaking (locale 16571 / 9630 / 52258 "You found my son, Slothi? Oh, thank you for letting me know he is ok. I wish he would come back and help out around here.
