- **Locale id notes:** the locale tool's row ids (e.g. 94358) and the client's hash ids (e.g. 3294784671) are different numbering. The hash id is the one the repo cites.
- **Giver:** Majorie (name ucdt 1605089228; `locale` row 78658). Her dog Spot is folded into her row. The guide says the town giver is "unknown" (Pet Trainer.md row E3). The Cobblestone record's npc_id is 9500000256 (`npcs/cobblestone_village.json:8651`), which is named "Spot" and holds Majorie's roles. Use it as the candidate giver, marked (R).

She's across the street, in front of the Pet Adoption Center."). Also "Majorie is near the Pet Adoption Center in Cobblestone Village." (2385252622; row 90498).

## Gaps

- **E3 title, completion text, rewards and prerequisites are not in any source.** Only the offer text (3294784671) and the "Talk to Majorie" line have clear evidence, and the objective match is inferred.
- **E3 giver:** the Pet Trainer guide says "unknown". The Cobblestone record id 9500000256 is a candidate only, and that record is named Spot.
- **Giver ids on the built tutorials are not Majorie's.** `quests/pet_trainer.json` gives Pet Tutorial: Basics a giver of 9000000489, and Happiness and Training a giver of 9000000452. These are placeholder keys in `npcs_unresolved.json` (lines 3473 "same trainer" and 3870 "the junior trainer chosen"), not Majorie's NPC id. The built records should be re-pointed to Majorie's NPC id, or the giver should be left unresolved.
- **Become a Pet Trainer (3120001)** has giver 9000000235, which is Zachery's id (`notes/Autopilot/T316.md:152`). The quest is shared by all three junior trainers, so this is also a mapping question, not a Majorie-specific one.
