# Quests of Chatty (Greenwood Forest)

## Scope

Chatty (npc 9500000294, Camp Chatty, Greenwood Forest) gives two quests that are not yet built in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json`: **Meet and Greet** (the only quest his wiki page lists) and **Battles** (a new-player station quest the Highroad notes attribute to him). Nothing in `quests/*.json` matches "Meet and Greet", "Battles", "Chatty" or the ids 180476835, 3003015076, 2871256052 (grep of the folder). The quest folder has no Chatty file.

Not included: **Confronting Bigfoot** (locale 429074) and **Bigfooted Friend** (locale 429188). Chatty is only the turn-in for Confronting Bigfoot ("Return to Chatty", locale 429059). Its giver is Counselor Edmond (`NPCs - Greenwood Forest.md` row 40). Bigfooted Friend is given by Robbie (row 44). **The Great Treeble Race** is Counselor Jake's (row 42).
