## 1. Confronting Bigfoot (strongest evidence)

- **Title:** locale 429074 "Confronting Bigfoot" (hash 394863758; ugdt 3309872787 per guide).
- **Giver:** Counselor Edmond, npc 9500000297 (Camp Chatty).
- **Offer / journal text:** locale 429075 "Edmond and the other camp counselors can't seem to make peace with Bigfoot. Sadly, it's time to drive it away from the camp grounds." (hash 2092064077 per guide).

- **Steps, in order (from guide and client text):**
  1. Speak to Edmond. Objective locale 429053 "Speak to Counselor Edmond" (guide: hash 4116429320).
  2. Go to Bigfoot's Lair (dungeon, Wilds/Greenwood, southwest of Camp Chatty). Objective 429067 "Find Bigfoot's Lair" is possibly this step, but the guide does not say so.

  4. Return to Chatty. Objective locale 429059 "Return to Chatty" (guide: hash 73896818).
  5. Talk to Bob. Objective locale 429089 "Talk to Bob" (guide: hash 3108351032). Locale 429076 is Robbie's line "Don't call me Bob!" and 429087 is Chatty's reaction "Haha! Bob was behind the Bigfoot scare?... Head to the other camp and give Bob my regards, will ya?" (guide: hash 75220103). The guide lists 429087 as the line that follows this step.
- **Prerequisites:** level and job unknown. The guide says the Bigfoot boss needs combat job level 5 or higher, and that is for the dungeon, not confirmed for this quest.
- **Rewards:** "You receive 1 Camp Chatty Hat" (video `L27vxozHOls/00326`, per guide). Item name locale 16979 "Camp Chatty Hat". Quest-completed line for this quest: not found in locale.
- **Evidence:**
