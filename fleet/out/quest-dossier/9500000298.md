  2. Chase off the Camp Chatty counselors. Jacen's first-person quest line is locale **429370**: "Those mean ol' camp counselors from Camp Chatty just won't quit! Enemies are Counselors Tyra, Edmond, Jake and Kaela, plus bears. Source: `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Bigfoot Defender.md:20,46,74-79`, citing the wiki.
     - Counselor Kaela's npc id is 9500000301 (the guide gives no npc ids for the others; `notes/Autopilot/T299.md:93`). Quest-target ids for the four counselors are not resolved.
  3. Return to Jacen. Objective "Return to Counselor Jacen" is locale **429378**.
  4. Turn in. Completion text is locale **429373** and, with identical wording, **820703868**: "Hopefully, Bigfoot will be OK for today. If those counselors from Camp Chatty come back tomorrow, I'll let you know! Thanks, [*player*]."
