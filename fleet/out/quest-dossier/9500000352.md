# Quests of Len Tyril (Lakeshore)

## Summary

Len Tyril (npc_id 9500000352, `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/lakeshore.json:7475`) is **not evidenced as the giver of any quest**. The only quest that names him is a group quest, **Lakeshore Reports**, where he is one of three guards the player must visit. The client does not name who gives Lakeshore Reports. The one built-quest candidate below is the only unbuilt quest that involves him, and it is marked as giver-unknown.

Note on ids: the record and the guide use the client's hash keys (ucdt, e.g. 4074649206). The `locale` tool uses its own line numbers (e.g. 76072 is the text whose ucdt is 4074649206). Both are given below.
