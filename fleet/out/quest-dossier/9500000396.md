## Forget-Me-Not (seasonal, Festival of Hearts, Merry Vale)

**Title**: "Forget-Me-Not". Global.Text 427169; ucdt 2721422270 (dat line 71659); ugdt 2196278491 (dat 57835). (The ugdt key is for the journal/quest-name form, the ucdt key for the title.)

**Giver**: Joyce, NPC name id 427282. Placement: legacy spawn at (-1112.485, -40.11236, -402.6696), `FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Scripts/Zone/FabledRealms.lua:1042` (spawnNpcWithGuid 2296). Same NPC id in `Sanctuary/src/Resources/Npcs.json:5931-5937` (Id 2296, NameId 427282, "Joyce") and `FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Resources/Npcs.json:6312-6314`. The quest's placement at the Merry Vale mail stop is from the guide only (`NPCs - Merry Vale.md:211`).

**Offer / intro text** (the sentence that describes the quest, probably the offer):
- 427170 (ucdt 2611687715, dat 68733): "Joyce is so certain that she gets more forget-me-nots than anyone else in Sacred Grove that she's willing to give up her biggest gift to anyone who can get more."
- Joyce's speech, possibly the offer dialogue (ucdt 2333594798, dat 61383; Global.Text 427168; the locale tool cut the line, the full text is from the dat file): "Oh, how loved I am! I got another forget-me-not today. Everyone knows the more forget-me-nots you receive, the more special you are, and no one's as special as me! I bet my biggest gift that you can't get as many forget-me-nots."

**Objective lines, in order**:
1. Objective "Receive forget-me-nots" (427308; ucdt 2286294952; dat 60188). Journal description: "Receive forget-me-nots from friends! Throw a few at them, and maybe they'll throw some back." (427307; ucdt 3379171481; dat 89369).
2. Where to get the cards: "You can pick up forget-me-not cards near Postmaster Vixie in Merry Vale. Throw a few, and maybe friends will throw some back!" (427309; ucdt 2615060581; dat 68820).
3. Return step: "Return to Joyce" (427310; ucdt 2998603566; dat 79167).
4. Completion objective: "Let Joyce know you've managed to get more forget-me-nots than her!" (427311; ucdt 3889686517; dat 102828).

**Joyce's hand-in or taunt line**, placement unknown: "How many forget-me-nots have *you* received? No where near as many as me, I'd bet." (427312; ucdt 1549944269; dat 41034).

**Steps (as evidenced)**:
1. Talk to Joyce (NPC 2296) and accept Forget-Me-Not. The offer is 427170, with the speech 427168.
2. Get forget-me-not cards from Postmaster Vixie at the Merry Vale mail stop (427309).
3. Throw cards at other players to receive forget-me-nots (427307, 427308). Being thrown at counts toward the total. The client gives no count.
4. Return to Joyce (427310), and let her know you have more forget-me-nots than she does (427311). Completion is a comparison against Joyce's count, not a fixed number. The fixed count is not evidenced.
