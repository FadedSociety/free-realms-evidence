## 1. The Party Pooper (best evidence: a real server quest record)

- **Title:** "The Party Pooper", locale **385597** (guide ucdt 3648425452 / ugdt 1619426854).
- **Quest id:** 3070 (`Sanctuary-main-20261019/src/Resources/Quests.json:681-706`; the same record is in `Lxzxrus-Free-Realms-Server/src/Resources/Quests.json:669-694`, where the giver is given as `GiverGuid 100000002177`).
- **Giver:** NPC **2177** = Mayor McFairy (`GiverNpcId 2177`, `GiverDialogueId 385596`).
- **Target:** NPC **4355** = Wugunbump (`Npcs.json:17714-17719`, NameId 40344). The Wugunbump line is also in the Lxzxrus copy as `TargetGuid 100000004355`.
- **Intro (giver dialogue, 385596):** "You seem to have a knack for making people merry! There's a chugawug in the east camp that is ruining our perfect atmosphere.<BR><BR>Why don't you take him some of our amazing cotton candy and see if you can *cheer him up*?"
- **Description (385598):** "Wugunbump is an unhappy Chugawug visiting Merry Vale with his family. Cheer him up with some cotton candy."
- **Objective (385601):** "Cheer up Wugunbump at the Chugawug camp."
- **Goal (Type 0):** name 385602 "Speak to Wugunbump"; description 385603 "Try cheering Wugunbump up with the famous Merry Vale cotton candy!"; goal dialogue 385599 (Wugunbump's refusal): "Keep that stuff away from me! Sugar makes me feel so gross!<BR><BR>Everyone else around here seems to love that stuff though."

**Steps**
1. Get cotton candy in the town. The welcome line "Welcome to Merry Vale!!! Would you like some cotton candy? Here, take some extra to share! … Circle around town, and *share your cotton candy*!" (385493) and "Sharing is caring! Pass out cotton candy to guests that look like they could use a pick-me-up!" (385495) read as the set-up. Speakers are not stated, so this is by wording only. The cotton candy item id is not resolved.
2. Talk to Wugunbump (NPC 4355) at the Chugawug camp with the cotton candy (goal 385602/385603).
3. Return to Mayor McFairy. Evidence is by wording only: "Hi again! Be sure to share that cotton candy! Talk to the mayor if you hand it all out." (385629). The server record has no return step; the chain's next step is 3071 (below).

**Prerequisites:** Lxzxrus record `PrerequisiteQuestId: 0`. Sanctuary has no prerequisite field. Level and job: unknown.
**Chain:** `NextQuestId 3071` = "Suspicious Serenity" (TitleId 74199, giver 4355; `Sanctuary Quests.json:707`). That is Wugunbump's quest, not McFairy's.
**Rewards:** `RewardCoins 6`, `RewardExperience 60`, `RewardItems []` (Sanctuary:690-693; Lxzxrus:679-681). Gear: none recorded.
**Notification:** `NotificationAvailable 2`, `NotificationActive 6` (icon 43278). Not documented as text.
**Evidence:** `Sanctuary-main-20261019/src/Resources/Quests.json:681-706`; `Lxzxrus-Free-Realms-Server/src/Resources/Quests.json:669-694`; `Npcs.json:5331-5336` (2177), `:17714-17719` (4355); locale 385493, 385495, 385596, 385598, 385599, 385601, 385602, 385603, 385629; guide `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Merry Vale.md:186`.
