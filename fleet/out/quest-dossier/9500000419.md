**Intro / offer text**

1. **Take the sasparilla shipment** from Postchug Runalug in Wugachug. Evidence for the speaker is only the intro text and the guide row. Item id: unknown.
2. **Carry it to Rimpadun** in Merry Vale, at the little Chugawug encampment. Objective texts: "Rimpadun in Merry Vale needs his sasparilla shipment!" (1589517611; locale 46573). "Deliver the sasparilla to Rimpadun" (locale 46574). "You can find Rimpadun at the little chugawug encampment in Merry Vale." (3362927797; locale 46575). I suppose you should return to Postchug Runalug and tell him his shipment got here safely, but you should come back to Merry Vale! We sure do have a lot of fun here. Well, here's payment for the sasparilla. See you aro[und]..." (text cut in the tool output).
4. **Return to Postchug Runalug** in Wugachug (the "tell him his shipment got here safely" line above). Report-back text evidenced as 2688819018 ("Return to Postchug Runalug in Wugachug and tell him what happened to his missing postchug.", dat 70801).
