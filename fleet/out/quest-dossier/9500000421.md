# Quests of Rosalie (Merry Vale)

## Summary

Rosalie (npc 9500000421, name locale 117030) has one client-evidenced quest connection: the bouquet errand "Postman: Beautiful Bouquet" (title locale 117017). That quest is **already present** in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/postman.json` as record **3130015**, so I did not build a new record. I found no second quest for this character.

Two corrections to the Rosalie record:
- **Rosalie is probably not the giver.** The offer text (117016) is spoken by an unnamed friend who asks the player to deliver a bouquet to Rosalie. Rosalie is the delivery target ("Talk to Rosalie", 117022). The record's `roles: quest_giver` and the `quests_given_text` entry describe the errand but do not show her as giver.
- The record's npc_id 9500000421 is the target, not the giver. The friend's id is unknown.
