### Quest 1: "Bixies Gone Bad" (rescue the Queen in the Bixie Hive)

**Title.** "Bixies Gone Bad": locale 15335 (tool id), hash `1823028492` ucdt (`FreeRealmsLocaleTools/test/FreeRealmsLocaleTools.Tests/data/en_us_data.dat:48141`; same in `opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:48153`). A separate string, "NOT LIVE - Bixies Gone Bad" (`781507191`, data .dat:20577), suggests the title was once marked not live. The title is not tied to any guide, so its link to this quest is by adjacency only.

**Offer (intro) text.** Locale 15888, hash `2785360215` ucdt:
> "Some bixies have gone bad and have captured the Queen! You must help her! Go into the Bixie Hives and save her before it is too late."
(`.dat:73385`). The offer's speaker is not named. The Hive guide cites this text as a companion of the main rescue (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Bixie Hive.md:97`).

**Objective lines.**
- Locale 16791 / hash `1777163323`: "Save the Queen from unruly Bixies." (`.dat:46932`). This is the dungeon's main goal (`Bixie Hive.md:36`; `dungeons/bixie_hive.json:286`).
- Locale 15339 / hash `1068589297`: "Save the bixie queen in the Bixie Hive next to Sanctuary." (`.dat:28310`). This is the location hint.
- Locale 1540338003 "Save the Bixie Queen" (`.dat:40812`). Cited in the Hive guide as the objective under the "Confront" chain (`Bixie Hive.md:98`). Its quest attribution is uncertain.
- Bonus goal (dungeon, not quest-evidenced): 342484617 "Save the Frightened Bixie Workers!" (`Bixie Hive.md:37`).

**Steps (in order, as evidenced).**
1. Go to the Queensfields door in Sanctuary and enter the Bixie Hive (difficulty 1; no level requirement; `Bixie Hive.md:18, 26`; wiki `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/2860-49119-53908a1959a7.wiki:10`).
2. Fight through the unruly bixies, Bixie Soldiers and Bixie Battlemages, to reach and save the Queen. Client ids for the enemies: Bixie Soldier 440711, Bixie Battlemage 440712 (`prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/field_battles.json` battle 12370 entry). Drone Fauzz (name 38510, boss) arrives after the waves. The Queen's lines: 2990020112 "Enter the Bixie Hive and rescue the Bixie Queen from the attacking rebels." and 375112226 (Fauzz betrayal line).
3. Optional bonus: free the four Frightened Bixie Workers (wiki `Bixie Hive.md:37-41`).
4. Turn in to the Queen. Candidate completion text: 1879695276 "The bixie queen has been saved and the uprising has been calmed." (`.dat:49590`). The Hive guide attributes this to the other chain (672547916 "We now know the location...", `Bixie Hive.md:98`), so the completion line is **uncertain**.

**Prerequisites.**
- Level: none evidenced. Difficulty 1 "can be entered by anyone" (wiki, `Bixie Hive.md:26`). The quest's own level gate is unknown.
- Job: none evidenced.
- Earlier quest: none evidenced. The Hive guide says "Unlocking quest: None" (`Bixie Hive.md:29`).
