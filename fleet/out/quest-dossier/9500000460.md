# Quests of Cakenstein (Sanctuary)

## Summary

The record's quest label, "Defeat Cakenstein; fireworks fight", is a guide paraphrase, not a locale string. The quest it points to is **Downing Cakenstein**, and the evidence gives it to **Commander Kiggle** (Sanctuary), not to Cakenstein. Cakenstein (npc 9500000460) is the target. The record's `roles: quest_giver` and `quests_given_text` for Cakenstein look like a mis-attribution (see Gaps). The dossier below is for the quest, with Kiggle as giver.

Cakenstein's other Birthday Bash quests named on the wiki (Perfecting the Batter, All Cakensteins Must Die, Forgivable Matter) are Glerga's. Cakey Madness is General Glowlight's. None of them is this character's, so I did not build them.

### Steps (in order)
1. **Accept from Commander Kiggle** in Sanctuary (428096, 428677). Kiggle's NPC row: `sanctuary.json:14276`.
2. **Go to Cakenstein's fight in Queensfields**, the zone edge "just outside of Queensfield" (428094 and 428653 "Defeat Cakenstein in Queensfields"; wiki Cakenstein page lists the Queensfields battle). Cakenstein's entry is the Birthday Bash boss: name 2839953702, `sanctuary.json:11177`, npc_id 9500000460. The Queensfields location is also cited in `sanctuary.json:11202-11204` as "Queensfields (581950168), other zones too".
3. **Fire Party Fireworks at Cakenstein.** Locale 9042 "Party Fireworks". The wiki says fireworks come from crates on the paths (`refs:.../df131c7...json`, "Open World": "fireworks appear in crates on the pathways"; Birthday Bash page lines 37-50). Item id is unresolved. Cakenstein must be defeated with fireworks only (428098). Count: unknown.
4. **Defeat Cakenstein** (enemy; cakelings may spawn and heal him, per wiki).
5. **Return to Commander Kiggle** (428680) for completion (428099).

## Gaps
- **Giver mismatch.** The record for Cakenstein (`sanctuary.json:11197-11209`) lists `quest_giver` and "Defeat Cakenstein; fireworks fight". The evidence puts this quest with Kiggle. Recommend moving the giver to Commander Kiggle (npc 9500000465) and treating Cakenstein as the target only. The "quests_given_text" label is a guide paraphrase, not a locale string.
- **No server quest definition.** No quest id, step counts, flags, repeat window or reward item for Downing Cakenstein is in the repo, the research coverage file (`notes/Research/original-quest-coverage.json`, which has no Birthday Bash quest journal entries) or the wiki. Those fields are all (R) or unknown.
- **Speakers unverified.** 428096 and 428099 have no speaker in the evidence. The attribution to Kiggle is inferred.
- **Item ids unresolved.** Party Fireworks (locale 9042) has no resolved item id. The reward item is unknown.
- **Queensfields spawn vs. Sanctuary spawn.** The wiki's Vs. Cakenstein list (Birthday Bash page lines 40-46) does not include Sanctuary or Queensfields. Where Kiggle's fight spawns is therefore not confirmed.
