# Quests of Camellia (Sanctuary)

## Camellia (npc 9500000461, Sanctuary, Birthday Bash party planner)

Not the Arch Druid Camellia (`npcs/shrouded_glade.json` 9500000650). The guides say so too: `NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:121` and `:167`. No quest for this NPC is built yet. `quests/*.json` has no "A Piece of Cake", "Undelivered Confections" or "Birthday Cake Bake-Off". The only hit is `vendors_unresolved.json:10404` ("Quest:A Piece of Cake"), an unresolved vendor reference.

Three quests are listed. Ordered by evidence: A Piece of Cake, then Undelivered Confections, then Birthday Cake Bake-Off.

### 1. A Piece of Cake (daily, Birthday Bash only)

- **Title:** locale 428037 "A Piece of Cake". Locale-dump title id ucdt 409701653 (`refs: opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:10843`).
- **Offer / detail:** locale 428035 "Camellia, the royal party planner, wants you to grab a slice of today's daily baked good." Wiki summary matches (`refs: freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json:115`).
- **Objective text:** locale 428480 "Camellia, the royal party planner, wants you to grab a cupcake." Objective lines: 428481 "Grab a cupcake" and 428483 "Return to Camellia".
- **Steps:**
  1. Speak to Camellia in Sanctuary. Text 428033: "Hey, [*player*]! Did you grab a cupcake today? No? Well, you should!" (wiki: `freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9869-28956-4a0a7f005388.wiki:15-16`).
  2. Grab a cupcake: select the floating tray of cupcakes near Camellia (wiki `:17-18`). Item id not resolved; locale 4895 "Cupcake" is the only item-name hit.
  3. Return to Camellia (428483).
  4. Completion: Camellia's text 428036 "Oh, you ate the last cupcake? I hope it tasted delicious! It looks like we'll need to pick up another order of cakes." (wiki `:19-20`). This hands off to Undelivered Confections.
- **Target ids:** NPC Camellia, name string 2124725291 (`npcs/sanctuary.json:11676-11730`, which has position [-1415.99, -27.63, 359.02] and location "Near the World Tree (2476668007)"). Cupcake item id: unknown.
- **Prerequisites:** only during the annual Birthday Bash event (wiki `:7`). Repeatable daily (wiki `:5`). Level: unknown.
- **Rewards:** first reward "Mystery Gift" (wiki Quest2 template, `quest-pages.json:119`). Item id unknown. Coin amount: unknown.
- **Evidence:** `npcs/sanctuary.json:11676`; `NPCs - Sanctuary.md:132`; `NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:167`; wiki `wikitext/7912-44814-fd93476b7f62.wiki:7` and `wikitext/9869-28956-4a0a7f005388.wiki`; `quest-pages.json:115-119`; locale 428033, 428035, 428036, 428037, 428480, 428481, 428483.

### 2. Undelivered Confections (follows A Piece of Cake)

- **Title:** locale 428042 "Undelivered Confections". Locale-dump title id ucdt 1622536963 (`refs: opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:42948`).

- **Steps (as evidenced):**

The speaker and which quest it belongs to are not confirmed. It may be the A Piece of Cake completion or this quest's offer.

- **Prerequisites:** A Piece of Cake, then Birthday Bash event (wiki Birthday Bash list). Level: unknown.

### 3. Birthday Cake Bake-Off (Birthday Bash, sponsorship)

- **Title:** locale 428081 "Birthday Cake Bake-off". Locale-dump title id ugdt 960700330 (`refs: opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:25429`). The guide's spelling "Bake-Off" is a variant.
- **Offer text:** locale 428082 "Camellia wants you to speak with each of the chefs in the big birthday bake-off competition, so that you can figure out whom you'd like to sponsor."
- **Objective / explanation text:** locale 428080 "The cake bake-off is the best part of the birthday party. Everyone is sponsoring a chef these days. All you have to do is pick a chef and then help them! You should speak to all the chefs and see which one you want to sponsor. What do you say?"
- **Steps (as evidenced):** speak to the competing chefs and choose one to sponsor. Chef pitch lines exist, e.g. 428443 "I'm going to win this bake-off because I'm the best! If you sponsor me, I'll make you proud." and 428422 "Try my cake! If it's good enough, I might be able to compete in the big cake bake-off!". The chef roster is not confirmed for this quest. Per-chef "Helping the Local Chef" lines (428086, 428103, 428107, 428111, 428115, 428119, 428123, 428127) belong to the separate repeatable Helping the Local Chef quest (locale 428085).
- **Completion:** locale 428083 "They are all pretty awesome, aren't they? Now, you just have to go help out whoever you choose... I hope the chef you choose to sponsor can win the competition. Good luck, [*player*]!" This may be the closing line, but it is not confirmed as the completion for this quest.
- **Prerequisites:** Birthday Bash event (wiki Birthday Bash list). Level: unknown.
- **Rewards:** no evidence. (R) unknown.
- **Evidence:** locale 428080-428083, 428422, 428443; `wikitext/7912-44814-fd93476b7f62.wiki:16`; `wikitext/8079-50098-b4832217b740.wiki:14`; `NPCs - Seaside.md:239` (title id 2440607895, a different hash). No wiki page for "Quest:Birthday Cake Bake-off" is cached. Only links to it were found.
