## 2. "Food for All" donation quest (NOT resolved; not built)

The record gives client id **2126401800** and title "unknown". That id does not exist in the locale table (`2126401800: (no such id)`). No quest with that id was found in the client database.

The Helga/Food For All text in the locale does belong to two quests, both marked **NOT LIVE**:
- **Charity line:** locale 23827 "NOT LIVE - Charity". Intro 23828: "You may find at times that you have cooked too much food for you or your friends to eat. Waste not, want not. Why don't you donate the food to the Food For All organization? They deliver food to all the needy people around the world." Objective 23829: "Harvest 10 Gummyfruit and 10 Sunleaf." Then 23831 "Take the harvested ingredients to Helga Humbleloaf." 23832 "Helga Humbleloaf can be found in Sanctuary." 23833 and 23834 give the Helga/FFA intro and text.
- **Bigger version:** locale 24048 "NOT LIVE - Food for All". Intro 24049: "Helga is taking donations of fresh vegetables to help feed the less fortunate. Harvest 25 Gummyfruit and 25 Sunleafs and return them to Helga in Sanctuary." Objective 24050: "Harvest 25 Gummyfruit." Return text 24054: "Return the harvested fruit to Helga Humbleloaf."

These are the best evidence for a "Food for All" donation quest, but both are NOT LIVE in the shipped client, and neither matches the record's unresolved id 2126401800. I have not built them. The record's "wiki not found" is consistent with this. A builder should decide whether a NOT LIVE quest belongs in the set before building either one.

Other Helga-related locale strings (Food For All gathering events at 408667-408836, the Jenny Ironstomach pie quest at 46041-46050, "Helga the Goon" at 117242) belong to other givers or events. I did not attribute them to Helga's quest list.
