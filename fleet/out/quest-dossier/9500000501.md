- **Chest name:** locale **5102617** "Lost Treasure Chest" (also 188174247, 3183625195, in the same name family).

#### Steps, in order

1. Speak with Kara Loft (northwest of the Sanctuary Warpstone). She gives one riddle from the 14 below. Locale: long form 5102589–5102602, each ending "That must be where your treasure is!". How the riddle is chosen is not evidenced. The wiki lists all 14 under Steps, but not the selection rule.
2. Go to the place the riddle names and use the clue to find the lost treasure chest (objective 5102588; the short riddle line is the objective text in 5102603–5102616).
3. Open the Lost Treasure Chest (5102617). Whether a return to Kara is needed is not stated.
