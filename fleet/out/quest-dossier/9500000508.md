**Steps (in order, as the evidence allows).**

2. Travel to Merry Vale. Enter the caverns through "the secret passage behind the mushrooms near the Den of Secrets" (`442598`). The wiki calls the entry a "swirling portal of Gloam magic" (16877 line 36).
3. Defeat Necronomicus in the group battle (encounter 432, "Encounter 432 - Necronomicus' Lair", `5100662`). 4. Destroy the zombie portal (dev strings "Destroy Zombie Portal 1–8", `1233963050`; unassigned to this fight).
5. Defeat the powered skeletal form. This ends the fight.
6. Complete the quest (`442601`).
- **Unknown:** whether the quest requires one fight (the 13 version) or the Awakened fight (`442600`), and what order they come in. `442597` ("defeat him at his full power") suggests the quest's fight is the Awakened one, and `442598` suggests a return visit. Mark both as **(R)**.
