- **Alternate completion, ucdt `2007929038` (dat:52877):** "I can't believe those <font color='#d9781A'>hooligan delinquents</font> stole my candy!<BR><BR>It's so nice of you to offer to get it back for me." (Likely the in-progress or return-to-giver variant; the source does not say.)

- **Prerequisites:** Super Spooktacular event window. Locale 194928811 (dat:5146) says "trick-or-treat every day in Sanctuary during the Halloween celebration." Level, job gate and prior quest: unknown. The guide's "(Miner)" tag (`NPCs - Sanctuary.md:65`) is the only job evidence.
- **Rewards:** (R) "the biggest piece out of the bunch" of candy, from the intro. No item id and no XP or coin amount is evidenced.
- **Evidence:** dat keys above; `NPCs - Sanctuary.md:65`; `sanctuary.json:49986-50016`. The wiki cache has no "How Rude" page.
