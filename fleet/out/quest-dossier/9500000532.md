# Quests of Sergeant Larkspur (Sanctuary)

# Sergeant Larkspur (npc 9500000532, name 2094170236): Southern Wilds chapter 1 (Queensfields bixies)

**Scope.** Larkspur gives a four-part chain. The client's dev labels order it as "SGRevamp - Wilds South - 1-1 Bixie Skirmish", "1-2 Bixie-B-Gone", "1-3 Spray and Play", "1-4" (title not found) and "1-5 Into the Hive". None of it is built: no file in `quests/*.json` cites these titles, ids or Larkspur (`search` over `quests/` finds only unrelated Medic and Postman hits). The record's quest list is the 1-2 spray caravan job plus the four Heroes Journal items. Those items are 1-1 (3939074800), 1-4 (3940458382), 1-5 (2925884091) and the caravan (3050504235), which is the 1-2 offer. The four sections below are the most evidenced parts, in chain order.

Locale ids (`locale` tool numbers, e.g. 441674) are cited with the client hash key (ucdt/ugdt) from `opening-native-lobby-20261009T163603Z/locale/en_us_data.dat` (same in the other opening-* builds). The hash keys are the record's ids. Quest ids, levels and rewards are not in any source.

## 1. Bixie-B-Gone (chapter 1-2): the caravan job
- **Title:** "Bixie-B-Gone", locale 441674; ucdt 3272766846, ugdt 3158841696 (dat lines 86449, 83383). Dev label "SGRevamp - Wilds South - 1-2 - Bixie-B-Gone" is locale 441767 (ucdt 2942558815, dat 77631).
- **Giver:** Sergeant Larkspur (name ucdt 2094170236, dat 55216; NPC record `npcs/sanctuary.json`; guide `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sanctuary.md:40`).
- **Prerequisite:** 1-1 Bixie Skirmish (chain order only; no explicit prerequisite text). Level: unknown.

**Intro (offer), locale 441675 (ucdt 3050504235, dat 80579):** "Sergeant Larkspur, an injured royal guard just outside of Sanctuary, needs you to track down a missing caravan and return with the cargo."

**Objective, locale 441673 (ucdt 2553062471, dat 67268):** "Our caravan of <font color='#D9781A'>Bixie-B-Gone bixie repellant</font> from Merry Vale should have arrived by now. I fear the worst with these recent bixie attacks.<BR><BR>Please <font color='#D9781A'>track down the missing caravan and return with any cargo that you find!</font>"

**Steps:**
1. Accept from Larkspur (intro 441675).
2. Find the caravan, which is south of Queensfields (wiki `Necronomicus` page: "the missing caravan south of Queensfields"). The guide gives no coordinates.
3. The caravan is on fire with no passengers. Locale 441676 (ucdt 2466506152, dat 64925): "You found the caravan on fire, but there were no sign of any passengers? I wonder what happened to them.<BR><BR>In any case, you were able to salvage three cases of the Bixie-B-Gone bixie repellant. Now let us put them to good use!" (Item: "Bixie-B-Gone Spray" ucdt 2571891690 / ugdt 2175923530 is the likely item name; item id not resolved. The three cases are the count.)
4. Mysterious Stranger: the wiki says a "Mysterious Stranger" (later Necronomicus) is met near the missing caravan during this quest (`freerealms-wiki/wikitext/16877-50130-b385ebd250b0.wiki:14`, Quest:Bixie-B-Gone listed at line 59). The client text does not show this; wiki-only.
5. Return to Larkspur. Locale 441677 (ucdt 2206482218, dat 58112): "With the return of the Bixie-B-Gone spray canisters we are able to take the fight to the bixies! <font color='#D9781A'>Use this sprayer</font> to chase away some of the nearby <font color='#D9781A'>Bixie Soldiers</font> and <font color='#D9781A'>Bixie Battlemages</font>! ... Come back and talk to me..." This is the hand-off to 1-3 (inferred from the text).

**Completion:** 441677 is the closest completion line. The text is the hand-off, not a reward line (the reward is unknown).

**Rewards:** (R) unknown. No reward line is evidenced for this step. Only item: three cases of Bixie-B-Gone (441676).

## 2. Spray and Play (chapter 1-3)
- **Title:** "Spray and Play", locale 441678; ucdt 4094828787 (dat line 108169), ugdt 2917117196 (dat 76945). Dev label "SGRevamp - Wilds South - 1-3 - Spray and Play" is locale 441775 (ucdt 3719543812, dat 98426).
- **Giver:** Sergeant Larkspur.
- **Prerequisite:** 1-2 Bixie-B-Gone (chain order; 441677 hand-off). Level: unknown.

**Intro (offer), locale 441679 (ucdt 1822330904, dat 48127):** "Sergeant Larkspur, an injured royal guard just outside of Sanctuary, has requested that you use the Bixie-B-Gone you retrieved to spray the Bixie Warriors and Bixie Battlemages."

**Objectives:**
- Locale 441776 (ucdt 2942807942, dat 77645): "Spray Bixie-B-Gone on the bixies in the Eastern Bixie Fields. Speak to Sergeant Larkspur if you run out of spray." Eastern Bixie Fields is locale 442487.
- Locale 441779 "Spray Bixie Battlemages" (short objective).
- Locale 4420135 (ucdt 2589133751, dat 68113): "Use your Bixie-B-Gone spray on the Bixie Soliders and Bixie Battlemages in the fields to the west. Come back and talk to me when you have sprayed enough!" (The "west" and "Eastern" wording conflict; the guide does not settle this.)
- Generic return objectives "Return to Sergeant Larkspur" (441765, 441773, 441781, 441789, 441797) exist but cannot be tied to one quest.

**Steps:** accept from Larkspur (441679) → spray Bixie Warriors/Battlemages (Bixie Soldiers in the 4420135 text) with the spray, in Eastern Bixie Fields → refill from Larkspur if empty (441776) → return.

**Targets:** Bixie Warriors, Bixie Battlemages, Bixie Soldiers; the count is not evidenced ("enough" in 4420135). Enemy ids: the Battle Template and Field Enemies note give Bixie Soldier NPC 2920/2932/2933 (`notes/Design/Field Enemies.md:32-34`); these are spawns, not confirmed quest targets.

**Completion, locale 441680 (ucdt 488140423, dat 12982):** "Brilliantly done! We used to use the spray to keep the bixies out of town, but lately we have had to use more and more to try to keep our citizens safe.<BR><BR>Now let us turn our attention to a more lasting solution!"

**Rewards:** (R) unknown.
