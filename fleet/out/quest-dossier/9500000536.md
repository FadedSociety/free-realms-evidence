## 1. Milk Rocks (Sunflower variant, the rock-collection quest)

- **Title:** "Milk Rocks", loc 403233 (dat ucdt 775617196; ugdt 934814554). Same text is the activity name in `notes/Research/original-activity-coverage.json` lines 11119-11120 (id 594, name_id 403233, type 1, difficulty 2, members_only false, restoration_status "missing-implementation").
- **Giver:** Sunflower 9500000536 (name 7669). The offer names him, so the giver is evidenced.
- **Intro/offer (Sunflower variant):** "Sunflower in Sanctuary wants you to collect some rocks", loc 403351, dat 495719647 (`client:locale/en_us_data.dat:13181`).
- **Description:** "Sunflower is competing for the largest rock collection in Sanctuary! You've been asked to gather some rocks, so he can win a milk carton signed by The Dares!", dat 2169745116 (line 57154). Loc 403234 is the same text for Perry ("Perry is competing ... so she can win").
- **Steps (evidenced in text, count not stated):**
  1. Talk to Sunflower, who gives the offer.
  2. Play the "Milk Rocks" game, loc 403346 ("Play the "Milk Rocks" game"), dat 3094112236 (a minigame, with activity id 594 above).
  3. In the game: "Find as many rocks as you can, but watch out... you're not the only one looking for them." (loc 403347). Collect them with the bucket: "Click on the bucket" (loc 403344) and "This bucket is sturdy enough to carry quite a few rocks." (loc 403345).
  4. Return to Sunflower: "He's waiting patiently in Sanctuary for you to return with the rocks." (loc 403357). The Perry return line is loc 403349 ("She's waiting patiently at her pastures near Snowhill"). The Bordon Cloo intro is loc 403359 ("Bordon Cloo in Sanctuary wants you to collect some rocks"), so this is a shared chain.
- **Completion text (not assigned to Sunflower):** loc 403235 "Look at all these rocks! Thanks, [*player*]!... Don't forget... Milk Rocks! Spread the word." (dat 923062867). Also dat 1742382663 (line 45983): "Thanks to you some of the kids at the Ayani Hospital can join the rock collection merriment... I hear that Perry near Snowhill and Sunflower in Queensfields may need your help too." This text names Sunflower as a future helper, so it is probably a completion for another giver in the chain.
- **Prerequisites:** unknown. (R) none evidenced.
- **Rewards:** not evidenced. The only prize in the text is the milk carton signed by The Dares for the largest collection (a competition prize, not a quest reward, per 2169745116). (R) none.
- **Evidence:** `client:locale/en_us_data.dat:13181, 57154, 45983`; loc 403233, 403234, 403235, 403232, 403343, 403344-403349, 403351, 403357, 403359; `notes/Research/original-activity-coverage.json:11119`.

## 2. Milk and Honey (Sunflower variant of the Royal Honey text)

- **Title:** "Milk and Honey", loc 403221 (dat ucdt 1698661714; ugdt 167518848).
- **Giver:** Sunflower is named in the intro, so the giver is evidenced. The offer loc 403220 has no speaker name. Its "I'm in no shape to knock out any unruly bixies... sneak in and get some more honey" reads like the same person, so this is inferred.
- **Offer (loc 403220):** "The queen needs her daily calcium, but she's bored of plain milk. Maybe if we mix in some honey, she'll drink it! ... Hey! If you help me out, I might be able to sneak in and get some more honey for her. I'll be sure to make it worth your while."
- **Intro (Sunflower variant):** "Sunflower in Sanctuary wants you to defeat some of the rebellious bixies, so that he can gather some Royal Honey from the Bixie Hive." loc 403222, dat 1809597463 (line 47796).
- **Objective:** "Help Sunflower in Sanctuary get honey by knocking out unruly bixies in the Bixie Hive!" loc 403256, dat 2233070703 (line 58780). The sub-objective is "Knock out Unruly Bixie" (loc 403257). Count unknown.
- **Completion:** loc 403223 "Nicely done, [*player*]. I think the queen will be sure to drink her milk now! Here, you have some too..." (speaker not named). Return target not stated.
- **Steps:** (1) accept from Sunflower; (2) enter the Bixie Hive (Queensfields, see `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Bixie Hive.md` line 102); (3) knock out unruly bixies (count unknown); (4) return to Sunflower (inferred, not stated in these lines); (5) completion.
- **Prerequisites:** unknown (R). The Bixie Hive is open to all (Bixie Hive.md Requirements).
- **Rewards:** not evidenced for the Sunflower variant. The "20 stars and 42 coins" seen in the video belongs to Yuri's Royal Honey (title loc 390329, dat 2360985440; Bixie Hive.md line 102; sanctuary.json Yuri row), not to this text. (R) none.
- **Overlap:** Royal Honey (Yuri Petaldancer, loc 390329-390515 incl. "Recover Royal Honey from Fauzz in the Bixie Hive", loc 390492) is a separate quest. The Sanctuary guide Conflict 3 leaves open whether Sunflower's text is a separate or earlier version. This dossier treats it as a separate Sunflower quest.
- **Evidence:** `client:locale/en_us_data.dat:47796, 58780, 1698661714`; loc 403220-403223, 403256, 403257; `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Bixie Hive.md:102`; `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sanctuary.md:212`.
