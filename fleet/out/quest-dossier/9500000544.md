Completion text (probable, not confirmed by a key): locale tool id 390490, "Yay! I was beginning to think I was going to have to wait here until this whole bixie situation calmed down. Thanks!" It sits next to Yuri's journal line (390489). I could not match it to a hash key, so the attribution is inferred from adjacency only.
