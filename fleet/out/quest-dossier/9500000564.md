# Quests of Cakenstein and Cakeling (Seaside)

## Summary

The record (Cakenstein and Cakeling, npc_id 9500000564) gives no quests itself. `quests_given_text` only says "Event fight only; Diggy's Birthday Cake Bake-off and Cakey Clue belong to the same event". The sources do not support Cakenstein or Diggy as the giver of those two quests:

- **Birthday Cake Bake-off**: Camellia gives it (wiki Camellia page lists "Quest:Birthday Cake Bake-off"; `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sanctuary.md:132`; `sanctuary.json:11709`). The Seaside guide (`NPCs - Seaside.md:239`) says Diggy, and no locale text I read supports that.
- **Cakey Clue**: its intro is Mayor McFairy's (locale 428049). The Merry Vale guide says the same (`NPCs - Merry Vale.md:188`). The Seaside guide says Diggy (`NPCs - Seaside.md:238`).

Neither quest is built. `quests/*.json` has no match for "Cake", "Cakey", "Bake" or "Birthday".

Locale ids below are the `locale` tool's ids (for example 428048). The `ucdt` hashes in the guides (2930939421, 2440607895) are not tool ids.

## 1. Cakey Clue (title locale 428048)

**Giver.** Mayor McFairy, from the intro text (428049, "He has asked you…"). Sources disagree (see Summary).

**Prerequisite (chain start).** Mysteriously Cakey Remains (title 428044). Chef Jarvie gives it, per 428045 ("Chef Jarvie thinks Cakenstein's minions will leave behind a clue once they're defeated…"). Its Merry Vale title record is `merry_vale.json:3743`. Notes: `NPCs - Merry Vale.md:189`. The chain's earlier link is Undelivered Confections (title 428042; text 428041).

**Intro (offer), 428049:** "Mayor McFairy thinks the mysteriously meaty clue will help find the culprit behind all these cakey beasts. He has asked you to speak to some of the chefs participating in the cake bake-off."

**Steps, in order:**
1. Defeat cakelings in Merry Vale and bring back any clue. Locale 428043: "…Could you defeat some cakelings? Return immediately if you find any clues, [*player*]!" Cakeling is the enemy; the Cakenstein enemy record is `enemies.json:926`. The cakeling spawn id is not resolved.
2. Return the clue to Jarvie. Locale 428046: "Ah, you've found a clue… Mystery meat? Well, that sounds less than appetizing." The clue is mystery meat.
3. Visit Diggy first. Locale 428047: "…see if there's some kind of correlation between the mystery meat and the cakey monsters. Try to visit Diggy first." Speaker of the reply (428397, "By golly, is that mystery meat?… Go tell Mayor McFairy…") is not stated in the text.
4. Speak to the other bake-off chefs (objective 428506, "Mayor McFairy wants you to check up on the chefs participating in the big cake-off."). Some chefs refuse to give mystery meat: 428050 ("Bah! Why would I give you mystery meat?…"); 428052 gives a recipe and says "Oh don't worry about the mystery meat. I have PLENTY of that." Speakers are not named in the text.
5. Return to Mayor McFairy. Locale 428500 "Speak to Mayor McFairy"; 428502 "You've returned? Did you speak to Mayor McFairy yet? He'll want to hear about our clue right away!"

**Completion text.** Not found. 428502 is a pre-hand-in line.

**Rewards.** Unknown (R). No evidence found.

**Evidence:**
- Locale: 428043, 428044, 428045, 428046, 428047, 428048, 428049, 428050, 428052, 428397, 428500, 428502, 428506.
- Merry Vale guide: `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Merry Vale.md:188-189`.
- Seaside guide: `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:238`.
- `merry_vale.json:18415-18431` (Cakey Clue, page_lists).

## 2. Birthday Cake Bake-off (title locale 428081)

**Giver.** Camellia (Sanctuary, Birthday Bash). Evidence: wiki Camellia page (`refs freerealms-wiki/raw/df131c7af32b0f0c9d89e1456a76bcf148b22bb03adff6465dd3881084f08146.json`, pageid 8079, "Quest:Birthday Cake Bake-off"); `NPCs - Sanctuary.md:132`; `sanctuary.json:11709`.

**Prerequisite.** None evidenced. Level and job unknown (R).

**Intro (offer), 428080:** "The cake bake-off is the best part of the birthday party. Everyone is sponsoring a chef these days. All you have to do is pick a chef and then help them! You should speak to all the chefs and see which one you want to sponsor. What do you say?"

**Objective, 428082:** "Camellia wants you to speak with each of the chefs in the big birthday bake-off competition, so that you can figure out whom you'd like to sponsor."

**Steps, in order:**
1. Speak to Camellia and accept (428080).
2. Speak with each bake-off chef and choose one to sponsor (428082).
3. Help the chosen chef. Text 428083 ("They are all pretty awesome… Now, you just have to go help out whoever you choose… Good luck, [*player*]!") is probably the post-choice line; its speaker is not stated.
   The sponsor tasks are separate titles in the text, and I did not confirm they belong to this quest:
   - Glerga: "To Bake a Pretty Cake" (title 428053; objective 428054, "help her gather some ingredients").
   - Chef Jarvie: "Crispy Choychoy Delight" (intro 428084, objective 428086, completion 428087 "Nicely done! Those look delicious… Now back to baking cakes with me.").
   - Balloons: "Bundles of Balloons" (title 428077; objective 428076, "collect a blue, orange, and green balloon"); Quills' version is 428078/428079.
   - "Feeding the Crowd" (title 428089; text 428088, harvest Honeytwig and Bumbleberry).

**Completion text.** Not found.

**Rewards.** Unknown (R). Chef completion lines promise "something for your troubles" (428087) but name no item.
