**Steps (wiki Quest:Scaredy Pants, rev 28848, 2013-07-20; `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json:238`):**
1. Speak with Cutes Dozen (Seaside, Luxurious Oceanfront Homes; `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/seaside.json` NPC record, Cutes' zone entry). She gives the offer (389240) and pours the illusion on the player. The buff is "Cutes' Illusion", which lasts 30 seconds (wiki).
2. While under the illusion, speak with Slinky (npc_id 9500000626) before it expires. Objective 390979 "Scare the pants off of Slinky". He replies with the completion text (384097).
3. If the illusion expires, speak with Cutes again to get another dose (wiki; locale 390991). Her goal text (389288) says the same.
