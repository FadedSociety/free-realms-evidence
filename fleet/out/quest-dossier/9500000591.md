**Key correction:** the record's wording "Receives No Prey, No Pay from Admiral Fishfinder" is best read as Jolly Roger being the **hand-in/turn-in** NPC. The quest is offered by Admiral Fishfinder, and both Fishfinder records already list it as hers (`prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/briarwood.json:37-40`, npc 9500000083; `seaside.json:366-369`, npc 9500000547). Jolly Roger's own record has `quest_ids_given: []`. The guide row also reads "Admiral Fishfinder (hand-in Jolly Roger)" (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:264`). A builder should attach the giver to Fishfinder and the turn-in to Jolly Roger.

No quest in `quests/*.json` matches "No Prey" or 2548613727, so this quest is unbuilt.

The matching `ugdt` entry is `2303861083` (line 60628), a second table for the same string. The guide's "Text not matched" note (`NPCs - Briarwood.md:293`) is now resolved.
- **Giver:** Admiral Fishfinder, pixie pirate, Nettleseed water's edge southeast of the bridge to Tanglewood Fort (`briarwood.json:26-36`; wiki via guide). Her name is client string 1719553458 (ucdt). Two NPC records exist for her (9500000083 and 9500000547), and the builder must pick one.
- **Turn-in:** Jolly Roger (npc 9500000591, name 436629).

The text is in Fishfinder's voice and points the player to Jolly Roger. The speaker is not captured in the sources.
- **Objective line (probable):** locale **427881**: "Find Jolly Roger in Seaside and challenge a pirate to Pirate's Plunder!" Its placement right after the quest title is the only link. No source names it as this quest's objective.
- **Steps (in order, as evidenced):**
  1. Talk to Admiral Fishfinder in Nettleseed (Part H) and accept No Prey, No Pay (427880, 427879).

I'll turn ye in to shark bait in no time..." The player's reply is locale **427916**: "Yar, take me to Seaside." The guide says "after it she teleports the player to Seaside" (`NPCs - Seaside.md:172`). Speaker of 1276500393 is not captured; the record ascribes the teleport to the game.
  - No explicit kill/collect targets are evidenced. The step text only says "challenge a pirate to Pirate's Plunder" (427881), which is the minigame, not an item or enemy.
- **Prerequisites:** none evidenced. The quest sits in Part H (Nettleseed). Level, job, and earlier-quest prerequisites are **(R) unknown**.
