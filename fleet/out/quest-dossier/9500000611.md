3. Defeat Orchid Eventide (npc 9500000611). Her enemy/boss record id is not found in `enemies.json` (not searched). The wiki says the battle removes the pearls she uses to control the ghosts. The pearls are the item "Orchid Eventide Gloam Pearls" (ucdt 1798150763 / ugdt 366198207, locale rows 47497 and 9740). Count and pickup mechanics are unknown.
4. After her defeat she is cleansed. Nekark is released (wiki 18601: "she loses control of the Nekark"). The next quest, Defeat the Kraken (title 2022662996), begins with Nekark.
