# Quests of Sizzlebottom (Seaside)

## Summary

Sizzlebottom (npc_id 9500000625, Legacy NPC Id 1754, NameId 101556) gives two sticker-for-firework trades, not multi-step errands. The client has a title, offer, objective and completion text for each, so I dossier them as two short "quests". Neither is in `quests/*.json` (searched for Sizzlebottom, 101556, 400763/400768, the hash ids, and Pinwheel/Sticker; no hits). No server quest ids, levels, or reward tables are evidenced anywhere I checked.

- **Description:** locale **400744**: "Trade in Seaside Stickers for a gold pinwheel. Where else are you going to get fireworks so easily?"
- **Objective 1:** locale **400764** "Obtain 10 Seaside Stickers".
- **Objective 2 (likely, unconfirmed):** locale **400766** "Talk to Sizzlebottom".
- **Confirm prompt before the trade:** locale **400768** "That's ten Seaside Stickers for a gold pinwheel... No changing your mind, and no refunds! Ready to trade?" (hash **3909291392**, also cited in T317.md:164).

**Steps**
1. Obtain 10 Seaside Stickers (item **60255**, "Seaside Sticker", NameId 399529; sources below).
2. Talk to Sizzlebottom (NPC 1754 / npc_id 9500000625).
3. Accept the trade (400768). Give 10 Seaside Stickers, receive 1 Gold Pinwheel Firework.

**Reward (item):** Gold Pinwheel Firework. NameId 395520 (text "Gold Pinwheel Firework"), DescriptionId 395527. Two entries share that name:

- Id **16033** at the same file, line 292360.

Which one the trade grants is unresolved.

**Prerequisites:** none evidenced. Level, job and earlier quests are unknown. The event is the "Seaside Summer Splash" (NPCs - Seaside.md:201), and its run dates are not known.

**Evidence:** `repo: npcs/seaside.json:38571-38625` (NPC record); `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:49`; `notes/Autopilot/T317.md:164`; locale ids above; `refs: FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Scripts/Zone/FabledRealms.lua:516` (spawn of 1754 at -474.2243, -1.513627, -1167.455, which matches the record position); `FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Resources/Npcs.json` Id 1754, NameId 101556.
