**Steps, in order:**
1. **Accept:** speak with Slinky at Luxurious Oceanfront Homes (Seaside). Source: wiki "To Start" and Steps 1 (wikitext :7, :12). Accepting this dialogue starts the quest. Locale 389236.
2. **Get the illusion:** Slinky turns the player into a monster with "Slinky's Illusion" (locale **390994**; the guide calls it an "Illusion potion" in Services, seaside.json:39114-39116). The illusion lasts **30 seconds** (wiki only, wikitext :15).
3. **Scare Cutes Dozen (name string 45787; NPC id not captured):** speak with her at Luxurious Oceanfront Homes while the illusion is active. Use of a pet with a speed boost is suggested (wikitext :17, a tip only).
   - If the illusion expires first, speak with Slinky again to get a new one ("Return to Slinky", 390975; wikitext :16).
4. **Finish:** Cutes' reaction (389239), then the reward.
