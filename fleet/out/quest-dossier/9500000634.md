### 2. Not the Ghosts You're Looking For

**Title:** "Not the Ghosts You're Looking For", locale 5100941. Dat keys: 3282489757 (ucdt), 395688645 (ugdt). Record title 3282489757 matches.

**Offer / intro text:**

**Objective line:**
- 5100942 (no dat key retrieved): "The fighting style of the ghost ninjas did not match what was taught in the Flying Dragon Dojo. Kai wants you to speak to the Great Koodini to learn more about what the mysterious specters may actually be."

**Steps (in order):**
1. Prerequisite: Ghosts of the Past (Kai). Its intro is 5100936, title 5100937 ("Ghosts of the Past"), objective 5100938 ("Defeat some of them in battle..."), completion 5100939 (dat 1559955100: "They were using Deadly Blow!..."). Defeat ghost ninjas.
