# Quests of Tikia (Seaside)

## Summary

Tikia (npc 9500000635, name_string_id 93556 = "Tikia", Seaside, "Birthday host") is tied to two unbuilt balloon quests. Neither exists in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json`; a repo-wide search for "balloon", the four ids and "Party Ball" found only the NPC records and guide rows. The guide (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:79`) lumps both under Tikia with "Title not found". The locale text shows they are two different quests with different speakers, so I split them.

**Id caveat.** The ids in the record (820298032, 996472849, 2893565907, 1259248118) are hash keys in the `en_us_data.dat` files. The `locale` tool does not accept them ("no such id"). It indexes a different numbering, where the same texts sit at 390370–390378 and 384176–384203. Each text below gives both ids.

  1. Speak to Tikia, south of the Seaside start, at her birthday party.

- **Items:** the collectible is probably the item named "Party Balloon". Two ugdt entries exist: 901601519 (`en_us_data.dat:23885`) and 1202377185 (`:31842`). Neither is bound to this quest, and no item id is resolved. Unknown which one is the collectible.

- **Rewards:** none evidenced (R). The Birthday Bash wiki and guide list no reward for Tikia.

Evidence: `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:79`; `seaside.json:43601`; `opening-anvil-placement-v1/locale/en_us_data.dat` lines 21486, 21646, 76325 (hash keys, verified by text); locale 390370–390378.
