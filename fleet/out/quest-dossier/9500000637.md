## Not a Tozer quest: Merry Vale ambassador target (3536021443)

- The client string keyed 3536021443 (dat line 93613; locale 41739) is the intro for a **Seaside leg of Luella's ambassador chain**: "Are you up for a trip to Seaside? I need an ambassador... deliver personal messages to ... Tozer, Muggy Wuggle, and Poco Pogo." Tozer is one of three targets.
- The quest is given by **Luella** in Merry Vale (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Merry Vale.md` line 184; `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/merry_vale.json` lines 16333-16334).
- The Seaside leg is **cut**: its title is locale 41651 "NOT LIVE - Ambassador to Seaside" (the cut twin is 197025053 per the Merry Vale guide). Muggy Wuggle and Poco Pogo are the other targets (locale 73771: "Have you talked to Tozer, Muggy Wuggle, and Poco Pogo in Seaside yet?").
- Tozer's own speech for this leg is locale 73750 "Speak with Tozer about taking a vacation in Merry Vale." and 73755 "Speak with Tozer about traveling to Merry Vale." These are objective lines for a leg that was not live.
- Conclusion: do not add this to Tozer's quest list. Its giver is Luella, and it is not live.
