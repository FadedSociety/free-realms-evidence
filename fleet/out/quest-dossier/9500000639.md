# Quests of Troy Ridewrangler (Seaside)

# Troy Ridewrangler (npc 9500000639, Seaside, name string 440997): unbuilt quest dossier

**Scope.** Troy gives the 5-quest "Glide Training" series. None of the five is built: `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/` has no Glide or Troy entry (a search for "Glide", "Troy", "Ridewrangler" and the title hashes returned nothing in that folder). The four most evidenced quests are written up below (cap of 4). "Glide Training: License to Glide" is the fifth and is listed in Gaps with its ids.

**Id note.** The record's title hashes (487749906 etc.) are the keys in the shipped `en_us_data.dat` (e.g. `refs:opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:12969  487749906  ucdt  Glide Training: Getting Started`). The `locale` tool reports the same strings under different ids (4419680 etc.). Both are given below. The locale ids follow a pattern: for quest n the title, intro and completion sit at 4 consecutive ids, and the step-dialogue and objective strings sit next to them.

**Shared facts for the series** (all quests):
- Giver: Troy Ridewrangler, "near the Seaside Warpstone" (wiki Goals/To Start). Client line 441985 says "outside of the ride stable in Seaside"; 4419712 says "near the ride corral in Seaside". Name string 440997 ("Troy Ridewrangler"). Repo NPC record: npc_id 9500000639. The Seaside guide lists a different name id (1290590531, `repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:60`); unresolved.
- Series is five quests, each one day after the previous completion (wiki `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9696-45171-8406a8a2fef6.wiki`; `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9633-27858-9e2de906e051.wiki` line 5).
- Each step: speak to Troy; ride the untrained glide-trainable ride through the fiery hoop above a launch pad; return to Troy. The hoop objective is "Bounce through the fiery hoop above the launch pad ... while on your untrained glide-trainable ride" (goals text), so the ride is a required target, not an item.
- Requirement wording: "Player must own a usable glide-trainable ride" (wiki). The client's ride-side text: 441987 "This ride is able to learn how to glide, but is currently untrained. Speak with Troy Ridewrangler in Seaside to learn more!"
- Series reward: Proof of Training parts 1 to 5 (tickets, one per quest), then the Training Saddle of Gliding and Glide Training Certification (the latter only on the first completion of License to Glide; `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9696-45171-8406a8a2fef6.wiki` line 1). Wiki also: "The ticket has no effect and is taken away upon Glide Training completion" (`refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9635-27790-603c03242c2a.wiki` line 2).
- Level, job gating (except the Freestyle Jobs requirement on the ticket) and XP/coin rewards: unknown.

## 1. Glide Training: Getting Started

- **Title:** "Glide Training: Getting Started", locale 4419680 (ucdt key 487749906).
- **Locale ids:** title 4419680; intro (offer) 4419681; step-1 dialogue 4419679; objective 4419704 "Bounce through the fiery hoop above the launch pad outside of Sanctuary while on your untrained glide-trainable ride."; sub-objective 4419705 "Bounce through the Sanctuary hoop"; return 4419711 "Return to Troy Ridewrangler"; completion 4419682.
- **Intro (4419681):** "Troy Ridewrangler in Seaside has asked me to use a launch pad to launch myself and my untrained glide-trainable ride through a fiery hoop in Sanctuary."
- **Step-1 dialogue (4419679):** "The first step in training a ride is to ensure that the ride understands what it is that you want to teach.<BR><BR>Go to the launch pads outside of Sanctuary and jump through the fiery hoop above while riding your untrained glide-trainable ride."
- **Completion (4419682):** "Good job! Your ride's understanding of your commands has been transferred to the Training Saddle!<BR><BR>Training is hard work. Come back tomorrow and we can continue the training!"

**Steps**
1. Speak with Troy Ridewrangler (NPC 440997) near the Seaside Warpstone. Starts the quest (4419679 is the dialogue).
2. Ride the untrained glide-trainable ride onto the launch pad near the Sanctuary Warpstone, aim for the middle, and bounce through the fiery hoop. Target: 1 hoop, Sanctuary (4419705). The guide's Sanctuary row in the Seaside NPC list is not needed for this.
3. Speak with Troy Ridewrangler again. Completes the quest (4419682).

**Prerequisites:** must own a usable glide-trainable ride (wiki). First in the series, so no earlier quest. Level: unknown.

**Rewards:** Proof of Training - Part 1 of 5 (ticket; "Required: Freestyle Jobs"; cannot sell or trade). Evidence: wiki `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 70 ("first reward: Proof of Training- Part 1 of 5"); `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9625-27755-99b852f43b1e.wiki` line 2; ticket description locale 441977 ("Proof of completing step 1 of Troy Ridewrangler's glide training..."); `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9635-27790-603c03242c2a.wiki`. Item id for the ticket: unknown. Any other reward: (R) unknown.

**Next:** Glide Training: Basic Training (wiki `9625` line 3).

**Evidence:** wiki `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9625-27755-99b852f43b1e.wiki` (full steps); `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` lines 66-71; repo `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md` line 259; client locale ids above.
