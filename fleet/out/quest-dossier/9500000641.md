## Quest 1: Ninja: Restoring Honor (unbuilt; best evidenced)

- **Title:** "Ninja: Restoring Honor", locale row **78917** (ucdt 2039669218 / ugdt 2547376537; dat lines 53720 and 67113 in `locale/en_us_data.dat`).
- **Giver:** Ty (inferred, see below). Turn-in or next stop: Master Kai, Seaside (name 447517828; `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md` row for Kai).
- **Intro / objective text (Ty's direction):** row **78918** [hash 4126007044]: "Ty directed you to seek out his own master, Kai, the founder of the Flying Dragon Dojo and let him know what has transpired."
- **Probable variant objective:** row 91532: "Seek out master Kai, the founder of the Flying Dragon Clan and let him know what has transpired." Row 91531 is the short objective "Speak to Kai". I cannot tie either to this quest with certainty; they sit far from row 78917.
- **Completion text:** row 78919: "Your name travels on the wind like a leaf in the breeze, [*player*]! Now that Ty has found his way again, I have hope for rebuilding the dojo and taking the fight to Mari Duskblade, the leader of the Shadow Talon Clan. I will be glad to have you with us when that day comes." The speaker is not named. It reads like Kai's closing line, but this is unverified.
- **Other candidate closing lines (unassigned):** row 78915: "Together, we are a force unstoppable. My honor has been restored today." Row 93977: "Honor has been restored to the Flying Dragon Clan, thanks to the efforts of [*player*]."
- **Steps (evidenced order):**
  1. Speak to Ty (Ninja trainer). Ty directs the player to Master Kai (78918).
  2. Speak to Master Kai in Seaside (91531, 91532, 78919).
  3. Quest ends. Reward: Molten Dragon Blade.
- **Prerequisites:**
  - Level 20 (R). Evidence: row 93743 "You must complete your training! Ty will need our help, but you can't assist him properly until you have attained level 20." (hash 3740575301, cited in `notes/Build Guides/Content/Jobs/Ninja.md` row F6). The weapon is also level 20 (wiki).
  - Ninja job active (R), as for the other Ninja line quests.
  - Earlier quest: unknown (R). The place of Restoring Honor in the Ty chain is not stated in any source.
- **Rewards:**
  - **Molten Dragon Blade**, level 20 Ninja sword, Power 5, cannot be traded. Evidence: `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/16996-44228-36b8682ee3ff.wiki` line 4: "acquired by completion of the quest Ninja: Restoring Honor". Item definition id **48322** from `refs:Sanctuary/src/Resources/ClientItemDefinitions.json:992522-992526` (community reference, not checked against the client's own item table).
  - Coins, XP and other items: unknown (R, not evidenced).
- **Giver evidence:** Ty is the giver only by inference. The text at 78918 is Ty's direction, and the Ninja job page says "Ty is the giver" of several titles, but Restoring Honor is not on Ty's quest list in `lakeshore.json:15086` or in the wiki Ty page (`refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/3177-49344-d4b5aba6be42.wiki`). The Seaside record `seaside.json:46272` claims it, but that is the same text quoted back.
