# Quests of Adrian Treebreeze (Shrouded Glade)

# Adrian Treebreeze / Branchlord (npc 9500000647, Shrouded Glade): Power Stone quests

Scope: the four Power Stone quests this NPC hands out. None is built yet (no `giver_npc_id` 9500000647 and no title id from these quests in `quests/*.json`). The record's `quests_given_text` strings are step or objective lines, not quest titles. Titles come from the locale ids below.

**Locale ids.** The locale tool takes two id systems. Its small numeric ids (e.g. 437833) are the tool's own index. The 9–10 digit ids (e.g. 2896304119) are the `ucdt`/`ugdt` hashes used by the game and the guides. Both are cited where known. Every hash below was found in `refs:opening-*/locale/en_us_data.dat` and matches the tool text.

**Pairing caveat.** The tool's ids sit in blocks, and the block order does not reliably show which text belongs to which quest. Offer, objective and completion pairings below are by wording unless marked otherwise. The guide (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:200`) lists the same five titles, but says its pairing is also "by wording".

Recommended build order (guide row 200 order, not verified by a server): Gloamy Little Critters, Bringing Our Brothers Home, That Tingling Feeling, A Test of Power. Purifying the Darkness is the arc title (see Gaps).
