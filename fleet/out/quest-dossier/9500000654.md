# Branchlord / Adrian Treebreeze: Power Stone quests (Shrouded Glade)

**Giver.** The record `npcs/shrouded_glade.json` (npc_id 9500000654, "Branchlord", name ucdt 524662094) has the same role as record 9500000647 "Adrian Treebreeze" (`npcs/shrouded_glade.json` lines 7-10, 31-32; guide `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:124`). The locale greeting at 437833 has Adrian introduce himself as "leader of the Druids of the Branch" and bind the Power Stone, so Adrian and the Branchlord are the same giver. This supports the record's "[inferred]" note. The locale name row for "Branchlord" is 815.

**Id note.** The four ids in the record (774932266, 911792609, 1511567341, 3412782135) are the client's ucdt string keys, found in the `en_us_data.dat` files under `refs:opening-*/locale/`. The `locale` tool uses its own row numbers (e.g. 437830), so the locale tool does not resolve the record's numbers directly. Both are given below.

**Built status.** No quest in `quests/*.json` covers these (searched "Power Stone", "Branchlord", "Gloam", "Tingling", "Gloamy" etc.: no matches).

**Quest order.** The wiki's Shrouded Gloam page (`refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9545-48900-ffebdcdd728a.wiki` lines 15-25) lists the druid arc as: Fortify Our Defenses, Essssspionage, Slithering Agents, The Taste of Truth, A Tree for the Ages, **Gloamy Little Critters, Bringing Our Brothers Home, That Tingling Feeling, A Test of Power, Purifying the Darkness**, Report to Sunspark. The four quests below are in that order. Each title row is followed by its intro, objective and completion rows in the locale table (the ordering is inferred from row adjacency).

## 1. Gloamy Little Critters (wolves)

- **Title:** "Gloamy Little Critters": locale 437751 (quest log) and 437829 (tracker). Client keys ucdt 3301582582 / 3402787771 (guide, `NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:200`).
- **Intro (offer):** 437833 "Greetings [*player*], I'm Adrian Treebreeze, leader of the Druids of the Branch / We must begin cleansing the glade! I will bind this Power Stone to you. Use it on Wolves in the glade and defeat them to seal the corruption in the stone."
- **Objectives:**
  - 437830 (key 911792609): "Use the power stone on Corrupted Wolves. Visit the Branchlord for a new Power Stone."
  - 437944: "Cleanse Corrupted Wolves" (tracker).
  - 437831 (key 3412782135): "Speak with the Branchlord".
  - 437752: "The Druids have cut off the Gloam reinforcements to Shrouded Glade. It's time to help the Druids regain their powers by cleansing the Gloamed creatures of Shrouded Glade. / Return to the Branchlord if you lose your Power Stone."

- **Steps:**
  1. Talk to Adrian Treebreeze / the Branchlord (NPC 9500000647 / 9500000654) and accept.
  2. Receive a bound Power Stone (item id unknown; see Gaps).
  3. Use the Power Stone on Corrupted Wolves in the glade to absorb their Gloam, which fills the stone. The enemy "Corrupted Wolf" is listed in the wiki (`9545-48900...wiki:76`). The kill count is unknown.
  4. If the stone is lost, return to the Branchlord for a replacement. Locale 437884 "Give Power Stone", 437886 "Misplaced your Power Stone? ... I can provide you with another if you require it.", 437887 "I need another Power Stone." (a generic service, not quest-specific).
  5. Return to the Branchlord to finish (completion 437753).
- **Prereq:** the previous druid-arc quest "A Tree for the Ages" (wiki order, `...wiki:20`). Level and job: unknown.
- **Rewards:** unknown. (R) not evidenced.
- **Evidence:** locale 437751, 437752, 437753, 437829, 437830, 437831, 437833, 437884-437887, 437944; wiki `9545-48900-ffebdcdd728a.wiki:21`; guide `NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:200`; record `npcs/shrouded_glade.json:44`.
