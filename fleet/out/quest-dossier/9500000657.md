# Quests of Captain Sunspark (Shrouded Glade)

# Captain Sunspark (npc_id 9500000657): quest dossier

**Build status.** No quest in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json` cites Sunspark or any of his titles. The only title matches in that folder are unrelated ("Pondblaster Payback", "Party Payback"). So none of his quests are built yet.

**Id key.** Two id spaces appear. The `locale` tool uses small ids (e.g. 438472). The client's `ucdt` hashes (e.g. 3259793364) come from the `en_us_data.dat` files in `refs`. Each section gives both where I could match them.

**Selection.** Four quests are written up: Backs Against the Wall, Trapped and Alone, Report to Sunspark, and This Glade is Ours!. Payback is left out (see Gaps).

## 1. Backs Against the Wall (druid arc, first choice)

- **Title:** "Backs Against the Wall". Locale 438472. `ucdt` 3259793364 (`refs` …/locale/en_us_data.dat line 86114). A `ugdt` 1852976794 also exists (dat line 48892).
- **Giver:** Captain Sunspark, at the Shrouded Glade warpstone. Wiki: "In front of the Warpstone in Shrouded Glade" (wiki Quest:Trapped and Alone, To Start; the same placement is used for the dwarf side).
- **Offer / detail text:** "If you want to help the druids of Shrouded Glade, Captain Sunspark has suggested that you talk to Headmaster Merk." Locale 438473.
  - Druid context: "The druids have been hit hard by this invasion. They were forced out of their homes and separated from their leader, Arch Druid Camellia. Now all they can do is try to defend themselves as best they can. If you want to help them the person you need to talk to is Headmaster Merk." Locale 438471.
- **Steps, in order:**
  1. Speak to Captain Sunspark. He gives the choice "Captain Sunspark in Shrouded Glade has sent you to help the druids." (locale 438498; `ucdt` 2551761769, dat line 67229). This is the druid side. The dwarf side is the other choice (Trapped and Alone).
  2. Objective "Talk to Headmaster Merk" (locale 438499). Merk is "down the path past the warpstone. He's right in front of the druid refuge cave." (locale 438514; `ucdt` 384305230, dat line 10185).
  3. Later druid quests run through Merk (Fortify Our Defenses and the rest; see the Shrouded Glade guide, Quest index). Their order is not part of this quest.
- **Objective lines:** 438498 ("…has sent you to help the druids."), 438499 ("Talk to Headmaster Merk").
- **Completion text:** not found. No locale line is tied to the quest's completion.
- **Prerequisites:** none found. The wiki says Defending the Glade (Queen Valerian; read the Welcome Screen story) comes first in the invasion, but it does not say it is required for this quest. Level: unknown.
- **Rewards:** none evidenced (R marks rewards only if a builder wants a guess; none is offered here).
- **Evidence:**
  - Locale: 438472, 438473, 438471, 438498, 438499, 438514.
  - `refs` …/locale/en_us_data.dat lines 86114 (title), 67229 (choice), 10185 (Merk location).
  - `refs` wiki: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9545-48900-ffebdcdd728a.wiki lines 11–12 ("Helping the Druids: Backs Against the Wall") and wiki Captain Sunspark page (10108-50063, line 12).

## 4. This Glade is Ours! (finale)

- **Giver:** Captain Sunspark, at the Shrouded Glade warpstone. Wiki: offered "only after you have completed both the Druid and Dwarf story arcs" (wiki Shrouded Gloam, Conclusion).
- **Offer text:** "Captain Sunspark is rallying the troops for an attack against the Gloam. Join the druids and dwarves in the final battle to take back Shrouded Glade!" Locale 438260 (`ucdt` 4069316701 per the guide).
- **Steps, in order:**
  1. Speak to Captain Sunspark. Objective "Join Captain Sunspark for the final attack against the Gloam in Shrouded Glade." Locale 438268 (`ucdt` 3171990650, dat line 83765).
  2. Speak with Captain Sunspark to learn when the attack starts. Locale 438270 (`ucdt` 68398882, dat line 1775 of the opening-harvesting locale).

- **Druid context lines (not tied to a step):** "We're ready for the final attack!… charge to the Athenaeum!" (locale 438258; `ucdt` 2657905705, dat line 69997). "Do you want to face General Drahk again in the final battle?" (`ucdt` 1046625125, dat line 27721).

## Gaps

- **Payback** (`ucdt` 479553884, 2318893923, 3083773695; locale 437142, 437953, 437956) is listed on Sunspark's card. The wiki lists him as an appearance, and the Shrouded Glade guide (line 201) files Payback under the dwarf arc, which Gus and Golmie give. I did not find a Sunspark-side line for it, so I left it out. The Golmie line "It's payback time, [*player*]!… Gloam Obelisks" (locale 437952) and "Now it's payback time!" (locale 438364) are the only texts found, and they do not name Sunspark.
- **Report to Sunspark: which side?** The client has one title (438627), but the dwarf side has its own "report to Captain Sunspark" lines (locale 438686 "We're almost ready to move out…", 438688, and Gus's 438698). It is unclear if the dwarf side uses the same quest.
- **Objective ids and hashes.** Several objective lines (438632, 438273, 438499, 438503) have locale ids but no matched `ucdt` hash. The 3618279993 hash for Sunspark's dwarf line and 3148258200 for the druid pre-step came from the guide and were not matched in the dat files.
- **Rewards.** Only Trapped and Alone has an evidenced reward (20 coins, wiki). The other three have none.
- **Levels and prerequisites.** None evidenced except the arc order.
- **Completion lines.** Not found for Backs Against the Wall, Report to Sunspark, or Trapped and Alone (the wiki gives no completion line). For This Glade is Ours! the Drahk chest text (438294) is a guess.
- **Other quests on the card, not covered.**
  - Visiting the Job Center (locale 5100584; 5100580 and the "Talk to Captain Sunspark" objectives 5100592/5100609/5100615, all `ucdt` 3440602433 for the completion) is a Sanctuary job-tour quest. Sunspark is the hand-in, not the giver. The Sanctuary guide covers it.
  - Seaside Report (locale 5101722; 5101402 "Report to Captain Sunspark") is a hand-in on the Seaside chapter. The giver is not Sunspark.
  - The Way of the Wilds (locale 5100111) and Troubling Waters (wiki only; client has "Troubled Waters", 2309051198) are on the Seaside chapter. The Seaside guide covers them.
