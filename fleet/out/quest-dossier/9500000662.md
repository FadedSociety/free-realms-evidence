# Quests of Corrupted Druids (Shrouded Glade)

## Verdict: no quests to build for this record

The record for **Corrupted Druids** (npc_id 9500000662) is an enemy-type row, not a quest giver. Its own fields say so: `roles` is `["quest_giver", "enemy"]`, `title_or_role` is "Enemy types of the invasion.", `quests_given_text` is "Quest targets.", and `quest_ids_given` is empty. The zone guide says the same thing in its row: `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:143` lists "Gloam Constructs, Corrupted Wolves, Corrupted Vipers, Corrupted Birds, Corrupted Druids, Elite Gloam Constructs | Enemy types of the invasion. | The corrupted area. | Quest targets. | Not given rows." So the "quest giver" role on this row is a mis-tag, and "Quest targets." is a note, not a quest.

## Where the Corrupted Druids quest actually comes from (pointer, not a dossier)

The quest that targets Corrupted Druids is a Power Stone step, handed out by the Druids of the Branch leader, **Adrian Treebreeze / Branchlord**. That NPC is a separate record, `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/shrouded_glade.json`, npc_id **9500000647** (lines 6-45). Its `quests_given_text` quotes the step as "...on Corrupted Druids" (1511567341). It has no built quest file yet: no file in `quests/` references 9500000647 or 1511567341. A builder who wants this quest should take it as a separate job for Adrian. I have not built it here, and I have not checked the chain's order or rewards beyond the points below.
