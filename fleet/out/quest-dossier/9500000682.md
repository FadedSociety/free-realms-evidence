## Quest 1: Scoring a Chess Table

**Title.** "Scoring a Chess Table": locale 71124, ucdt 1086900492 (`refs:opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat:28780`). The ugdt copy is 4116372044 (same file, line 108742).

**Giver.** Fedrick, Shrouded Glade. The guide's quest index lists "Old Gregory, Fedrick" as givers (`repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:232`). Old Gregory starts the chain through his well and sends the player on (`repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Durango Zoo.md:40`). Old Gregory's camp is "just north of Durango Zoo" (973743465, locale 386157 text).

**Prerequisites.** Not evidenced as a level or job. The chain begins with Old Gregory's well quest, which runs through Lorna: "Let Old Gregory know his 'talking well' is really a frustrated chess player named Lorna." (70836). Old Gregory's reply, "Maybe if I had a table in my camp she'd be willing to visit...", is locale 70831 (ucdt 160616205). That reply is the hand-off to this quest. The well quest itself has no matched title (guide, Durango Zoo row 40).

**Intro (offer).**
- Old Gregory, the hook: "Poor Old Gregory, he's so excited to meet Lorna... Maybe if Old Gregory's camp had a chess table, Lorna would be willing to come for a visit." (70833, ucdt 3975030787).
- Fedrick's offer: "So you want a chess table to place in Old Gregory's camp, huh? You can have one, sure. If you can prove you take the game seriously by getting a high score." (71117, ucdt 3016703429).
- Solace variant of the offer: "So you're interested in acquiring a chess table to place in Solace? Prove to me that you take this game seriously by getting a high score, and I will gladly bestow upon you your very own chess table." (71123, ucdt 1240439822). This version names Solace, not Old Gregory's camp. It is unresolved which one a player gets.

**Objective lines, in order.**
1. "Find a chess table in Shrouded Glade." (71114, ucdt 4154630724).
2. "Find Fedrick" (71115, ucdt not captured in the dumps); later "Speak to Fedrick" (71119, ucdt not captured).
3. "Convince Fedrick to give up one of his handcrafted chess tables!" (71130, ucdt 1618203983). This is the main objective.
4. Background, from the quest description: "The Druids in Sacred Grove are experts when it comes to Chess... It'll be tough trying to convince Fedrick to give one of the game tables up! Maybe if you prove to him how serious you are about Chess..." (71125, ucdt 2080925599).
5. Win a game: "Fedrick will set a chess table up in Old Gregory's camp if you can win a game a chess!" (71120, ucdt 1297674821). Tracker lines "Beat a game of Chess" (71128) and "Win a game of Chess." (71129). These are the minigame objectives.
6. Return to Fedrick: "Let Fedrick in Shrouded Glade know you won a game of chess!" (141050; ucdt 4246867039 per the guide, not found in the dump).
7. Hand-in to Old Gregory: "Return to Old Gregory at his camp just north of Durango Zoo and let him know of your success!" (386157, ucdt 973743465). The title for that step is "Return to Old Gregory" (385891, ucdt 2880660657).

**Steps in order (player).**
1. Get the request from Old Gregory (well chain, 160616205).
2. Find Fedrick in Shrouded Glade (71114, 71115, 71119).
3. Ask Fedrick for a table. He wants a high score, not a single win (71117, 71130).
4. Win a game of Chess. The minigame is type 6 (`repo:notes/Build Guides/Gameplay/Chess.md:23`). Which difficulty or mode counts is not evidenced. The one-player/AI rows are 9 of 12 (same file, line 23).
5. Report the win to Fedrick in Shrouded Glade (141050).
6. Fedrick gives the table, which is set up for Old Gregory's camp (4050188210 or 2614459416).
7. Report back to Old Gregory at his camp (386157; 2880660657).
