### What the record actually holds
- **Boss, not giver.** Drahk is the invasion boss ("The merciless General Drahk has invaded Shrouded Glade!", locale 3258784759). The guide lists him as "Open-world fight every 15 minutes" and as the finale boss (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md`, line 141). The wiki agrees: "General Drahk - *during the quest This Glade is Ours!*" (`refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9545-48900-ffebdcdd728a.wiki`, line 51).
- **Chest, not a quest.** "Only players that helped defeat General Drahk can use this chest." (locale 3344252398; also 749 in the locale). This is loot eligibility.
- **Illusion item, not a quest.** "Illusion: General Drahk - 3 minutes" (locale 639805013, ucdt). This is a consumable.
- **Defeat objective.** "Defeat General Drahk" (locale 438273) is an objective string. Its owning quest is not confirmed by a quest record here.
- **Drop-line mentions.** Other strings naming Drahk are cast or flavour lines: "Use the Gloam Shard to turn into General Drahk." (7032 / 1763671084), "Drahk's army cannot be stopped!" (438409 / 2315886703), "You did it! You defeated Drahk!..." (438294 / 685922349). None of these is an offer or hand-in.
