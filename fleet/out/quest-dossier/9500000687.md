# Gus Geargrinder (npc 9500000687, Shrouded Glade): dwarf-arc quests

**Built already?** No. `quests/*.json` has no file or entry for Proving Your Worth, Jail Break or No Bro Left Behind (searched titles, ids 2217357817, 1533049601, 197736516, 1266850435, 383497806, 3121392492; the only hits are in the NPC zone file and the guide).

**Speaker problem (read first).** Two of the strings that the record and guide assign to Gus are spoken about him in the third person, so they may come from Dorn. Locale 437344 (Proving Your Worth intro): "Gus has a lot on his mind, but we do need your help..." Locale 437331: "The Freewheelers, led by Gus Geargrinder, need your help. His brother, Dorn, needs you to recover specific parts..." Locale 437371: "Gus Geargrinder is giving you a chance to prove your worth; talk to his brother Dorn and do what he asks." Taken together, the giver of Proving Your Worth is more likely Dorn, with Gus named as the one who sends you to him. This is the main conflict with the record. The NPC zone file (`npcs/shrouded_glade.json` ~L19309) and the guide (`NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md` L201) both credit Gus. The guide also says the pairing of titles to strings is "by wording".

## 1. Proving Your Worth (the first dwarf quest)

- **Title:** locale 437330 "Proving Your Worth". Title hash `ucdt` 2217357817; `ugdt` 1839584936 (refs `opening-anvil-placement-v1/locale/en_us_data.dat` L58374 and L48532). The "NOT LIVE - Proving Your Worth" variant is 23547 (`ucdt` 2859169749), so ignore it.
- **Offer text:**
  - 437344: "Gus has a lot on his mind, but we do need your help. Consider this a chance to prove your worth - so don't blow it!<BR><BR>Bring back the parts on this list. A bunch of good parts were accidentally tossed into the junk piles around Shrouded Glade in our haste to set up camp."
  - 437331: "The Freewheelers, led by Gus Geargrinder, need your help. His brother, Dorn, needs you to recover specific parts from the scrap piles around Shrouded Glade."
- **Objective:** collect the parts on the list from the junk piles around Shrouded Glade (the list itself is not in the locale; `1266850435` is a title-table hash and returns "no such id").
- **Completion / reply:** 437332: "Hmm... not bad. You might be worth our time after all." Attribution is unclear. It is the likely completion line, but it is not tagged as one.
- **Steps (evidenced):**
  1. Talk to the giver (Gus or Dorn, see above) and accept the offer (437344, 437331).
  2. Collect the listed parts from the junk piles in Shrouded Glade. The guide cites the parts-list title ids 1266850435, 3704763293, 2493874798 (`NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md` L201). Those are title hashes, not resolved item ids.
  3. Return the parts. Hand-in NPC and reply text: 437332 (unattributed).
- **Prerequisites:** Trapped and Alone (`ucdt` 1498778877). The wiki says it is "no longer available" and that the next quest is Proving Your Worth (guide L199). Level: unknown.
- **Rewards:** (R) none evidenced. The guide says no source gives rewards for town quests except the 20 coins from Trapped and Alone (guide L277).
- **Item ids:** unknown. The parts are not resolved to item ids.

- **Item:** 437569 "Dwarftech Smoke Bomb"; 437574 "Smoke Bombs" (item-table name). Item id: unresolved. The `383497806` in the record is a title-table hash, not an item id.
- **Completion text:**
  - 437521: "The guys you just brought back saw the Druids forcing other Freewheelers into cages back toward their camp. Cages! Gus wants to talk to you about it. I'm sure he's got a plan." (This is the lead-in to Jail Break; it follows the rescue of the missing supply party.)
  - 437524: "You did good back there, [*player*]. Now I see why Dorn trusts you. With most of the gang back we can now gear up and find a way out of here." (Assigned to the rescue step, not to Jail Break, by position only.)

  4. Return to Gus Geargrinder (437533).
- **Prerequisites:** the rescue of the missing supply party (437811 "One of the supply parties didn't make it, find them."; 437812 "Find the missing supply party"; 437814 "Defeat Gloamed Dwarf"; 437815 "Defeat the 1st Gloamed Dwarf").
