## 3. Friend or Foe: The Tradesgoblin (Rumble Ridge, Sunstone Valley)

- **Completion (438890, speaker not in the locale; by wording it is the Robgoblin deal):** "I would normally not place my trust in Robgoblins, but the promise of so much junk is simply too much for any Robgoblin to pass up. Excellent job in securing their services!"

- **Rewards:** unknown. Friend or Foe: The Savage has a stated reward (Mini-Manguana, wiki `quest-pages.json:63`), but nothing is evidenced for this quest.
- **Evidence:** locale 438887-438890, 438948; wiki `quest-pages.json:63`; wikitext `16386...wiki:128-130`; guide `NPCs - Sunstone Valley.md:203`.
