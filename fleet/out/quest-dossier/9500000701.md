# Quests of Marius Shadetree (Shrouded Glade)

## Marius Shadetree (npc_id 9500000701): no quest to build

**Verdict:** Marius Shadetree does not give a quest. The `quest_giver` role in the record is a misclassification. The "quests_given_text" entry is a respawn schedule for a mini-boss battle, not a quest. No quest is evidenced for him, so there is no dossier entry to build.

### What the evidence shows

- **Role is a battle, not a quest.** The wiki Gloam page says: "The remaining battles, the mini-bosses, respawn 15 minutes after the last mini boss was beat. Only one mini boss appears at a time, and they are found in one of three locations: the stairs to the north wing of the academy, the stairs to the south wing or near the greenhouses across from the south wing entrance." It lists "Marius Shadetree" under the heading **Battles**, next to Gloamtooth, Brickface, the Druid/Dwarf Rally and General Drahk. (`refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/9545-48900-ffebdcdd728a.wiki:43-50`)
- **Guide row.** `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:142` puts Marius in the Gloam invasion cast as a mini-boss. Its "Quests given" column is the respawn text, and its "Gives" column is the chest and illusion. The record's `quests_given_text` copies that column. The record's `roles` field and its `title_or_role` text ("Mini-bosses ... Each drops a chest") are the same battle data.
- **Client strings are battle and loot strings.** Locale ids: 
  - 290346421 / 512754003 (ucdt/ugdt): "Marius Shadetree" (locale 438531). 
  - 438606, 438634, 438636: "Marius' Teleport 1/2/3". 
  - 438685: "Only players that helped defeat Marius Shadetree can use this chest." 
  - 438696: "Marius' Treasure Chest". 
  - 438809: "Illusion: Marius Shadetree - 3 minutes". 
  - 7031: "Use the Gloam Flower to turn into Marius Shadetree." 
  - 438800: "Gloam Flower". 
  
  None of these is a quest title, intro, objective or completion line. The lines are chest, illusion and transform items. The chest string 4262166742 cited in the record was not re-checked, and it is not needed for this verdict.
- **Peers match.** Gloamtooth (locale 438529, "Defeat"-style strings 438683 and 438807) and Brickface (438530, 438684, 438808) follow the same pattern. The only "Defeat the ..." quest-style line in this cluster is "Defeat the Gloam Branchlord" (437204), and that is for the Gloam Branchlord, not Marius.
- **Nothing built or cited for him.** A search of `repo` for "Marius", "Shadetree" and 9500000701 finds only the NPC record, the guide row and an unrelated Briarwood merchant Marius. `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/` has no quest that names him. The Briarwood merchant "Marius" (npc 2558, NameId 5102440) is a different NPC.
- **Quest-line context.** The wiki places the Gloam invasion battles inside the "This Glade is Ours!" line, but Marius is not named as a quest giver or quest target there. His only quest-adjacent text is the Gloam Flower transform line (7031).

### Gaps

- **Quest:** none evidenced. There is no offer, objective, completion, prerequisite or reward text for Marius, and no step list.
- **Reward:** the wiki says only that the mini-boss chest holds "dwarf/druid armor, a housing item, a consumable that grants an illusion, coins, or pets". It does not say which item Marius's chest gives, so any reward is unknown.
- **Suggested fix to the data:** the `roles` value `quest_giver` on record 9500000701 looks wrong. It should be a battle or mini-boss role, and `quests_given_text` should be moved out of the quest fields.
- **Not checked:** the chest string 4262166742 and the Gloam Flower mechanics beyond the locale line. Neither bears on whether a quest exists.
