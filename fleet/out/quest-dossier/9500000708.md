# Quests of Perci Perriweather (Shrouded Glade)

## Summary

Perci Perriweather (npc_id 9500000708) gives no quest that I can evidence under her own name. The only unbuilt quest tied to her is the **Potions for the Arch Druid** chain. The client's text shows **Ceard Bauchlog** as the one asking for the potion ("Ceard wants you to get a potion from Perci", "...for Ceard Bauchlog"). Perci is the shop where the potion is bought, and the place that redirects the player to Daphne when she is out of stock. The record's `quests_given_text` and `roles: quest_giver` overstate her role. Treat her as a vendor and redirect node in this chain, and record Ceard as the quest giver, unless a source shows Perci's own offer.

The Card Duelist stop it cites ("Visit Perci in Shrouded Glade", 1457307539; Misty Mountain Fizz, 1483953870 / 2518614605 / 3935063848) belongs to **Card Duelist: Cardie Party**, which Bry gives. That quest is already built (see Already built below).

The quest has no client title. The name "Potions for the Arch Druid" comes from the Shrouded Glade guide's quest index ("title not found"), not from the client.

3. **Deliver the potion to Ceard Bauchlog** in Shrouded Glade (385583, 385582, 385575). Target: Ceard. Evidence: locale ids above.

Note: the step-1 buy is described as a daily habit for Ceard (385489, 385492). Whether the quest is repeatable or one-shot is unknown.
