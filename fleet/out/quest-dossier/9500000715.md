# Quests of Suni Sorrowsmile (Shrouded Glade)

**Correction to the record.** Nothing in the community server data or our quests/*.json shows Suni as a quest giver. Looking for Lavender (3010) is Feldman's quest (legacy NPC Id 2419). Suni is only a target in its step 3011 (A Reliable Source).
