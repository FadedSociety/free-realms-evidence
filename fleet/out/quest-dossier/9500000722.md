# Quests of Venomlord Breinne Ultrafang (Shrouded Glade)

## Scope

Venomlord Breinne Ultrafang (npc_id 9500000722, Shrouded Glade, druid camp) gives two unbuilt quests in the Druid arc: **Essssspionage** and **Slithering Agents**. Neither title appears in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json`. A search for "Essssspionage", "Slithering", "Viper" and the title hashes found no hits there. The record's `quest_ids_given` is empty, so both are new.

Hash ids below are the `ucdt` hashes from `opening-journal-text-v2/locale/en_us_data.dat` (the same ids the zone guide uses). The `locale` tool takes its own row numbers (for example 437225–437266 for this cluster); I give those in brackets where useful, but the hash is the citable id.

## 1. Essssspionage (title ucdt 3836031845; second title entry ucdt 4031107830, same text)

**Giver:** Venomlord Breinne Ultrafang (npc 9500000722). The offer that starts the step chain is her own line. Merk sends the player to her first.

**Prerequisites (partly evidenced):**
- Druid arc start. Merk's pointer to her: "Headmaster Merk needs you to find evidence that the Dwarves are behind the invasion of Shrouded Glade. Speak to the Venomlord then investigate the Dwarven Camp." (754690363; locale row 437231).
- Also "Headmaster Merk believes the Dwarves are behind the invasion of Shrouded Glade, but he needs more proof. Speak with the Venomlord to find a way to sneak into the dwarven camp and search for clues." (3713767280; row 437226).
- The "Tainted Gear" hand-off: "This Tainted Gear you found makes me believe the Dwarves are behind the invasion, but I need more evidence before we can react. Speak with the Venomlord Breinne Ultrafang, leader of the Druids of the Viper, she can get you into the Dwarven camp undetected." (759655078 and 437224 are the same text, different name form). The gear comes from the Fortify Our Defenses chain (guide: ucdt 4201029798 "a mysterious tainted gear" after the last seed). That prerequisite is inferred from the guide (see Gaps).
- Level, job, and the exact prior quest: unknown (R).

**Intro (offer) text, from the Venomlord:**
- "My little serpents tell me that you'll be our agent to pin this invasion on those greasy Dwarves. Druids of the Viper are masters of stealth and deception. Use this Serpent Form to infiltrate the Dwarf camp and search their Dwarven Supply Crates." (ucdt 1669315856; a variant with the same wording is 2964662275; locale rows 437234 and 437235).

**Steps in order:**
1. **Speak with the Venomlord** (objective ucdt 2784320720 "Speak with the Venomlord"; 1675517751 is the later "Tell the Venomlord of your findings" objective). Accept the offer. She grants the spell **Viper Form**. The spell title is ucdt 117628643 "Viper Form" (also 1049046180 and 1931722419 ugdt). The instruction text is "You must be in Viper Form to use the Dwarven Supply Crate. Visit the Venomlord to gain this spell." (1524490423; row 437266 is the title "Viper Form"). The intro calls the form "Serpent Form"; the spell is called "Viper Form". Both names appear in the client.
2. **Search the Dwarven Supply Crates in the Dwarf Camp** while in Viper Form. Objective texts:
   - "Search the Dwarven Supply Crates in the Dwarf Camp to find evidence they are behind the invasion of shrouded glade." (3373266465; row 437233)
   - "Search the Dwarven Supply Crates for clues" (2384140142; row 437232)
   - "Search the Supply Crates located in the Dwarven camp." (1328830920; row 437229)
   - Object name: "Dwarven Supply Crate" (row 437236). Crate-loot item ids: not resolved.
   - If the player leaves the form, the reminder is: "Proof of the dwarves' betrayal can be found in the Dwarven Supply Crates in their camp. Do you need me to return you to Serpent Form?" (1327302600 and 3304077072; rows 437239 and 437240).
3. **Return to the Venomlord** and "Tell the Venomlord of your findings" (1675517751). Objective labels: "Return to the Venomlord" (1901035913; 413292354). Repeat turn-in reply: "Hisssss Not a shed of evidence!? Let me see what you found... All useless! Those gear headed dwarves couldn't hide evidence if they wanted to! We'll get the information we need." (115864179; row 437227). This line leads into Slithering Agents.

**Completion text:** 115864179 (above) is the most likely completion line. It is inferred from position and wording, not from a quest-end flag. No dedicated completion string is labelled as one.

**Rewards:** unknown (R). No reward text or item was found in the locale or the cached wiki.

## 2. Slithering Agents (title ucdt 3068509244; ugdt 954534258)

**Giver:** Venomlord Breinne Ultrafang (npc 9500000722). She sends the player to her Viper Agents.

**Prerequisites:** Essssspionage (the Venomlord's Viper Form and "Return to the Venomlord" chain). The reply line 115864179 leads into this step (inferred). Level, job: unknown (R).

**Intro (offer) texts:**
- "[*player*], I've managed to get my Viper Agents within observation range of the Dwarven Camp. You can find them inside the corrupted area of the glade. They haven't let me down yet. Go pay my wonderful serpents a visit and collect the information they've gathered for me." (ucdt 1250544886; row 33101 in dat).
- "My precious Viper Agents should have all the information we need to shut this invasion down. Find my agents in the corrupted area of Shrouded Glade, near the dwarven camp and find out what they have learned." (ucdt 2464824888; row 64910 in dat; a variant is 3304823505).
- Journal line: "The Venomlord still has no information to pin the attack on the dwarves. She has positioned Viper Agents around the Dwarf camp to gather intelligence. Visit the Viper Agents in Shrouded Glade to find more information." (ucdt 2743310240; row 72303).

**Steps in order:**
1. **Search for the Viper Agents in the corrupted area of Shrouded Glade** (ucdt 2197622544; dat row 57881). The objective "Speak with the Viper Agents" is 3787861087.
2. **Speak with the first Viper Agent** (ucdt 820917877), then **the second Viper Agent** (4257087591), then **the third Viper Agent** (499082192). The three agent NPC names are "Viper Agent" (ucdt 118047604, 350972542). The agent npc_ids are not resolved in the shipped record.
3. **Return to the Venomlord** (objective ucdt 1901035913 or 413292354). Her reaction: "*HISSSS* No information!? My agents haven't failed me before… and I'll make sure they don't again. How are these dwarves covering themselves so well? We'll get the information we need, believe me, even if it means turning to extreme measures." (ucdt 2106602330; locale row 437244). The next beat is "No new information… The Venomlord won't stand for this… What's that!? Defend yourself!" (ucdt 920020833). That is the Gloam attack that the Tree for the Ages chain starts from. Whether this line is the completion of Slithering Agents is inferred, not evidenced.

**Completion text:** 2106602330 is the candidate (inferred). The guide has no explicit completion line for this quest.

**Rewards:** unknown (R).

**Evidence:**
- `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Shrouded Glade.md:200` ("Slithering Agents (3068509244): visit the Viper Agents in the corrupted area (1250544886, 2464824888, 2743310240, 2197622544)").
- `repo: shrouded_glade.json:34560` (the record's "sends the player to her Viper Agents (1250544886, 2464824888, 2743310240)").
