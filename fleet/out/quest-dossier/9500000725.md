**Giver:** Annabelle, npc 9500000725, Snowhill (`npcs/snowhill.json` ~line 427-470). Repeatable once a day after the Snow Days series.

4. **Completion text** (probable end line, not confirmed as the quest-end node): ucdt 1991914829 = locale 421072: "You found the gifts! I promise, I'll keep a close eye on my present pile from now on."
