# Quests of Downey (Snowhill)

## Quest 1: Curious Gift Baskets (Downey, Snowhill)

Only one quest is evidenced as given by Downey. It is not built in `quests/*.json` (no title or id match there).

**Title:** "Curious Gift Baskets", locale **421001**. The hash key 275173309 in the guide and the journal dump is not a locale id.

**Recipe hint, locale 421052:** "If you don't know how to make Burnetta's Curious Creation, you can buy the recipe from Mae Farnum in Farnum Farmstead."

**Completion text:** not isolated. Locale 421003 ("Mmm, these smell delicious!<BR><BR>I'd better pack them in the gift baskets before I decide to eat them instead.") is probably a cooking or turn-in line, but its speaker and trigger are unknown.

**Steps (in order):**
1. Talk to Downey at her house on Snowdrip Lane, Snowhill. Address: locale 3653979331 ("...You can find her house on Snowdrip Lane."); guide row NPCs - Snowhill.md:64 and :196.
2. Get Burnetta's Curious Creation. Either buy the recipe (item 848, "Burnetta's Curious Creation Recipe") from Mae Farnum (Farnum Farmstead; 30334094, 2373329029) or Bordon Cloo (Sanctuary; 829103903), or learn it through the recipe in the vendor records. Then cook it. The recipe is chef recipe_id 38, job profile 45, required rank 1, output item definition **21812**. Ingredients: definition ids **2859 x3, 2885 x3, 2890 x3, 2902 x3** (`recipes.json:124-155`). Chef.md:216 names them Mystery Meat, Stickfruit, Tumtumnip and Butter. I did not re-check those names against the client Resources, so that mapping is unverified.
3. Return to Downey and hand over the cooked dish(es). The number required is not evidenced. Locale 421000 says "a few" and the guide says "treats"; the count is (R) unknown.
4. Repeatable: once a day, after the Snow Days series (wiki).

**Prerequisites:** completion of the Snow Days quest series from Candi Ivy (wiki `Snow Days`: "These repeatable quests only become available after completing the quest series: ... Curious Gift Baskets from Downey (repeatable once per day)"). Chef rank 1 for the recipe. Player level is unknown.

**Rewards:** none evidenced. (R) unknown.
