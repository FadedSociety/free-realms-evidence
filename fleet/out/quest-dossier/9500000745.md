### Snowman Showdown (continuously repeatable, Snow Days event)

**Title.** "Snowman Showdown". Locale ucdt 497898902 (the locale tool's index shows it as 402638). The ugdt copy is 2041049229.

**Offer (intro) text.** Locale 420811 (ucdt id not in the tool's index; the tool's index is 420811):
> "Our <font color='#D9781A'>coal</font> supply has been a bit short lately. Someone's been stealing it to craft snowmen.<BR><BR>I know of one way to <font color='#D9781A'>get it back</font>! You've heard the saying fight fire with fire? Well, snowballs are the best way to <font color='#D9781A'>take down snowmen</font>!<BR><BR>Do you have good aim?"

Locale 420810 reads "Coal Collector". It is probably the NPC's dialog or role label; the tool does not say which.

**Objective / description.** Locale 420813:
> "Be on the lookout for snowmen running amuck in Snowhill! When they appear, be sure to use your special snowball ability to knock them over and collect coal."

Locale 420816 (hint): "Pelt snowmen with snowballs to knock them over and collect their coal."

**Objective titles.** "Collect Coal" (420817, step 1) and "Give the coal to Everett" (420819, step 2). The tracker line is 420820: "Take the coal back to Everett in Snowhill."

**Turn-in not yet satisfied.** Locale 420821:
> "You'll need to collect some more coal before I can reward you.<BR><BR>Throw snowballs at snowmen around Snowhill to knock them down and snag their coal."

**Completion text.** Locale 420814:
> "Great work out there!<BR><BR>Recovering all that coal won't go unrewarded."

**Steps, in order.**
1. Accept from Everett in Snowhill (the offer above).
2. Hit snowmen around Snowhill with a snowball (the player's special snowball ability) until they fall. Each knocked-down snowman drops coal. The item is Snowman Coal, item id **70454** (Resources ClientItemDefinitions, NameId 421075, DescriptionId 421076 "This piece of coal was left behind by a toppled snowman."). The number of coal needed per turn-in is unknown.
3. Return to Everett and hand in the Snowman Coal. The client shows the 420821 retry line if the count is short.
4. Receive the completion text (420814). The quest is repeatable.

**Target snowman id.** Not resolved. Candidate client names are "Snowman" (ucdt 653471170) and "Evil Snowman" (ucdt 501690674). Nothing in the evidence ties the Snowman Showdown snowmen to either one.

**Prerequisites.** Unknown. The guide places it in the Snow Days town and event quests (`NPCs - Snowhill.md:195`), so it is tied to the Snow Days event period. No level, job or earlier-quest gate is evidenced.
