## 1. Misplaced Meeps (title id 527088448)

**Title:** "Misplaced Meeps" (locale 5102406; the ucdt title id is 527088448, per the Snowhill guide and locale data).
**Summary / detail line:** "Locate Momma Meepster's lost baby Meeps." (locale 5102407)
**Limited-time:** 8-Bit Month, September 2013 (wiki quest page revision timestamp 2013-09-15; the Meep page says the Meep was introduced 10 Sep 2013 and became unobtainable 8 Oct 2013).

> "My baby Meeps are gone! They wandered off to explore the world even though I said not to! Oh, woe is me!<br><br>Wait, can you help me find them? They're much smaller than me, but we all have the same beautiful edges and shapes!"

**Objective lines:**

- 5102410: "Meeps found" (progress line, counted out of 6 per the wiki: "Meeps found - /6")

**Completion text (locale 5102409):**
> "Oh thank you! Thank you! My babies are safe and ready to be adopted!<br><br>Could you find it in your heart to give a few poor baby Meeps a good home?"

The completion line is the hand-in text. The adoption offer is not a separate quest step.

**Steps, in order:**
1. Speak with Momma Meepster, located in Snowhill near Big J's. This starts the quest and gives the intro text (5102408). Evidence: quest-pages.json "To Start".
2. Find Momma Meepster's lost Meeps, which are scattered through Sacred Grove. The wiki says 6 are needed (progress "Meeps found - /6"). The "Lost Meep" is a sparkling baby Meep NPC, present only during 8-Bit Month. The wiki page's "Possible locations include:" list is empty in the cache, so spawn points are unknown.
3. Return to Momma Meepster and hand in (completion text 5102409).

Target ids: the Lost Meep NPC id is unknown. The map or zone id for Sacred Grove is not resolved here. The count is 6.
