# Quests of Pumpkin Prince (Stillwater Crossing)

## Summary

The Pumpkin Prince (npc 9500000794) gives no quest. He is the Super Spooktacular roaming boss. The quest his event ties to is **The Great Scare**, which is offered by the **Ghost Hunter** (name id 3947454227; Pixiewood Hills, Sanctuary), not by the Pumpkin Prince. It is unbuilt: `quests/*.json` has no Great Scare entry, and no file cites title id 416778. The string in the record, "Foil the Pumpkin Prince's plans!" (3335381554), is not tied to a giver. Its locale id is 432142, and the only quest that uses it is the Great Scare chain, by wording. Nothing else in the record is evidenced as a quest.

- **Steps, in order (inferred from the locale, no full dialogue found):**
  1. Talk to a Ghost Hunter (objective "Talk to a Ghost Hunter", 416789; Ghost Hunter is the giver).
  2. Accept the offer (432125). Fight the Pumpkin Prince when he spawns. He appears at random in Snowhill, Bristlewood or Stillwater Crossing (416782: "...He'll show up either at Snowhill, Bristlewood, or Stillwater Crossing."; 416783: "...He randomly shows up in Snowhill, Bristlewood, or Stillwater Crossing."; town warpstone lines 416809-416811).
  3. In the open-world fight, use the "Scare" ability on his minions (416783: "scare his minions off with your 'Scare' ability"). Click the freshly spawned graves that appear (416786: "...you'll find freshly spawned graves. Click on one, and help scare the ghosts and zombies away."; 431558 says the same). The prince summons ghouls and zombies (416738). Ectoplasmic destabilizer dispensers appear on the paths (wiki: `refs: freerealms-wiki/raw/d521996be54dd453e50183ed124ce6b2b1700494c90cd7db5dec70312c3e2e65.json`, "Open World" section).
  4. Defeat him (objective 416121). The wiki says the chest drops once he is defeated (wiki, Vs. Pumpkin Prince section, `3113-...wiki:74`).
  5. Return to The Ghost Hunter (432146, "Return to The Ghost Hunter").
