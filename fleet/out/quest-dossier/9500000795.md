# Quests of Two of Three (Stillwater Crossing)

## Two of Three (npc_id 9500000795, Stillwater Crossing): no quest dossier

### What the sources do say

- **Identity and placement.** Wandering enemy, added 8 October 2013 for the Super Spooktacular. Spawns north of the Cursed Graveyard in Stillwater Crossing and north of the spiralmint patch in Highroad Junction.
  - Wiki: `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/11490-50194-1d9ee3146e4e.wiki` lines 7 and 10 ("introduced in-game on October 8, 2013").
  - Guide: `repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Stillwater Crossing.md:91`, which says "Not seen in video" and "Wiki is the only place source."
  - Name ids: locale 5102447 "Two of Three". Hash ids (repo only): ucdt 1736228564, ugdt 2882839841.
- **The only quest-like text.** Client objective "Find and Defeat Two of Three".
  - Locale 5102459 = "Find and Defeat Two of Three". Repo ref: `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/npcs/stillwater_crossing.json:11455` and `highroad_junction.json:9328`, cited as "(322643404)".
  - 322643404 is the hash form. The `locale` tool returns "no such id" for it, so 5102459 is the id to use.
  - Sibling strings: 5102458 "Find and Defeat One of Three" (Cobblestone Village NPC, see `cobblestone_village.json:6794`) and 5102461 "Find and Defeat Three of Three".
  - Neighbouring ids 5102460 and 5102462 are empty. No intro (offer) or completion line sits next to 5102459, so no quest dialogue was found.
- **The achievement family.** The three "Find and Defeat X of Three" objectives sit next to the Super Spooktacular achievements.
  - "One Down": "Defeat One, Two or Three of Three", name 5102456, description 5102457, Silver Trophy, 10 points. Source: `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/achievements.json:7725-7748`. Wiki: `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/3792-48170-a7b0483ad483.wiki` lines 649-656.
  - "Three Less": "Defeat One, Two and Three of Three", Platinum Trophy, 25 points, plus Pumpkin Carver.
  - The wiki row for One Down links "[[Two of Three|Two]]", so the wiki treats Two of Three as one of the three targets. The wiki does not say these enemies give anything.
