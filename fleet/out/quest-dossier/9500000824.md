# Quests of Honeydew (Sunstone Valley)

## Honeydew (npc 9500000824, Sunstone Valley): unbuilt quests

None of the three quests is in `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json` (that folder has only adventurer, archer, blacksmith, brawler, card_duelist, chef, demo_derby_driver, fisherman, kart_driver, medic, miner, ninja, pet_trainer, postman, scavenger_hunt, soccer_star, warrior, wizard). The NPC file already lists all three with `quest_id: null` (`repo:…/npcs/sunstone_valley.json:10897-10946`). Warm Welcome (Peppermint's quest, which sends the player to Honeydew) is the lead-in and is not Honeydew's to build.

Build order: Helping Honeydew, then Meeting the Princess, then Distracting Druids. Only the first has a wiki page with text.

### 1. Helping Honeydew

- **Title:** locale 413084 "Helping Honeydew". Title key ugdt 1500599317 (locale line 39774 in the test `en_us_data.dat`). The record and guide give the ucdt title key as 4087056571; I did not see that key in the locale output, so treat it as unverified.
- **Prerequisite:** Warm Welcome (wiki "previous: Warm Welcome"). Level and job: unknown.
- **Giver:** Honeydew (npc 9500000824; name string 413059). Location: near Skysong Ledge, north-west of Peppermint (locale 2697546481: "Meet Honeydew near Skysong Ledge, to the north west of Peppermint.").
- **Intro / offer:**
  - ucdt 1905476990 (detail summary): "The baby horses in Skysong Glade are running around all over the place! Honeydew has asked you to help her bring them back to the nursery as quickly as possible."
  - ucdt 819268604 (offer summary): "Honeydew needs help getting the baby horses near Skysong Ledge back to the nursery. They're playing hide-n-seek, so every baby horse you find will go straight back to the nursery."
  - Wiki upon first contact (quoted in the wiki, not in locale ids): "Those little horses just won't sit still! Oh I wish I could get them back to the nursery... Wait a minute, you seem pretty quick! Want to help me? ... all you need to do is find them and they'll go right back to the nursery on their own."
- **Objective:** ucdt 2109799825: "Help Honeydew find the baby horses hiding near Skysong Ledge."
- **Steps:**
  1. Talk to Honeydew near Skysong Ledge (2697546481; the guide also cites 128326071 "Find Honeydew near Skysong Ledge…" and 1809172557 "Find Honeydew").
  2. Accept. Find the hidden foals around Skysong Ledge and Skysong Glade. Each one found goes back to the nursery on its own. The wiki says each is "located in various spots around the immediate area" and that interacting with one updates the goal. The number of foals is unknown.
  3. Return to Honeydew once all are found. ucdt 3311262555: "You should let Honeydew know that all the baby horses have gone back to the nursery."
- **Completion text:** wiki only, not located by locale id: "Oh thank you! I'm sure I would have been looking for those little horses all day if not for your help."
- **Reward:** unknown. Nothing evidenced (R).
- **Next:** Meeting the Princess (wiki "next").
- **Evidence:** `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json:136-140` (Quest:Helping Honeydew, rev 49932); locale ids above in the test `en_us_data.dat` (lines 50206, 55595, 21604, 71016, 87513) and locale tool 413084; guide `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sunstone Valley.md:284`.

### 3. Distracting Druids

- **Title:** locale 413092 "Distracting Druids". Title key ucdt 2725293763 (test `en_us_data.dat` line 71761).
- **Giver: ambiguous.** The record and the guide list Honeydew. The offer text, however, is spoken by Sadie: ucdt 2315799302 "Shhh! Can't you see I'm in the middle of a very important investigation!… Oh, you're friends with Honeydew? Huh, I guess you can help me with my investigation then." Honeydew's line is only the hand-off: ucdt 453574182 "Honeydew wants you to meet her friend Sadie. You should be able to find Sadie near Green Watch, west of Skysong Ledge."
- **Prerequisite:** Honeydew's introduction to Sadie. Whether Meeting the Princess must come first is unknown.
- **Intro:** ucdt 1269319625: "Apparently a suspicious horse named Sadie thinks the druids in Pony Vale are up to no good. Find Sadie to help her investigate these mysterious druids."
- **Objectives:**
  - ucdt 841720068: "Sadie near Green Watch wants you to distract Yarith Yewroot with an engaging conversation. Keep him occupied so she can investigate the druid's supplies."

- **Steps:**
  1. Go to Honeydew, then to Sadie near Green Watch (453574182; Sadie: "suspicious horse", 1269319625).
  2. Accept from Sadie. Go to Green Watch and talk to Yarith Yewroot (ids and NPC id unknown) to keep him occupied.
  3. Hand-off back to Sadie: unknown.
- **Targets:** Yarith Yewroot (NPC id not resolved). Green Watch (location 3094120407 per the guide's Sadie row; 775603887 for Green Watch as the druid camp).
- **Completion text, reward, count:** unknown (R).
