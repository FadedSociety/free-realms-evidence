**Scope.** Rizzle's chain in the record is A Beautiful Friendship, Ka-Boom or Not To Boom, Junk or Bust, Robot Woes, The Legendary Sunstone, plus the hand-in of Wheelie Pete's For Rizzle. None of these has a file under `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/` (title searches on the quests folder returned no matches). `wilds.json` lists Junk or Bust only as a `page_lists` row with `quest_id: null`. **Locale ids.** The numbers below are `locale` tool ids. The `ucdt`/`ugdt` hashes in `locale/en_us_data.dat` are separate keys and are noted only where I saw them.
