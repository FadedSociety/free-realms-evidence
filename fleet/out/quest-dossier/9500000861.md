# Wheelie Pete (npc_id 9500000861, Sunstone Valley): quest dossier

**Scope.** Pete gives five quests in the Sasparilla chain. None is built yet: a grep of `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/` for Wheelie, Sasparilla, Harley, Oasis, Rizzle finds only unrelated Kart Driver and Postman hits. I wrote up the first four in chain order, since the cap is four. The fifth, For Rizzle, is summarised in Gaps.

**Id convention.** The `locale` tool's number (e.g. 29609) is the id I cite as the locale id. Where I found it, the `.dat` hash key (`ucdt`/`ugdt`, e.g. 35102436) is given as a second id. The two are not the same number, and I could not confirm the builder's convention.

## 1. Like Scavengers in a Barrel

- **Title:** locale 29609 "Like Scavengers in a Barrel". Dat keys: ucdt 35102436 (`locale/en_us_data.dat:903`), ugdt 1464128141 (`:38777`).
- **Intro (offer):** locale 29611: "Can you believe it! A pack of Sandscale manguana made off with all my sasparilla. Why, if I still had a good knee I'd march right down there and...<BR><BR>Hey, I bet a young'n like you could <font color='#D9781A'>bring my Barrels of Sasparilla back</font>, eh?"
- **Journal summary:** locale 29610: "Wheelie Pete has had his share of trouble since the Skullz started throwing their weight around, but recently things have gone from bad to worse. Now he also has to deal with the Sandscale Manguanas who recently stole several barrels of his famous Sunstone Sasparilla! Will you brave the wilds of Sunstone Valley to recover his stolen barrels from the Sandscale?"
- **Objective:** dat 541529997: "Find Wheelie Pete's stolen Sasparilla Barrels in Sunstone Valley." (`locale/en_us_data.dat:14381`)
- **Return / completion:** locale 29612: "Hey, look at that, you found my sasparilla!<BR><BR>Good on you, [*player*]. Now how about doing me another favor?" This is the return line that hands off to Hog Wild Harley. No separate completion string was found.

**Steps (in order):**
1. Speak with Wheelie Pete in front of Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse, Sunstone Valley (wiki).
2. Find Barrels of Sasparilla, "all around the Sandscale Territory". The wiki goal is "Find Barrels of Sasparilla - /5", so 5 barrels (wiki). The item name is locale 16451 "Barrel of Sasparilla". Its item id is unresolved.
3. Return to Wheelie Pete (locale 29612).

- **Prerequisites:** none stated in the wiki. The wiki gives no level or job requirement.
- **Rewards:** coin amount 10 (wiki quest-pages "coin amount: 10"). Item rewards (R), unknown.
- **Evidence:** locale ids above; `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 199-203 ("Quest:Like Scavengers in a Barrel", revision 23330); `refs:freerealms-wiki/wikitext/16386-50424-83492f089ef4.wiki:136`.

## 2. Hog Wild Harley (repeatable)

**Steps (in order):**
1. Speak with Wheelie Pete in front of the Roadhouse (wiki).
2. Find Harley in "Sandscale Territory South-East of Sandspear" (wiki).
3. Escort Harley to safety: protect her from the Manguana until she is out of Sandscale Territory (wiki). The client has dev strings "sunstone valley - harleyEscort 04/06" (ugdt 44295829, 32640573) and "remove harleyEscort 03" (190083487), which support an escort step.
4. Return to Wheelie Pete (locale 29616).

- **Prerequisites:** complete Like Scavengers in a Barrel (wiki: "Player must complete Quest:Like Scavengers in a Barrel"). Repeatable per the wiki "Notes" line.
- **Rewards:** wiki "first reward: Mystery Reward" (`Sunstonemysteryicon.jpg`). Coin amount not given by the wiki. Coin and items (R).
- **Harley's NPC:** name keys ucdt 183959239 (`locale/en_us_data.dat:4874`); other name keys in the guide. Her NPC id is unresolved.
- **Evidence:** `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 206-210 ("Quest:Hog Wild Harley", revision 23452); locale ids above; `notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Sunstone Valley.md:37`.

## 3. The Water Must Flow!

- **Title:** locale 29617 "The Water Must Flow!". Dat key ucdt 752245978 (`locale/en_us_data.dat:19815`).
- **Intro (offer):** locale 29619: "I'd like to brew more of my famous Sunstone Sasparilla, but I'm out of oasis water! It's a critical ingredient and those Sandscale manguana have been hoarding it.<BR><BR>Think you can <font color='#D9781A'>get me some Oasis Water from Sandscale Oasis</font>?"
- **Journal summary:** locale 29618: "The recent manguana uprising has really messed with Pete's business, and theft isn't even the worst part! Pete hasn't been able to get a single drop of oasis water, which is a critical ingredient in his famous Sunstone Sasparilla, since the manguana locked down the oasis. Venture into Sandscale Oasis and collect some oasis water for Pete."
- **Objective:** locale 439818 "Collect Oasis Water from Sandscale Oasis". The wiki's sub-objective is "Enter the Sandscale Oasis Battle found North-West of Wheelie Pete's Roadhouse" (wiki).
- **Return:** locale 29620: "Thanks for bringing me this Oasis Water, [*player*]! I'll make sure you get the first barrel of Sunstone Sasparilla I brew with it.<BR><BR>But first I need one more ingredient."

**Steps (in order):**
1. Speak with Wheelie Pete (wiki).
2. Enter the Sandscale Oasis battle, north-west of the Roadhouse (wiki).
3. Collect Oasis Water: "Collect Oasis Water from Sandscale Oasis - /4", so 4 (wiki). The oasis has Jumping Cacti (wiki). Item id unresolved. Do not confuse it with "Pirate Oasis Water" (locale 147851).
4. Return to Wheelie Pete (locale 29620).

- **Prerequisites:** complete Hog Wild Harley (wiki).
- **Rewards:** coin amount 20 (wiki). "First barrel of Sunstone Sasparilla" is promised in the return text but this is not a formal item reward (R).
- **Dungeon cross-reference:** the Sandscale Oasis dungeon note lists the Pete offer as `4239374670` with companions (`notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Sandscale Oasis.md:84`).
- **Evidence:** `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 290-294 ("Quest:The Water Must Flow!", revision 23697); locale ids above.

## 4. The Secret Ingredient Is...

- **Title:** locale 29621 "The Secret Ingredient Is...". The dat key was not located by grep.
- **Intro (offer):** locale 29623: "The last ingredient in my Sunstone Sasparilla is top secret! Only my old friend <font color='#D9781A'>Peace-guana Joe</font> knows what it is.<BR><BR>Joe was the Sandscale boss before the tribe went crazy. Now he won't trade with me unless I <font color='#D9781A'>send him some help</font>. Are you up for it?"
- **Journal summary:** locale 29622: "The last ingredient for Pete's Sunstone Sasparilla is a well guarded secret, so secret that he can't even tell you what it is! But Pete does need your help retrieving it. Apparently he purchases the ingredient from Peace-guana Joe, the former leader of the Sandscale Manguana tribe, but before Joe will give up the secret ingredient he may need you to do something..."
- **Joe's offer (wiki quote):** "Hey man, you're Pete's friend right? That's righteous dude. So here's the deal. I'm trying to win back my tribe with these Peace Flyers. If you pass them out I'll hook you up with Pete's secret ingredient. Sound good?" (locale 440108).
- **Objective:** locale 439826 "Pass out the Peace Flyer". Reminder line when still incomplete: locale 440057 "Looks like you still have some Peace Flyers to pass out, yeah?..."
- **Joe's return:** locale 440137: "Right on, [*player*]! The war for peace and love may not be over, but you've won a major victory today.<BR><BR>And here is Pete's secret ingredient, just like I promised. Let him know I've sent along some positive vibes with it." (dat 212294426). Joe's nudge to Pete: locale 440059 "...now you just need to take Wheelie Pete his secret ingredient."
- **Completion (Pete):** locale 29624: "Ha, the secret ingredient! With this I can finally finish brewing my famous Sunstone Sasparilla!<BR><BR>And as promised you get the first barrel."

**Steps (in order):**
1. Speak with Wheelie Pete (wiki). He names Joe as the source.
2. Meet Peace-guana Joe in Sandscale Territory (wiki). He gives the Peace Flyers task.
3. Pass out the Peace Flyer: "Pass out the Peace Flyer - /5", so 5 Peaceful Sandscales (wiki). Flyer item id unresolved. Peaceful Sandscale NPC ids unresolved. Their lines are on the wiki page.
4. Return to Peace-guana Joe in Sandscale Territory and receive the secret ingredient (locale 440137). The ingredient's item name and id are not in the sources.
5. Return to Wheelie Pete (locale 29624).

- **Prerequisites:** complete The Water Must Flow! (wiki).
- **Rewards:** coin amount 30 (wiki). "The first barrel" of Sasparilla is promised in locale 29624 (R, item unknown).
- **Joe's NPC:** legacy resource `FreeRealms-Legacy/src/Resources/Npcs.json:15462-15464` has Id 3905, NameId 439160. That is a legacy resource id, not the zone-file id 9500000861 used by this record. Mapping is unverified.
- **Evidence:** `refs:freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json` line 297-301 ("Quest:The Secret Ingredient Is...", revision 23741); locale ids above; Joe's guide entry is in `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Sandscale Oasis.md:84` (names ids 1479341964, 3715668275).
