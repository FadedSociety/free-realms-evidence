# Quests of Dane Blueshift (Wilds)

## Summary

Dane Blueshift (npc_id 9500000891, name_string_id 100868 "Dane Blueshift") is a **Kart Driver trainer**. The evidence does not show him giving a quest of his own. His quest-like content is a referral step inside Francis Panic's Power Problems errand. Nothing for him is built in `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json`, and no quest title for his branch is in the locale. The only quest-shaped candidate is the Laurin-to-Dane branch below, which is partly evidenced and unnamed.

Two ids in the record (3535310333, 2917648096) are **hash keys** from the raw locale dump (`ucdt`), not locale-tool ids. The locale tool rejects them. Their text resolves through the small ids noted below. I did not verify the hash-to-small-id mapping beyond matching the text.
