# Greenthumb (npc 9500000904, name_string_id 18721, Wilds, Pixie Nursery)

Summary: the record's three quest entries do not all hold up as Greenthumb-given. Only the tin-node quest has Greenthumb speaking in his own voice. The seed-sack and choychoy quests have the wrong giver or only use Greenthumb as a step. None of the three is in `quests/*.json`. `quests/miner.json` has id 3100008 with title_string_id 77043 "Miner: Simple Surveying", but that is Shimmer Shinyaxe's live Dartmoor quest, not Greenthumb's. Do not attach Greenthumb to it.
