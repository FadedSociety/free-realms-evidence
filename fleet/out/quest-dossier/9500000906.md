Then... maybe, he'll be allowed back into the clan!" The combined chain objective is `116744` (ucdt `2597468242`): "Talk to Hogosh at the Forest Troll Fort and then return to Gulsh under the bridge near Sanctuary."
4. **Return to Gulsh at the cave under the Sanctuary bridge.** Objective `15219` (ucdt `1778243198`): "Return to Gulsh at the cave under the Sanctuary bridge".
