# Quests of Hogosh (Wilds)

## Summary

Hogosh (npc 9500000909, name locale 15202) is **not evidenced as the giver** of any quest. Both quest chains that name him are given by other NPCs (Gulsh; Nog). Hogosh is the step NPC in the Gulsh chain and the delivery target in Noglash Delivery. Neither quest is in `quests/*.json` (no hits for 93775906, 2597468242, 582503029, 837473822, 1061620688, 224929741, or "Gulsh"/"Noglash" under `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests`). Both are candidates for a Hogosh-linked dossier, with the giver mismatch flagged.
