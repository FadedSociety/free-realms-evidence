## Gaps

- No intro, objective, completion or step text for any of the four "Free X" titles. The locale holds only the titles and one-line "from the Forest Troll Fort!" strings.
- The giver is unproven. The guide says the captives are not givers. The likeliest giver is Grosh or the Fort, but no source ties the titles to them.
- No quest id, level, job, prerequisite or reward.
- No wiki page in `refs` mentions "Free Tido/Sola/Mifa/Dore". The Forest Troll Fort wiki page does not list the captives as quests.
- Whether the captives are in-instance (Fort combat) or a separate quest is unknown (`Forest Troll Fort.md:114`).
- Recommendation: do not build these as quests until a source shows the offer or completion text. A player recording of Mifa's dialogue, or a Lxzxrus/Sanctuary quest table entry for the rescue chain, would close most gaps.
