# Quests of Boranug (Wugachug)

# Boranug (npc 9500000967, Wugachug entrance): quest dossier

Boranug's record lists one quest, "Welcome to Wugachug!". The evidence actually supports a different first quest, **Join the Party**, which is the opening step of the Sasparilla Hunt chain. Nothing in the record or the guide's quest table is explicitly tied to Boranug except by wording. Two notes on the record:

- Its "Rival score in Kart Driver: Endorsement Deal (401856720)" line is not a quest he gives. Endorsement Deal is given by Shooblemoot and Twingnbor (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Wugachug.md`, row 45), and the locale line 401856720 does not resolve. Boranug is only the rival there.
- The "Talk to Boranug" lines (1770500935, 2285048403 in the record; locale 5101431 and 5101471 here) belong to the Chug-a-Mug Challenge block (locale 5101360 to 5101471). The guide gives that quest to Chugzy (`NPCs - Wugachug.md`, row 143), so they are not Boranug's own quest.

Quest-id note: the locale-tool numbers (e.g. 385979) are the ids the Lxzxrus server uses as TitleId/DescriptionId. The `en_us_data.dat` keys in the record (e.g. 1426312283, 1820519853) are different hash keys for the same strings. Both are cited below.

## 2. Welcome to Wugachug! — weak, candidate only

- **Title:** locale 385987 "Welcome to Wugachug!" (dat 1820519853 ucdt, `refs: FreeRealmsLocaleTools/.../en_us_data.dat:48057`).
- **Giver:** Boranug, only by the guide (`NPCs - Wugachug.md` row 36 "page_lists"; row 148 "Title match by wording"). Not in the Lxzxrus server data, which lists the 7-quest Sasparilla Hunt starting at Join the Party.
- **Intro candidate:** locale 385988 "Come on in, forget all your troubles!" (unconfirmed owner).
- **Other nearby strings (owner unknown):** 385989 "Listener"; 385990 "It's always a party here!"; 385991 "Enjoy our world famous Sweetwater Sasparilla!".
- **Steps, objectives, completion, rewards:** unknown. The guide's "Meet the Brewmaster" cites 1426312283, 4125888773 and 3177744145 (dat keys). These are the same strings as 385980, 385983 and locale 3177744145 ("Talk to Sasparilla Brewmaster Wugawump in Wugachug."), which are already Join the Party's text. So "Welcome to Wugachug!" may be the same quest under another title, or a separate one. The evidence does not settle which.

Do not build this as a separate quest without a server source. If it is built, it should be the same record as Join the Party until a separate step is found.
