# Chuggoom (npc 9500000970, Wugachug): quest dossier

**Read this first.** Chuggoom is one of four party guests (Sal, Delilah, Happalot, Chuggoom). The guide says "Four party guests. Never givers." (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Wugachug.md:48`). The record's `roles: quest_giver` and empty `quest_ids_given` contradict this. Chuggoom is a **target** in these quests, not the giver. No line of text in the locale names who gives them. Three unbuilt quests use him as a target, and I list them below. None is built: `quests/*.json` has no Sasparilla, party or Chuggoom entry. Titles and giver are the main gaps.

Id note: the `locale` tool takes its own row numbers (e.g. 46730). The record's `ucdt` hashes (e.g. 1567186020) are different. I give both where I have them. "dat line" means the line in `opening-*/locale/en_us_data.dat` of the refs tree.
