# Guard Thoomlug (npc 9500000983, Wugachug): quest dossier

**Scope.** One unbuilt quest is evidenced for this character: **A Sweetwater Climb**. No file under `prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json` cites its title id (13709596 / 442624 / 5101364) or the names "Thoomlug" or "Sweetwater Climb", so it is not built. The patrol job is not a titled quest and is listed under Gaps.

**Important correction to the record.** The wiki start NPC is **Chugzy**, not Thoomlug. The Chug-a-Mug offer line (442648) is attributed to Chugzy on the wiki. The guide row for this quest says "Chugzy, then Guard Thoomlug" (`notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Wugachug.md:142`). Thoomlug is the middle and return NPC. A builder should set the offer NPC to Chugzy (`npc_id` for Chugzy is in `npcs/wugachug.json` near line 1989) and use Thoomlug (9500000983) as the step and hand-in NPC.

## A Sweetwater Climb

- **Title.** Locale **442624** "A Sweetwater Climb" (the same string also appears at **5101364**). Client keys: ucdt 13709596, ugdt 367953053, and a second ucdt 2820884500 (`NPCs - Wugachug.md:142`).
- **Quest summary (detail).** Locale **442636**: "Prove your worth in order to participate in the Chug-a-Mug Challenge in Wugachug."
- **Goal line.** Locale **5101365**: "Do what needs to be done in order to participate in the Chug-a-Mug Challenge."
- **Intro (offer), speaker Chugzy.** Locale **442648**: "The Chug-a-Mug Challenge is the most prestigious event for Wugachug and their Sasparilla Festival!<BR><BR>Hey, you should join the contest! Nothing quenches your thirst like a mug of Sweetwater Sasparilla! And I know that we, err, you adventuring types work up quite a thirst during your adventures." Wiki: "Speak with Chugzy located in near the Wugachug Warpstone" (`refs: freerealms-wiki/quest-pages.json`, title "Quest:A Sweetwater Climb").
- **Objective lines and steps, in order:**
  1. **Speak with Chugzy** near the Wugachug Warpstone. This is the start (wiki, as above).
  2. **Talk to Guard Thoomlug** (goal, locale **5101493** "Talk to Guard Thoomlug"; **45040** "Speak to Guard Thoomlug in Wugachug." is a different Wugawump errand, so do not use it). Thoomlug's line, locale **5101596**: "Ruined! The festival is ruined! We always start the challenge with a fresh mug brewed right here in the Party Tent, but we can't continue because our Sweetwater isn't sweet!<br><br>Grah! I bet those bixies are at it again!" The player's reply is the wiki's "Oh no!" (locale **8149** "Oh no!" is the matching string, but it is shared with other quests, so it is probable, not confirmed).
  3. Thoomlug's request, locale **5101598**: "All of my guards are keeping the peace around town with the festival in full swing, but you look like you can hold your own in a fight!<br><br>Make your way to the top of Sweetwater Climb and put a stop to their tampering!"
  4. **Complete the Sweetwater Climb dungeon**, which is behind the Wugachug Brewery (wiki "Complete Sweetwater Climb"). Client text: locale **30448** "Complete the Sweetwater Climb encounter." (also 31099 for the group version). Dungeon data: `notes/Build Guides/Content/Dungeons/Sweetwater Climb.md` and `data/content/enemies_unresolved.json` (`dungeon_file: sweetwater_climb.json`). The boss is Rebel Mage Pollentine (client-named, guide `NPCs - Wugachug.md:92`). The wiki does not say Pollentine is the quest target, so do not treat her as one.
  5. **Return to Guard Thoomlug** (goal, locale **5101366** "Return to Guard Thoomlug"; **73064** "Return to Guard Thoomlug" and **70190** "Return to Guard Thoomlug in Wugachug" belong to other quests).
  6. Hand-in text, locale **442660**: "From what I hear, the Brewmasters of Wugachug are again able to create their award-winning Sweetwater Sasparilla.<BR><BR>With your help, they should be able to start the Chug-a-Mug Challenge in no time!" The wiki attributes this to Thoomlug.
- **Prerequisite.** Wiki: "Player must complete Quest:A Brewing Darkness" (`refs: freerealms-wiki/wikitext/10614-46083-b4ea1d23af23.wiki:10`). The guide gives A Brewing Darkness client ids 1669446019 / 2958099636 / 2969483822, but I did not resolve a locale string for it. Level requirement: unknown.
- **Next quest.** Wiki Questprog "next: Chug-a-Mug Challenge" (locale 442625 title "Chug-a-Mug Challenge"; 442637 "Enter the Party Tent in Wugachug and participate in the Chug-a-Mug Challenge!"; the offer for that quest is Chugzy's 442649 "...While you were away, I signed you up!"). That is a separate quest and is not built here.

Locale **442948** "Brawler's Power Shard of Durability II" (description 443002). Coins 88 are wiki-only. The shard is shown only as the first reward and is a Brawler-class item; the item id is not resolved (R).
