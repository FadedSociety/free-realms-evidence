# Wootmoot (npc 9500001005, Wugachug, name locale 41251): quest dossier

**Scope.** No quest in `repo:prototype/osfr-tools/data/content/quests/*.json` names Wootmoot, Sandi or Pear Jam, so nothing is skipped as built. Three Wootmoot chains are evidenced. Two have a locale title. The third has none.

**ID note.** The record and guides cite two kinds of id. Bare 9-digit keys (e.g. 359256987, 497711267) are the first column of the `ucdt` rows in `refs:opening-*/locale/en_us_data.dat`. The `locale` tool uses its own small ids (e.g. 391805, 71244). I matched the two by identical text. Both are given below.

## 1. "Done Deal" (title: locale 391804). Wootmoot cancels Sandi's booking

- **Title.** "Done Deal" (locale 391804). The title sits in the same id block as the text below.
- **Giver.** Wootmoot (name 41251), "near the stage in Wugachug" (locale 796026419; `repo:…/npcs/wugachug.json:18755`).
- **Offer / intro text.** locale 391805: "Wootmoot is not happy with Sandi's talent agency and wants you to let her know her services are no longer needed." (dat key 359256987; `refs:…/opening-*/locale/en_us_data.dat:9571`).
- **Step 1. Go to Seaside and talk to Sandi.**
  - Objective: locale 391808 "Head to Seaside and talk to Sandi." and 391809 "Talk to Sandi".
  - Hand-off line: locale 391816 "Please go to Seaside and let Sandi know that I no longer require her services." (dat key 983365281).
  - Sandi is "near the docks" in Seaside (locale 1538797401, per `repo:notes/Build Guides/Content/NPCs/NPCs - Seaside.md:87`).
- **Step 2. Return to Wootmoot.** locale 391803 is Wootmoot's closing line: "That will be the last time I ever book talent from Sandi." It is followed by "find her at Seaside … Tell her that all deals are canceled! You can have her payment instead..." (dat key 2261976758).
- **Bounce-back (Seaside side, then Wootmoot again).**
  - Seaside-side text, speaker not named: locale 391838 "Your musical talent shouldn't be wasted here in Seaside … on stage in Wugachug!"
  - locale 391840 "Head back to Wugachug and talk to Wootmoot. Perhaps your talent for music can convince him to hire Sandi again." (dat key 1517784129). Variant 391847 "Head back to Wugachug and talk to Wootmoot. Perhaps you can convince him to hire Sandi again!" (dat key 3148922920).
  - Objectives: 391848 "Talk to Wootmoot" and 391856 "Talk to Wootmoot again".
- **Next.** Leads into "Ready to Rock" (section 2).
- **Prerequisites.** Level, job and earlier quests: unknown. Nothing in the sources states them.
- **Rewards.** Not evidenced. The Sandi text mentions "her payment" (locale 391803), which is narrative only. (R) if a builder needs a value.
- **Other ids in the block, not placed.** 391802 "Tanglewood Sergeant" and 391806 "Ugh! Another one didn't show up? I can't afford to lose customers due to lazy talent." (likely Wootmoot's line, but its place in the quest is unconfirmed).

## 2. "Ready to Rock" (title: locale 71699). Feeling the Beat! for Wootmoot

- **Title.** "Ready to Rock" (locale 71699).
- **Giver.** Wootmoot, same NPC as section 1.
- **Offer / intro text.** locale 71705: "I'm never booking an act through Sandi again! This is the second band that's flaked out. Everyone here is ready to rock, but we have no show! Say, would you go to the music stand and give it a try? Even a novice might have the rhythm to feel…"
- **Step 1. Play the minigame.**
  - Objective: locale 71700 "Play the Feeling the Beat! minigame on Novice difficulty to entertain Wootmoot."
  - Tracker lines: 71704 "Wootmoot is near the stage in Wugachug, waiting to be entertained." and 71707 "Head to the music stand and see if you're "Feeling the Beat!"".
  - Minigame: "Feeling the Beat!" (title ucdt 3439382866). Stage stand in Wugachug. Difficulty variants 2275888162 and 2206444937 (`repo:…/npcs/wugachug.json:18767`). Locale 38766 "Feeling the Beat!" and 384435 "Win the Feeling the Beat! minigame on Novice difficulty" are related strings (win condition not placed in this chain).
- **Step 1 completion.** locale 71701 ""Feeling the beat" minigame completed".
- **Step 2. Return to Wootmoot.** Objective locale 71703 "Return to Wootmoot". Tracker 71702 "Wootmoot can't wait to hear what you have in store for him!" (dat key 3746960612).
- **Completion text.** locale 71708 "Wow! You're not bad. Much better than anyone Sandi would have sent." Wootmoot's praise line in 71708 is taken from the locale tool, which ties it to this chain.
- **Prerequisite.** Logically after "Done Deal" (the text says "again" and "never … through Sandi again"). Not a hard prerequisite evidenced by any id.
- **Level, job and rewards.** Unknown. Not evidenced. (R) if needed.
- **Wiki and the Snowhill/Seaside maker.** Snowhill has a "Feeling the Beat!" Maestro challenge (locale 675351909, 392139). It is a different quest, out of scope.
