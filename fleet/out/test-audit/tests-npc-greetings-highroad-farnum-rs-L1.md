  Why it cannot catch a regression: every `guide_row` marker embeds `target.name` (for example `"| Chef: Fireworks Frenzy! | Ranger Ricardo |"`, `"| 27 | ... | Maple Sugarleaf"`, `"| **Lost Sheep** ... | Bo |"`). `guide_row` returns the first line that starts with the marker, so the `.contains(target.name)` check at line 426 is true by construction.   Fix: check the speaker cell specifically. Split the returned row on `|`, take the speaker column by index, and assert that its trimmed text equals (or starts with) `target.name`. Keep the marker to the non-speaker cells only.

- **[medium] tests/npc_greetings_highroad_farnum.rs:57, 71/83/95/107/119/131/143 (`guide_line`) and 383-385 - the cited physical note line is never checked against the note file.**
  Why it cannot catch a regression: the test looks up the row by content (`guide_row`, lines 281-300) and never compares its position to `guide_line`.
