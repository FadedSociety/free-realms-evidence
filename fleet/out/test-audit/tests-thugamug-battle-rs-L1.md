- **[medium] tests/thugamug_battle.rs:738-742 `a_second_player_can_run_its_own_instance` - the "first player's hit lands on its own boss" check is a disjunction that also passes when the encounter has no active enemy or the first living enemy is a different GUID.**
- **Why it cannot catch a regression:** `active_enemy(&first).is_none_or(|(guid, _, health)| guid != target || health < target_health)` is satisfied if a change drops the melee hit but the arena then reports a different first-living enemy (or no active run). For example, a change where the hit is silently ignored and the target slot is reordered, or a change that clears `active` after a tick, still passes this line. The only other check, `second`'s health staying at `target_health`, does not prove that the first player's own boss took damage.
- **Fix:** Replace it with a direct check on the first session's copy of the boss: `let (_, _, health) = active_enemy(&first).expect("the arena still runs"); assert!(health < target_health, "the first player's hit damaged its own boss");`, or look up `ENEMY_GUIDS[0]` in `first.snapshot()...encounter.active.enemies` and assert its health dropped below `target_health`.
